The provisional notice (Fiche à valider) lists Robert de Cailliaco as dean in 1353–1358, Simon Frère of the diocese of Lisieux in 1358–1360, and Guillaume de Bragelogne in 1360. These are leads requiring verification in the underlying records.
It cites Aube G 4325 f 34v, 9 G 1, 9 G 822 and 2 O 1883 (O/zero as displayed requires checking), and Roserot, Dictionnaire historique de la Champagne méridionale, t I pp 796–800 (1948 as the notice gives it). The official Aube finding aid gives a differing Roserot volume/year citation; establish the correct edition before using the page reference.
No original appointment instrument was read through this database. Its prose history is not treated as independent evidence of Shroud acquisition or transfer. The reported tenure ranges are not exact appointment/death dates.
