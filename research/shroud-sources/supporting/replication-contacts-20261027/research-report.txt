SHROUD REPLICATION EXPERIMENTS AND CONTACTS
Reviewed 27 October 2026

Purpose: find useful people, archived experiments and reproducible comparisons without new access to the original cloth. No messages sent, no specimens ordered, no paid services commissioned.

MAIN DISTINCTIONS
1. A photographic display copy reproduces appearance using a known printing process.
2. An experimental image-forming process is evidence about physical feasibility; its agreement must be measured property by property.
3. Laser tracing of the existing photograph copies geometry and negative tones by construction. It is not a spontaneous body-to-cloth reconstruction.
4. Demonstrating a modern radiation process does not by itself establish a medieval energy source or identify the actual origin.
5. Existing-original photographs/spectra and archived experimental swatches can support new comparisons without touching the Shroud. Access, calibration and comparability still matter.

RESEARCH PRIORITY
First approach Di Lazzaro/Murra and Donnet for retained linen, raw micrographs, spectra and explicit mismatch lists. Fanti is already contacted; a later follow-up can address corona experiments. Garlaschelli and independent experimenters offer possible period-technique comparisons. Display replica providers are useful for teaching and referrals, not proof of matching chemistry.
Do not claim the 2012 and 2019 papers were both fully reviewed solely because their abstracts were read. The Donnet mirrored full paper has now been obtained for targeted reading; identify the particular sections reviewed. The 66-page Di Lazzaro presentation is a separate author source.

TARGETED FULL PAPER REVIEW OF DONNET 2019
Introduction and discussion/conclusion on printed pages 2163–2165 reviewed after download. Authors report local sponge-like structures interpreted as possible melt/resolidification or polymerization; they do not establish exact coloration depth. Their FTIR/Raman findings do not support significant photo-oxidation under these conditions. The conclusion explicitly leaves full reproduction unresolved. The source image controls the laser scan, so visual similarity is not an independent body-shape reconstruction. Ask for original swatches and any follow-up depth measurements rather than presenting this as a complete Shroud match.
