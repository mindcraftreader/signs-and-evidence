All six scanned pages visually read,27October2026. The running headers confirm1993; the Southampton later bibliography assigning this exact title to1990Dresden is inconsistent with the actual paper and publisher record.

p104: four Withyham Passion scenes, late14th-centuryFlorentine, possibly Gerini circle; study in progress.
p105: each approximately1m², each two fabric pieces neatly backstitched; medium-weight closely woven linen,17×15yarns/cm².
p106: later animal-skin-glue attachment to mahogany, assumed English. Ground unpigmented white calcium sulphate, mixture of anhydrite and gypsum identified by XRD, normal skin-glue binder indicated by solubility. No evidence of layered application; later glue stained ground yellow; original size cannot be distinguished. Authors describe ground as thin and contrast it with Northern sized/no-ground cloths. Starch/sugar not tested. They report grounds in Spinello, Barnaba da Siena, Monaco Intercession and Lippo di Dalmasio based on technical examinations/personal communications, not numerical data reproduced here.
p107: very thin red bole for gilding; egg-tempera paint medium identified.
p108: generally one paint layer with glazes, occasionally two; Cennini's advice for thicker canvas paint is not borne out by these cross-sections. Flesh has three described strata: thin green earth, verdaccio, vermilion/lead-white finish. Pigments studied by optical microscopy and sometimes SEM/EDX.
p109: only Flagellation and Mocking closely examined by publication; other work ongoing. Fiber identification credited to Dinah Eastop. FTIR and differential scanning calorimetry credited for medium identification. No calibrated cross-section image or micrometre film/fiber-depth distribution printed in this six-page paper.
