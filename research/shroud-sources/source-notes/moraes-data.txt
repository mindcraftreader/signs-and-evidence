Cicero Moraes — Digital 3D approach, public supporting data
========================================================================
Source ID: MORAES-DATA
Category: 3D and reproduction
Year: 2025
Recorded/reviewed: 2026-10-26
Source: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29645060

What it contributes:
Dataset linked by the published paper, for inspecting/reproducing its digital setup. Saved archive contains Fabric.blend, Low_relief.blend and Body_3D.blend. These are the author’s simulation inputs, not an independently recovered Shroud body.

Limits and interpretation:
An available simulation is not validation of its assumptions.

Access: Public CC BY 4.0 dataset saved; archive contents inventoried, simulations not executed
