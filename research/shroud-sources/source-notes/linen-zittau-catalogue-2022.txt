What it contributes:
Primary curator catalogue: Brandenburg cloth ca.1290, 428 × 206.5 cm (printed p.30); Lüne linen cloth mid-13th century, 369 × 114 cm (p.18); large Zittau cloth 1472, 820 × 680 cm (p.10); small Zittau cloth 1573, 415 × 340 cm (p.14). Includes named scholarly authors and restoration histories.

Limits and interpretation:
Exhibition displayed reproductions: use original object dimensions printed in each entry, not reproduction dimensions. Zittau 1472 was torn in 1945 and reassembled in 1994/95; not an untouched intact sheet. Halberstadt 340 × 190 cm entry is incomplete linen-thread net/filet work, not broad plain linen.
