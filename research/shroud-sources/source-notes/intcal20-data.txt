Reimer et al. — IntCal20 Northern Hemisphere radiocarbon calibration curve and data
========================================================================
Source ID: INTCAL20-DATA
Category: Dating and sampling
Year: 2020
Recorded/reviewed: 2026-10-27
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/RDC.2020.41

What it contributes:
Official calibration data used for the audit’s illustrative two-component isotope-mixing calculation.

Limits and interpretation:
The calculation interpolates curve means and does not propagate full calibration uncertainty or fit a Shroud repair geometry. Fractions are carbon fractions remaining after pretreatment, not percentages of DNA reads.
