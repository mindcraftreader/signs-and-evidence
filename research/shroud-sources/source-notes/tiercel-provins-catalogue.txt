Provins municipal manuscript catalogue — ms 85, item 148: Pierre de Tiercelieue and Jean Truelle

Catalogue p 25 describes a report to Parlement by Jean Truelle, lieutenant of Pierre de Tiercelieue, concerning annual dues disputed by Saint-Quiriace and Saint-Jacques of Provins. The correct target is manuscript 85, item 148.

Limitations: Catalogue description only; original act not obtained. This is Pierre, not Jean the Langres patron. No proved kinship or Lirey connection; different Provins institutions must not be conflated.
