Additional 75-record ancient-linen literature screen — 27 October 2026

All 75 previously unprocessed metadata candidates now have individual screening
decisions. This means 26 relevant full-text assessments, 2 partial primary-text
checks and 47 title/available-abstract decisions; it does not mean 75 complete
full texts were obtained. The CSV and JSON preserve the evidence level, source,
academic affiliation provenance, access gap and reason for every decision.

New eligible non-Shroud calendar-age predictions paired with a C14 or explicitly
labelled historical reference: 0 accepted. The pre-existing comparison data and
their descriptive statistics are unchanged. Co-use of C14 and FTIR for material
identification does not provide a second calendar date, and so gives no numerical
dating error or sample SD.

Added detailed records include Boano (University of Turin, Life Sciences and
Systems Biology) on C14-dated linen bandages; Oras (University of Tartu, Chemistry
and History/Archaeology) on two mummy textiles; Ferrari (Turin / La Venaria Reale
and Philadelphia Museum of Art) on a wool tunic; Quarta (Salento CEDAD, Engineering
for Innovation) on bone-collagen FTIR alongside C14 dates on several materials;
and Kalisz (Medical University of Lublin, Biopharmacy) on hemp macrofossils. The
last three are retained with their material differences explicit, outside the
flax/linen calendar-clock validation count.

Eleven requested / attempted full-text routes remain incomplete: S08, S09, S12,
S29, S42, S43, S44, S62, S64, S71 and S75. Their abstracts or partial text have
nevertheless been screened; the workbook never labels them full-text reviewed.
S09 (Fanti / Basso 2016 ISSAT proceedings) is the priority remaining methods
verification: its abstract matches the three specimen categories in the 2017
paper, but complete reuse / independence cannot be established from the abstract.

All retrieved valid PDFs, the four XML primary full texts, searchable extractions
and the Tartu chemical supplement are archived in the bibliography. Every frozen
candidate also has its retrieved metadata saved in this folder. The review is a
documented research snapshot; it is not certification that every database and
non-indexed publication in the field has been exhausted.
