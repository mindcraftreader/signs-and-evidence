1. RIVAL CATHOLIC SIGN MOVEMENTS: A SOURCE-BACKED LEAD, NOT YET PARITY
Uppsala University church historian Magnus Lundberg's A Pope of Their Own,
second edition (2020), printed19–20/PDF25–26, reports dancing-sun claims,
photographs, messages, stigmatists and fragrances at El Palmar de Troya.
Its early movement was Catholic; the independent Palmarian papacy arose
later. These descriptions show a relevant rival sign/message bundle but do
not independently authenticate the phenomena or reproduce Fatima's full
source quality. Consult footnotes20–23 for underlying sources. The book's
scholarly credentials establish provenance, not a demonstrated natural cause.

William A Christian's Zainak18 (1999), pp74–75, reports his presence during
an alleged Palmar solar miracle in1968; he also discusses predicted-climax
solar reports at Aldeamoret and failed predicted signs elsewhere. This is
research-based comparative evidence, including firsthand attendance, rather
than an optical replication. His historical interpretation and fieldwork
were not a controlled experiment. It does not establish equivalence to D115
or the complete Cova/distant-witness record.

NEW ORIGINAL NEWSPAPER LOCATED
El Diario Palentino,20August1969, article Las apariciones en el Palmar de
Troya, by Ana Aldaz of Agencia Cifra, Madrid dispatch18August. Original
issue OCR at the Spanish public historical-press archive, path8006897.
It quotes Jesuit Francisco Albarracin on a solar disc, rotation and colours,
and mentions photographs and an absence of observatory records. It describes
his March17 visit separately from his August15 Mass. Thus a later UFO
retelling placing all solar details at the August15 Mass must not be adopted
without visual verification of the interleaved newspaper columns.
The article is an interview with Albarracin; Aldaz is not thereby established
as a firsthand witness of the solar phenomenon. It is months-later reporting
of the March visit, so it does not equal Almeida's event-date reportage.
Printed page and complete column order remain to be visually verified.

STRONGEST REPLY
Catholic origin supports rather than automatically contradicts a broad
apostolic-Christian exclusivity claim. Later schism does not prove earlier
experiences false. No equivalent full evidential package has been established.
Consequently do not award a counterpoint or assert ordinary causation from
these sources. It merits an independent source audit if comparative work
continues, particularly around exact timing, named direct observers and
photographic provenance. Do not invoke the group's smallness as disproof.

2. THE CHURCH'S OWN APPARITION EPISTEMOLOGY
Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger's2000 CDF Fatima commentary distinguishes prudent
human assent from doctrinal faith. It treats authentic interior visions as
involving the recipient's limitations and symbolic representation. Thus a
sincere/genuine experience does not, within this theological account, require
literal accuracy of every reported detail. This is a third possibility
besides deliberate lying and verbatim divine transcription. It does not
refute Fatima's event, Catholic message, or God-authenticated authority if
that final scope is independently established.
The2024 DDF norms likewise distinguish pastoral approval from certainty of
divine origin, while recognizing spiritual value. They do not overturn
older approvals or supply an ordinary physical explanation.
