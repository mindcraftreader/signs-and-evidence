ZEITOUN — ADVERSARIAL SOURCE CRAWL
11 November 2026

RESULT
No material new defeater of the retained Zeitoun core was established. Eight candidate families were tested. The strongest surviving criticism concerns what the photographs and institutional endorsements independently add to the testimony. That criticism is bounded: it does not erase the early named, prolonged moving-figure reports or identify their ordinary cause. Nothing in this pass warrants reopening Z-01 or Z-02, revising the 16–0 ledger, or presenting generic crowd psychology as a completed explanation.

The affirmative claim tested was that a luminous recognizable womanlike figure was repeatedly reported above St Mary’s Coptic church, sometimes for extended periods and making movements interpreted as blessing people or bowing toward the cross; early named observers, including Muslims, and contemporary Arabic press support that record. Exact totals of 90, millions of unique viewers, Nasser’s presence, and a measured blackout radius are unnecessary to that core. The report distinguishes actual observations, proposed cause, and religious authentication.

DEEP CHECK 1 — DOES THE PHOTOGRAPHIC RECORD INDEPENDENTLY ESTABLISH THE FIGURE?
Candidate ZAC-01; strongest bounded surviving criticism.

Tested inference: several photographs, independently made and authenticated, establish an externally resolved Marian human body in addition to the witness reports. A substantially altered or source-dependent image would weaken that corroboration even without explaining the sightings.

A. Contemporary newspaper image. Al-Ahram, 5 May 1968, p.1, was re-rendered from the saved complete issue and checked. It really carries a luminous form and an assertion by the newspaper’s photography department that it printed the original film and found no montage. This is meaningful contemporary evidence; it is not an independently inspectable negative, a named examiner’s technical report, or custody history. The existing passage ZA-008 preserves the caption’s relative date, the day before yesterday, versus later 13 April attributions. This audit does not resolve that discrepancy. It does not infer fabrication from a poor reproduction.

B. Watani’s refusal. The short Arabic item headed Around a Picture on Watani, 5 May 1968, p.1, was checked again in its saved scan. It says an offered apparition photograph was rejected because it did not represent reality. The photographer and image are unnamed. Therefore al-Azm’s juxtaposition of this item with Al-Ahram’s photograph cannot by itself establish that both editors evaluated the same image. It documents contemporary editorial doubt about a circulating image, as well as Watani’s willingness to discriminate while affirming the appearances. This was already recovered on 28 October (ZT-062), not new counterevidence.

C. The detailed woman illustration versus photograph. Ouellet’s 25 April 2013 original blog contains an image with a legible caption attributing the drawn figure to Dr Labib Shenouda of Alexandria, based on his memory. I downloaded and visually inspected that caption, the blurrier Ali Ibrahim reproduction attributed to Kamell et al. p.231, and the Fawzy Mansur reproduction attributed to p.224. The sharp Shenouda image is openly labeled a drawing; its details cannot be counted as an independent camera record of clothing, hands, or facial anatomy. It also cannot fairly be offered as a proven covert fraud. The blurrier Ibrahim reproduction and drawing share recognizable architectural composition, but no original negative was obtained. Authenticity of the underlying image is not established merely by the existence of an earlier publication.

D. Which Wagih photo did Jackson criticize? Dumsday §8.3, PDF p.220, summarizes Kamell, Jackson and Jackson’s analysis, saying the Wagih Rizk Matta photo was probably doctored and the Mansur/Ibrahim pictures showed no identified alteration. Its footnote points to the original 1996 book pp.219–233. Crucially, Ouellet’s 2011 discussion specifically locates Vern Miller’s criticism in the bird-form photograph, citing p.223; it describes shapes altered to resemble birds. I did NOT acquire those original technical pages. It would be an unsupported leap to identify this as a forensic rejection of Al-Ahram’s 5 May human-figure image. Multiple later writers repeating Jackson do not become independent photographic examinations.

Strongest defense: images need not carry the case; early named testimony already supplies recognizable form and motion. Some pictures may be genuine, and an admitted illustration is not evidence of a staged roof event. That defense survives. A static blur cannot either confirm or refute a sustained gesture series. The surviving criticism is that a claimed independent visual check must specify the image, photographer, first dated publication, examiner, original negative and alteration status. I found no verified chain that upgrades the surviving pictures to that standard, and no verified chain that establishes they are all fabricated.

Outcome: retain a bounded photographic-corroboration limit. No new core challenge. Next decisive evidence: original Kamell pp.219–233 with exact examined images and methodology, followed by original negatives/contact sheets and dated first-publication matches. Buying, borrowing or requesting material was outside this no-contact pass; no purchase was attempted.

DEEP CHECK 2 — DOES MUSLIM/STATE ATTESTATION SUPPLY AN ADVERSARIAL CONTROL?
Candidate ZAC-02; bounded criticism survives, fabrication theory does not.

Tested inference: a Muslim-majority state had no reason to endorse a Christian apparition, so its investigation provides especially strong independent hostile-witness authentication. The evidence supports a more complicated institutional setting, without cancelling named Muslim observations.

New primary acquisition: Ahmad Shalaby, Muqaranat al-Adyan, vol.2, al-Masihiyya, 10th edition 1998, title page PDF2; printed pp.108–110/PDF111–113. The title identifies him as Cambridge PhD and professor/head of Islamic history and civilization, Dar al-Ulum, Cairo University. Those are his publication’s credentials, not optical expertise. He says he and others attempted a response but political orders barred discussion. No order, date, official signatory or rejected manuscript is reproduced. The allegation is therefore his retrospective testimony, not independently proved censorship. He proposes state exploitation of post-1967 distress and calls the event a fabrication, without documenting a device or organizer. His light-reflection claim is attributed to Meinardus; the original passage was not acquired. His narrative also moves from a Shubra fatal crush to cessation: that cannot terminate Zeitoun’s separately documented later record.

The source chain matters. Ghazali’s own account, already acquired in the 1997 edition of Qadha’if al-Haqq, pp.59–64, similarly alleges suppressed criticism and reports an unsuccessful visit with Abu Zahra. These accounts provide actual named dissent, not a basis for claiming unanimous Muslim assent. Neither account is a date-matched contradiction of Athanasius’s or Shenouda’s prolonged-figure episode. An unsuccessful vigil is expected under an intermittent-appearance hypothesis too.

The strongest positive authority evidence must remain: Hamdy Haraz’s 12 May 1968 Watani interview reports his own observation and lighting check. Adel Taher’s statements show affirmative state action; the Ministry report dated 30 April is contemporary and explicitly favorable. However, William Farid Basili’s Ministry report and its newspaper republication share a source; its unequivocal p.4 conclusion attributes the matter to the church committee. State office, Muslim personal identity, and a technically independent investigation are different properties.

Strongest defense: actual Muslim workers and a named officeholder still report what they saw; the government could amplify a real phenomenon, and political benefits cannot manufacture a moving woman. Correct. Therefore neither political incentive nor censorship allegation establishes fabrication. The narrower surviving criticism is that state support should not be treated as a hostile experiment or proof that critical accounts could freely compete on equal terms. The case’s cross-faith witness evidence remains affirmative, with dependencies traced individually.

A source caution emerged in pursuing Shalaby’s strongest purported Christian rebuttal. Valerie Hoffman, Religion 27 (1997), p.57, explains Meinardus’s distinction as a light-appearance versus an apparition with verbal messages, retaining whole-person and bust reports. This differs from demonstrating ordinary reflected light. Dumsday §3.9/PDF115 also transcribes Meinardus’s affirmative recorded testimony. A McGill thesis indexed at printed p.77 explicitly compares Shalaby’s inaccurate dates and paraphrase with Meinardus p.264, but full retrieval returned403 and its metadata was not securely identified; it remains a lead, not an acquired scholarly refutation. Do not call Shalaby’s attribution verified, or treat disagreement in terminology as an optical experiment.

Outcome: actual dissent and political incentives qualify the independence/hostility inference. No demonstrated hoax or newly established core defeater. Next decisive evidence: a dated censorship order/rejected report, original Ministry/police engineering files with personnel and procedures, and Meinardus’s original pp.264–269.

DEEP CHECK 3 — IS TECTONIC/ATMOSPHERIC LIGHT A DOCUMENTED PHYSICAL FIT?
Candidates ZAC-03 and ZAC-04; no adequate core explanation established.

Derr and Persinger, Perceptual and Motor Skills 68(1), 1989, pp.123–128, DOI10.2466/pms.1989.68.1.123, is an actual research paper, not merely an internet conjecture. Its abstract reports a monthly correlation of0.56 and an exceptional seismic increase about400km southeast roughly a year after onset. That is evidence worth testing. ResearchGate indexed an author-upload record, but the returned local HTML was a blocked response; the publisher PDF also failed. I did not obtain or independently reanalyse the full paper or its dataset.

Read Dumsday’s detailed discussion and quotations at §§4.2–4.3/PDF132–136. The proposed chain is strain → light and electromagnetic effects → some observers’ richer perceptions. It is not the claim that a photographed woman herself is a mineral flash. Dumsday reports weaker daily associations and no daily magnetic association, and raises localization and prolonged-figure objections. The original paper remains the controlling source for a numerical reanalysis; an adversarial book’s summary cannot substitute for its data.

The relevant discriminators are specific: a measured local field/optical source; timing on actual apparition nights and controls; a mechanism holding above this roof repeatedly; and a plausible production of prolonged changing human form or reliably cued perception across observers. No such site measurement or reconstruction was recovered. A broad monthly association is compatible with several causal stories, especially with an incomplete and selective apparition calendar. This is not proof that earthquake lights cannot exist or that their mechanism must already be fully settled; it is a failure to connect this particular proposal to the strongest evidence.

The nearer contemporary atmospheric proposal is Muhammad Jamal al-Din al-Fandi’s St Elmo’s fire/electrical-discharge account reproduced by Ghazali, pp.60–63. He is introduced there as an atmospheric-phenomena professor at Cairo University; I did not separately verify his appointment or acquire the original article. No local field strength, weather record, spectral observation, or matched human-figure reconstruction accompanies the excerpt. Coronal light at high features might touch a subset of lights. It does not yet explain the retained woman and gestures. Invoking a laboratory God-helmet effect does not fill those site-specific gaps.

Strongest defense: these mechanisms leave the joint location, recurrent recognizable form, duration and gestures unexplained. That response survives. The real counterweight is that some light episodes need not be identical to full-figure episodes; the miracle case should not treat every glow as independently impossible. That distinction was already retained and is not a new victory.

REMAINING CANDIDATES
ZAC-05 — Simultaneous divergent perception. Watani5May1968 p.2/ ZT-061 reports unnamed twins together on9April, one seeing full form and one a faint ray. This is contemporary, not an invention from Nelson. But it is indirect, unnamed and without surveyed sightlines or durations; it was already examined. A selective experience is not ruled out by the user’s core, and the passage does not contradict the named prolonged observations on other nights. Do not recycle Z-01.

ZAC-06 — Later recurrence collapse. The6November audit already conservatively preserves several early separate nights and named1969 reports, plus Al-Fida3August1970. A1971 devotional article is not a fresh1971 sighting, but exact terminal dates and a90-night calendar are not needed. No new event-level contradiction was found. This candidate is closed as a renewed core objection.

ZAC-07 — Missing motion-film as contrary evidence. AP’s two contemporary records really are films about Zeitoun; shot lists show church/crowds, a photograph and an actual bird, not authenticated moving Mary footage. The9May television notice specifies witnesses/photos, not filmed bodily motion. No crew was placed filming the30April02:45–05:00 figure. Therefore failure to recover an original moving-figure sequence limits instrument corroboration; it is not evidence that a recording known to have captured the figure contradicted witnesses. Existing search limitations remain.

ZAC-08 — Exact identity and religious authentication. The luminous woman was not accompanied by a public verbal doctrinal message. Identifying Mary relies on form, church dedication, cross-directed actions and religious context. That is inferential but positively grounded; mere absence of speech does not sever it. A stricter claim that the event alone resolves every Catholic/Coptic authority issue would outrun what its record specifically says, but this user’s retained claim does not require that. No counterexample or contradiction was supplied by rephrasing the inference.

WHAT CHANGED
Newly obtained original Shalaby pages make the Arabic skeptical argument reviewable and reveal that its strongest physical claim is a secondary attribution, not a described experiment. New visual checks pin the sharp woman illustration to an explicit memory-drawing caption and separate it from Ibrahim’s blurrier reproduction. They also prevent an unsupported conflation of Jackson’s reported bird-photo criticism with Al-Ahram’s figure photo. These gains narrow what the photographs and official endorsements can independently establish. They do not materially undermine the retained early figure testimony.

The strongest surviving criticism is thus evidential architecture: the independent camera/control layer is thinner and less securely traceable than phrases such as many authenticated independent photos or state scientific verification imply. If those phrases are absent from the core, this pass has not found a new successful substantive objection to that core. No ordinary source, fraud apparatus, matched reconstruction, or direct same-episode contradiction was established. No tally edits were made.
