LIMITS OF SIMPLE SUNSET ACCOUNT
Steadily increasing atmospheric path alone does not explain her report that the sun became dazzling again at the end ofOctoberexperience despite lowering. Changing cloud/atmospheric transmission is a possible additional factor, not measured in this record. A literal structured monk apparition is not explained merely by a red sunset. Pareidolia/religious interpretation are candidate explanations requiring evaluation, not established diagnoses. Medical credentials do not rule out ordinary visual adaptation; sincerity and professional competence are compatible with perceptual effects.
Fatima's own cloud/rain setting also means its comfortably viewable disc cannot by itself exclude atmospheric filtering. Higher sun makes a specific sunset/smog analogue weaker; it does not establish supernatural agency by geometry alone. Its prediction, independent contemporary eyewitness journalism and distant reports require separate joint evaluation. Do not reopen closed challenges or score another point from generic optical mechanisms alone.
