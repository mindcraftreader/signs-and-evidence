HOW A FAILED MECHANISM CAN UPDATE THE MODEL
Suppose, after the old evidence, a particular mechanism carries fraction w
of O's weight. Suppose a genuinely additional test is r times as likely
under that mechanism as under the retained other states. If its likelihood
is equal for those other states, O is scaled by k=1-w*(1-r). This equality
is an explicit simplifying assumption, not guaranteed by a withdrawal.
The grade 'high' supplies neither w nor r. Re-evaluating evidence already
included should revise the original likelihood, not append a second test.
