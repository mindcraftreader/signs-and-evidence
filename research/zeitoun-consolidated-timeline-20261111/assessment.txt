ZEITOUN CHRONOLOGY AND Z-02 CONFIDENCE REVIEW
11 November 2026

Decision
The Pepper's Ghost challenge remains withdrawn. No new tally point: main score
remains 16–0. Retain MODERATE for the existing Z-02 geometry-based defense,
rather than upgrading it merely because the series recurred. The original
rating explicitly depends on unverified witness stations and roof dimensions.
Repeated operation increases what a proposed installation must explain, but
does not independently measure those dimensions or rule out a reusable setup.

There is strong documentary support for the narrower positive finding that
recognizable-figure reports recur on separate nights. The November 6 audit
already withdrew the attempt to reduce that history to a few ambiguous lights
or a single story repeatedly retold. This supports Z-01 and adds an operational
constraint to Z-02. It does not make a mathematical probability out of ninety
repetitions. No historical installation or adequate complete mechanism has been
identified in this research. That is a finding about the investigation's result,
not a demonstration that every possible installation is physically impossible.

What the consolidated timeline contains
93 inherited observation records, grouped in 64 historical event groups:
57 groups have dates; seven originally had unresolved dates. A dated group is
not necessarily one distinct observing night, nor necessarily a figure report.
Nine supplementary rows add later-source information and context, making
73 display rows. Some rows are period summaries, unsuccessful visits, corrected
false alarms or context-only newspaper publications. Do not call this a list
of 73 apparitions. The 65 older calendar candidates (53 distinct dates) are
preserved separately in the JSON, with their original verification flags.

Chronological findings
2 April 1968: first evening report, with 20:30/21:00 source variation. The early
hours of 3 April may be part of the same vigil, not another independent night.
28 and 30 April: substantial named-figure accounts published in Watani, 5 May.
30 April variants differ about uninterrupted visibility; preserve the break
and later return described in the 1969 booklet.
19, 22 and 23 May: separate nights in Watani, 26 May; Gregorian dates are
normalized from relative weekdays. Repeated phases do not become extra nights.
27 and 28 May: further figure reports in Watani, 2 June. Eight phases on one
night are not eight nights. Original printed page 3 was checked in the earlier
recurrence audit.
31 May: Gobreial couple's figure report, collected in interviews in 1980–81.
Keep its retrospective timing visible beside the earlier newspaper evidence.
Later 1968–May 1969: named dated witness extracts in the Gregorios appendix,
published in 2007. Those pages preserve attributed reports; the original signed
statements and their recording dates were not recovered.
4 April 1969: half-hour figure report traced through Egyptian Gazette, Zaki
and Dumsday. The recovered original headline/date is legible; the entire body
is not independently readable. Distinct source chain, not three confirmations.
21 August 1969–11 June 1971: Bayless summarizes an anonymous nun's diary via
Zaki: 105 visits, 17 individual Marian apparitions, bird forms on 24 nights,
other lights on 85 nights. Categories overlap; the seventeen sightings are not
individually dated in this summary.
6 January, 14 February, 6 March 1970: later chronology via Johnston, quoted
by Dumsday. Primary dated statements remain unrecovered.
Late July/early August 1970: contemporary Al-Fida evidence of renewed figures.
The November 6 audit verified the printed issue date, 3 August 1970, issue 78.
Thursday/Friday wording remains inconsistent; no fabricated exact-night list.
29 May 1971: claimed final apparition in later sources cited by Dumsday,
notes 140–142. No recovered contemporary dated eyewitness record here.
11 June 1971: final nun's diary entry describes a water-container phenomenon.
It is not evidence of a full-figure apparition on that date.
15 August 1971: Watani's original feature documents shrine crowds and worship,
recalling April 1968; it does not establish a new apparition that August.

Consequence for “ninety over three years, then never again”
Recurrence is well supported. Approximately ninety is a summary estimate,
not an inspected ninety-night primary-source calendar. Reports extend into
1971 in later accounts; the clearest original contemporary figure reporting
recovered in this dossier extends through 1970. Neither a precise final night
nor the absolute assertion “never again” has been established by this register.
No date-by-date blackout, equipment-inspection or illumination-measurement
series has been recovered. Those variables cannot be assigned to every row.

What would settle the remaining timeline questions
1. Recover the full nun's diary in Zaki, preserving each visit and category.
2. Obtain the original dated report behind the claimed 29 May 1971 finale.
3. Resolve Al-Fida's Thursday/Friday wording with a complete issue.
4. Continue the Watani serial archive, especially 1969–71, linking each report
   to its original publication and distinguishing phases from observing nights.
These are finite archival targets, not conditions for retaining the successful
defense against the recurrence objection already withdrawn.

Scope of this pass
Consolidated existing registers, incorporated the November 6 original-source
audit, and reread Bayless's diary passage and Dumsday's late chronology and
citations. This is not a fresh inspection of every Arabic scan. Live church
archive requests returned 403, and the live Bayless PDF URL returned 404;
previously saved copies remain linked. Provenance hashes are in provenance.json.
No new photometric experiment or camera/roof survey was performed.
