RESULT

No complete ordinary explanation or demonstrated photographic refutation was established. Two narrower challenges can be defended:
(1) The distant reports establish regional testimony, but the retrieved records do not independently reconstruct a low luminous object’s position near Fatima. Their directional, temporal and testimonial precision differ substantially.
(2) Long duration by itself does not exclude ordinary perceived motion. A primary experimental study reports increasing apparent movement during30-minute stationary-light sessions. Its dark-room conditions do not reproduce Fatima.

The new multiview camera-calibration attempt was inconclusive. It is not a reason to reopen the existing photographic point as defeated. This report earns no automatic tally point and does not call the full Fatima argument refuted.

1. WHAT IS RETAINED

Pre-event documentation of an October13 public sign is real, including the evidentiary purpose recorded in the children’s interviews. The event attracted a large public crowd. Immediate favorable reports and skeptical/anticlerical journalism are part of the record. Direct1917 descriptions include the pale disc, rotation, colors and apparent approach/descent; these are not all inventions of later devotional books. Geographic dispersion is also genuinely reported.

The reviewed camera/lens challenge did not establish a physical setup that fits the complete D115 photograph. It remains closed against that developed rebuttal. A later experiment’s limitations do not silently reverse that finding.

The user does not require identical experiences from everyone present. A non-seeing witness therefore does not by itself contradict the user’s actual premise. An unsigned negative letter or differences among witnesses must not be presented as a complete event explanation.

2. DISTANT REPORTS: WHICH CLAIM CAN BE ASSAILED?

Target claim: the regional reports independently confirm a physically descending or low light source localized toward the Cova, in a way that rules out regional sky conditions plus observer-specific perception.

The defensible criticism concerns that additional physical localization, not the existence of all remote testimony. Locations on a map are not viewing bearings. A reported direction to a mountain is not automatically a direction to a single emitter; a red flash is not the same measured observation as a descending disc. The record does not provide a synchronized multi-station reconstruction.

3. PROLONGED APPARENT MOVEMENT: AN ACTUAL EXPERIMENT

Robert M.Stern, then affiliated with Indiana University, published Long-Term Observation of the Autokinetic Illusion: Frequency and Direction of Movement, Perceptual and Motor Skills18(3),1964,825–830, DOI10.2466/pms.1964.18.3.825.

The publisher’s abstract reports40 observers watching a stationary pinpoint in a dark room for30minutes. Apparent-motion reports increased with time and favored vertical movement over horizontal movement. This counters the proposition that an ordinary motion illusion must disappear after a brief moment. It does not establish continuous illusion in every participant, any daylight solar trajectory, synchronization among thousands, the date prediction, or the D115 light-source direction. Motivation: identify focal geometry from overlapping exposures rather than camera catalogues, presumed face anatomy, flexible threads or shadows. This is potentially a genuinely independent test, provided enough corresponding features belong to a rigid scene and projection/crop metadata are understood.

Method reference: https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/da/de9/tutorial_py_epipolar_geometry.html

Used OpenCV5.0 SIFT, up to5000 features per exposure, bidirectional0.7 ratio matching; robust fundamental-matrix and homography estimates where at least8 matches existed. Screened78 distinct exposure pairs. Most pairs had0–6 matches. D109/D113 had8 but visual inspection showed mismatches on unrelated people/scene parts, so it was rejected as a calibration basis.

D110/D111 had41 mutual matches,38 fundamental-matrix inliers and28 homography inliers.26 potential static background correspondences survived a restricted tree-band selection. They span approximately1075pixels horizontally but only45pixels vertically. This weak vertical spread makes the homography sensitive to small coordinate changes. Feature/geometry inlier counts do not independently certify that every match is a fixed3D point.

A conditional pure-rotation fit with a9×12 top-crop center convention produced a21.6mm-equivalent minimum. The inferred rotation was not actually orthogonal: even the optimum retained about5.6percent relative singular-value spread. Pure rotation and the historical optical centers were not authenticated. This number is therefore NOT a recovered camera focal length or a refutation of Dalleur.

Descriptive stress tests resampled the26 points and added an assumed0.5pixel coordinate perturbation.100 runs gave conditional minima from12.4 to55.9mm equivalent. This is not a confidence interval, not a probability distribution for Ruah’s lens, and not an established historical measurement-error model. It demonstrates instability of this conditional fitting attempt.

Positive control: two copies of the SAME D115 exposure produced2380 mutual matches and2348 homography inliers. Their map is nearly uniform scaling by1.198 with a small top shift and a different lower crop. Thus the matching pipeline can recognize shared actual image detail; the different aspect ratio does not establish camera distortion. The different copies are not independent photographs. None is the requested6K TIFF.

Outcome: no reliable independent focal calibration, no complete ordinary-solar reconstruction, no reversal of the earlier camera concession. Higher resolution alone may not fix narrow-band geometry or unknown camera translation. A useful future source would contain additional rigid points at different heights/depths with reliable exposure/crop metadata.

• An online claim that photographer Ruah denied seeing the event was traced to an unsourced reader assertion that itself requests confirmation. No authenticated first-person denial was located. Excluded.

6. ADJUDICATION AND NEXT DISCUSSION

The narrow documentary criticism is sustainable: the retrieved distant corpus is not a set of independently timed and directionally calibrated observations locating a descending source near the Cova. The narrow experimental criticism is sustainable: long duration does not itself exclude ordinary apparent movement.

Neither establishes that the event was ordinary, that its prediction lacks evidential value, that the early eyewitnesses fabricated it, or that Dalleur’s actual shadow reconstruction has been disproved. The photographic argument remains a major unresolved obstacle for the ordinary account as framed in this discussion.
