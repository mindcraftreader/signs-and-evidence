ZEITOUN: EPISODE 601 CLAIM-AND-EVIDENCE REGISTER
Materially distinct Zeitoun claims and evidentiary arguments; excludes advertisements and unrelated doctrinal/historical claims. Grouped claims are not a count of independent evidence.
All five 30-minute readable segments reviewed, with the ending also checked in the timestamped file. Automatic English captions only; not full audiovisual review.
Approximate YouTube windows, including advertisements. Podcast timestamps differ. Values above 60 minutes denote elapsed minutes, e.g. 127:00 is 2:07:00.

Z01 | 03:47–04:15 | Mourad identifies himself as a parish priest of St George and St Anthony Coptic Orthodox Church, Ottawa.
Evidence type: Speaker self-identification.
Check: He is a present-day narrator, not claiming to have personally witnessed the 1968 appearances.

Z02 | 38:00–45:52 | The appearances followed internal church difficulties and the national crisis after the 1967 war.
Evidence type: Historical context and interpretation.
Check: Distinguish independently checkable history from the interpretation that the appearances healed national morale. Political and military claims were not independently audited in this pass.

Z03 | 45:52–47:42 | During a June 1967 wartime blackout, the Zeitoun church reportedly filled with unexplained light despite its lights being switched off.
Evidence type: Reported precursor; narrator says it was documented.
Check: Find the earliest account, named doorkeeper, date and recording date. This is separate from the later investigative power cutoff.

Z04 | 53:45–55:49 | A benefactor was reportedly promised in 1918 that Mary would visit a church he was to build within fifty years; the church opened in the 1920s.
Evidence type: Reported advance promise.
Check: Locate a document demonstrably recorded before April 1968, its exact words and the recipient. Later accounts of a promise are not yet a verified advance prediction.

Z05 | 54:45–55:16 | The promise was reportedly known to parish leadership and written down before the appearances.
Evidence type: Claim of a pre-existing record.
Check: Identify the manuscript, register or publication and date its inscription rather than merely the event it describes.

Z06 | 53:45–60:35 | The first appearance reportedly occurred on 2 April 1968 around 8:30 p.m. and lasted roughly ten to fifteen minutes.
Evidence type: Narrative chronology.
Check: Compare first-night statements; exact time and duration require source comparison.

Z07 | 54:00–57:15 | Muslim public-transport workers first treated the visible woman as a person in danger of jumping from the roof.
Evidence type: Relayed testimony, including Farouk Mohammed Atwa.
Check: Retrieve the workers’ individual contemporary statements. Their initial interpretation matters because it precedes identification as Mary.

Z08 | 56:15–57:15 | The church roof/dome reportedly had no ordinary internal access and its windows did not open.
Evidence type: Architectural constraint on an ordinary person being there.
Check: Check contemporary photographs, building plans and exact access routes; difficulty of access is not by itself impossibility.

Z09 | 57:00–59:15 | Observers reportedly noticed that the figure was above the dome, cast no shadow and moved away from and back toward it.
Evidence type: Reported visual features.
Check: Establish named observers, observation positions, lighting and the earliest wording of these particular features.

Z10 | 57:15–58:45 | The doorkeeper and Father Constantine identified the figure as Mary, and police came after a suspected suicide was reported.
Evidence type: Relayed sequence of witnesses and authorities.
Check: Compare Constantine’s actual report and police attendance records; do not merge different nights into the first night.

Z11 | 59:00–60:15 | A police chief reportedly ordered nearby street lamps broken, but the figure remained visible and brighter.
Evidence type: Reported first-night intervention.
Check: Find a dated account naming the officer and describing exactly which lights were disabled.

Z12 | 60:35–61:59 | Twenty-eight appearances were reportedly recorded during the first month, with about a thousand people gathering nightly within the first week or ten days.
Evidence type: Counts asserted without an episode event list.
Check: Recover the event register and counting rules. A near-contemporary booklet separately summarizes 27 appearances up to its governmental report date; differing cutoffs may explain differing totals.

Z13 | 60:45–61:50 | Reported appearances included a bust, full figure, olive branch, blessing gestures, bowing toward the cross and movement toward the crowd.
Evidence type: Descriptions of forms and actions.
Check: Compare dated descriptions by separately identifiable witnesses; do not presume every feature was present on every night.

Z14 | 61:15–61:58; 91:20–92:05 | Some observers reportedly perceived detailed features, a gentle smile and personal eye contact.
Evidence type: Witness perception relayed by Mourad.
Check: Record viewing distance and source date; perceived personal attention is distinguishable from an independently measurable direction of gaze.

Z15 | 64:30–65:30 | Both Muslim and Christian observers were interested in Mary, although they understood her religious identity differently.
Evidence type: Religious context and cross-confessional witness argument.
Check: Cross-confessional testimony broadens the witness base; shared Marian reverence must also be included when assessing independence of interpretation.

Z16 | 65:10–66:05 | Waguih/Wagih Rizk Matta reportedly took the first photograph on 13 April; a national newspaper authenticated it.
Evidence type: Named photographer; claimed photographic authentication.
Check: Find the original negative, first publication and actual authentication procedure. A newspaper printing a photograph is not automatically a forensic authentication.

Z17 | 66:00–67:35 | Large crowds and traffic led officials to create a tourist-police station with forty officers.
Evidence type: Claim of public administrative response.
Check: Retrieve contemporary municipal/police reporting; crowd-management action establishes public scale, not alone the cause of what was seen.

Z18 | 67:35–68:34 | Authorities reportedly cut electricity across Zeitoun for hours, yet the luminous figure remained.
Evidence type: Reported intervention against an electrical hoax.
Check: Recover dates, utility/police records, cutoff boundary, observations during the outage and duration. Stronger than an unsupported assertion that projection was impossible.

Z19 | 68:00–68:35 | Appearances reportedly occurred at varying times, sometimes before full darkness and sometimes late at night.
Evidence type: Temporal variation.
Check: Build a dated event chronology rather than assuming predictable nightly appearances.

Z20 | 68:15–68:42; 97:44–99:10 | Mourad attributes visible crowd faces in nighttime photographs and bright surrounding streets to light emitted by the apparition during a blackout.
Evidence type: Photographic inference plus reported eyewitness experience.
Check: Identify each photograph’s photographer, date, exposure, flash use and relation to an outage. A bright crowd photograph alone cannot settle the light source.

Z21 | 68:45–70:30 | Police and the Ministry of Tourism reportedly publicly accepted the appearances as supernatural.
Evidence type: Claimed official conclusion.
Check: Compare the original governmental report, contemporary reports about it, church statements and modern translations; avoid treating these as independent laboratory tests.

Z22 | 69:00–70:00 | Muslims, Christians of several churches and atheists reportedly watched together and experienced reconciliation.
Evidence type: Relayed cross-confessional and social testimony.
Check: Find named observers and contemporary accounts. Mourad explicitly does not identify a Jewish eyewitness when asked.

Z23 | 70:00–70:30 | The New York Times and an Enquirer described as being in London reportedly covered the events.
Evidence type: Press corroboration lead.
Check: Retrieve exact issues/bylines; identify the ambiguous Enquirer reference instead of silently substituting another publication. Distinguish reporting witness claims from editorial certification of a miracle.

Z24 | 71:00–71:59 | An estimated 250,000 people reportedly gathered after publicity; officials removed trees and the transport garage to make room.
Evidence type: Crowd estimate and physical administrative actions.
Check: Find contemporaneous estimates and dates for separate changes. Do not substitute later anniversary attendance for an early-1968 crowd.

Z25 | 72:00–72:50 | Mourad gives a conservative estimate of more than one million unique people seeing the appearances from 1968 to 1971.
Evidence type: Cumulative attendance/eyewitness assertion.
Check: No deduplication or estimation method is supplied in the interview. Distinguish visits, attendees, people claiming a sighting and individually documented testimonies.

Z26 | 72:45–74:10 | Three senior priests interviewed initial witnesses, examined healing claims and then reported seeing the figure themselves.
Evidence type: Claim of near-contemporary investigation and direct testimony by investigators.
Check: A reproduced 1969 committee booklet names Guirguis Matta, Hanna Abdel-Messih and Benjamin Kamel and dates their appointment to 23 April 1968. Inspect the signed report and Arabic original.

Z27 | 74:00–74:45 | Bishops Gregorius, Samuel, Athanasius and Abraham/Ibrahim reportedly investigated separately.
Evidence type: Claim of additional church investigation.
Check: Resolve names and separate commissions from shared records; several readers of the same report are not several independent observations.

Z28 | 74:30–75:40 | The medical committee reportedly required documentation of prior illness and recognized physicians rather than accepting every informal cure claim.
Evidence type: Claim about screening standards.
Check: Recover written criteria, case files, rejected cases, examinations and follow-up. A published summary is evidence of a medical claim, not the entire clinical record.

Z29 | 75:00–75:43 | A Muslim woman reportedly recovered speech and sight at the church.
Evidence type: Reported healing.
Check: The reproduced committee booklet identifies Madiha Mohammed Said and attributes onset to severe psychic shocks. Preserve that detail rather than automatically treating this as repaired structural eye damage.

Z30 | 75:45–76:45 | Atwa’s gangrenous finger reportedly healed before a scheduled amputation the next day; a non-Coptic doctor reportedly supplied a statement.
Evidence type: Reported medically attested healing linked to the first observer.
Check: Find the named doctor, before/after documentation and earliest publication. The physician is unnamed in this episode; do not call the original medical file inspected.

Z31 | 76:45–77:48 | Mourad also mentions disappearance of a tumor, restored vision after retinal detachment and dramatic improvement in an elderly cardiac patient.
Evidence type: Additional healing claims used against a purely visual explanation.
Check: Identify each patient, diagnosis, medical record and duration of recovery; these examples are not individually sourced in the interview.

Z32 | 77:45–78:20 | The patriarch publicly affirmed the appearances on 4 May 1968 after investigations.
Evidence type: Dated ecclesiastical statement.
Check: A modern reproduction of the dated statement is located. Compare the original Arabic and near-contemporary English translation, including duration details.

Z33 | 78:15–79:00 | Mourad links Zeitoun to a traditional route of the Holy Family but explicitly expresses uncertainty about the precise historical stop.
Evidence type: Religious interpretation with the speaker’s own qualification.
Check: Do not elevate the qualified geographical tradition into an independently established itinerary.

Z34 | 79:00–80:15 | He reports conversions and interprets the repeated choice of a Coptic church as religiously significant.
Evidence type: Reported effects and theological argument.
Check: No conversion register or aggregate count is supplied. Significance of the site is an argument to assess, not itself a headcount.

Z35 | 79:15–79:45; 88:00–88:30 | There were reportedly 94 documented appearances over three years.
Evidence type: Event count.
Check: Retrieve the actual list and distinguish figure appearances, other luminous phenomena and multiple episodes during one night.

Z36 | 80:00–80:55 | President Nasser reportedly personally witnessed the event and confirmed it.
Evidence type: Claim about a named official eyewitness.
Check: Locate a contemporary attributable statement or record of the visit; governmental endorsement alone does not establish that the president personally saw it.

Z37 | 81:20–82:47 | Mourad’s father, then about fourteen, reportedly saw a wheelchair user stand and walk after collective prayer.
Evidence type: Family testimony: son relays father’s claimed direct observation.
Check: Obtain the father’s own account, date, location, patient identity and diagnosis. Seeing someone rise from a wheelchair does not on its own identify the underlying illness.

Z38 | 82:45–83:20 | Mary reportedly delivered no spoken message during the appearances.
Evidence type: Consistent negative feature in the narrative.
Check: Compare the event register. Interpretations of silent gestures should not be quoted as messages she audibly spoke.

Z39 | 83:15–84:30 | Luminous dove-like forms reportedly moved at night without visible wingbeats, sometimes in cross formations and near the crowd.
Evidence type: Reported associated visual phenomena.
Check: Separate date-specific descriptions, original images and ordinary birds. The fact of nighttime flight alone would not establish a supernatural animal.

Z40 | 84:00–85:30 | Colored clouds and a powerful incense-like fragrance reportedly accompanied appearances; Athanasius reportedly judged the incense beyond ordinary censers.
Evidence type: Reported visual/olfactory phenomena plus inference.
Check: Retrieve Athanasius’s exact account; distinguish smoke seen, odor perceived, interior inspection and the inferred absence of an ordinary source.

Z41 | 86:00–87:50; 89:20–92:15 | Mourad knows surviving witnesses, including parishioners in Ottawa and an elder priest from Alexandria; one unnamed parishioner described camping out and watching.
Evidence type: Present-day witness access, with accounts relayed by Mourad.
Check: These are interview leads. Record each witness separately, with their own words, age, dates and earlier statements, respecting requested anonymity.

Z42 | 89:00–89:30 | The church website reportedly holds Arabic testimony, healing videos and scans shown by healed people.
Evidence type: Claim of accessible supporting records.
Check: Locate individual records and distinguish patient interview, displayed image, full diagnostic report and independent specialist review.

Z43 | 92:15–93:15 | Benedictine Jerome Palmer reportedly investigated and wrote a book; Catholic Patriarch Cardinal Stephanos reportedly saw the event and wrote to Rome.
Evidence type: Named near-contemporary research and correspondence leads.
Check: Locate Palmer’s 1969 Our Lady Returns to Egypt and the cardinal’s actual letter. Endorsement based on others’ accounts must be distinguished from personal sighting.

Z44 | 93:10–93:45 | The Catholic Church reportedly deferred to the local Coptic Orthodox authority rather than issuing its own declaration.
Evidence type: Institutional recognition claim.
Check: Obtain the actual Catholic diplomatic/ecclesial correspondence; deference and separate formal approval are distinct.

Z45 | 97:44–99:10 | The apparition was reportedly self-luminous, illuminated surroundings and caused white photographic overexposure while remaining comfortable to view.
Evidence type: Reported perception plus proposed explanation of photographs.
Check: Check exposures and original negatives. Overexposure is not itself physical film burning, measured solar brightness, or authentication of an apparition.

Z46 | 98:00–98:20 | The blackout/search area is described using an ambiguous five-kilometre expression.
Evidence type: Imprecisely specified spatial extent in the captions.
Check: Do not turn this into a verified five-kilometre radius or five square kilometres; recover the actual boundary.

Z47 | 99:30–100:20 | Authorities reportedly searched nearby rooftops, balconies and apartments for projectors and found none.
Evidence type: Claimed negative search result.
Check: Find the search report, officers, dates, coverage and equipment considered. A documented systematic search would constrain a hoax more than general technological speculation.

Z48 | 100:05–101:50 | Mourad argues 1960s technology could not generate the reported mobile three-dimensional figure; he also argues a Muslim government would lack a motive for this Christian hoax.
Evidence type: Technological and motive arguments, not experimental findings.
Check: Specify the display properties to be explained and period equipment. Lack of identified equipment/motive counts, but does not logically prove that only government equipment or no conceivable incentive existed.

Z49 | 102:00–103:20 | Present-day Muslim visitors reportedly venerate Mary as Mother of Light without accepting Christian teaching about her son.
Evidence type: Speaker’s account of current religious reception.
Check: This matters to interpretation: Muslim recognition is not automatically acceptance of every Christian doctrinal inference.

Z50 | 103:15–104:00 | The garage land was reportedly sold cheaply to the church and a large cathedral built opposite the original church.
Evidence type: Historical/property claim.
Check: Locate dated sale/building records; separate relocation, sale and later construction.

Z51 | 104:00–106:45 | On a recent visit Mourad met Father Bishoy, who showed original international press clippings; he observed visitors of several Christian traditions and Muslim women praying.
Evidence type: Mourad’s own recent observation and archive lead.
Check: Retrieve those specific clippings and catalogue them. His recent observations directly support present pilgrimage activity, not personal observation of 1968 events.

Z52 | 127:00–127:40 | Mourad acknowledges that some circulated photographs were touched up and differ from their original publications.
Evidence type: Explicit qualification by the affirmative narrator.
Check: Maintain separate original-publication, retouched, artistic and unidentified image categories. Retouching some pictures does not establish fabrication of every picture or event.

Z53 | 124:00–126:30; 136:15–138:10 | The hosts argue that well-established modern miracles should change how one assesses older Christian miracle accounts.
Evidence type: Cumulative philosophical/historical argument.
Check: One established event could change the prior; accumulation should track independent support and avoid treating the same story repeated as new independent evidence.
