NASSER AND ZEITOUN: QUOTATION AUDIT
Research date: 27 October 2026

Result: No verified first-person quotation by Gamal Abdel Nasser confirming that he saw the Zeitoun apparition was recovered in this search. This is a search result, not proof that no statement exists.

1. Mahmoud Fawzi, Pope Cyril and Gamal Abdel Nasser
The church's book-excerpts page attributes to journalist Mahmoud Fawzi an account of Nasser and Hussein El-Shafei watching from fruit merchant Ahmed Zeidan's balcony. Its English text says: "Nasser stayed awake until the Virgin Mary appeared at five in the morning!"
https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/book/excerpts-on-the-apparition-of-the-virgin-from-other-books/
Read the expanded visible Fawzi section in browser; saved visible text in sources/fawzi-excerpt-visible-en.txt.
The official parish history independently identifies Fawzi's book, page 78, as its source for this account:
https://stmaryztn.org/saintmary/ar/قصة-الظهور
This is Fawzi's narration, not Nasser's quoted words. Original book pages, publication date, and source for Fawzi's account remain unverified.

2. Benjamin Kamel's recollection, Watani, 30 April 2008
Victor Salama interviews/reports the recollections of Hegumen Benjamin Kamel, then secretary to Pope Kyrillos VI and an original investigating priest.
https://www.wataninet.com/2008/04/القمص‏-‏بنيامين‏-‏كامل‏‏البابا‏/
Full article text recovered through the web search index after direct access failed. Kamel recalls a telephone inquiry from Nasser's secretary seeking clarification for the president. Kamel consulted the pope and relayed the pope's interpretation to the presidential secretary, who expressed gratitude. The quoted explanation is the POPE'S, and the appreciative reply is the SECRETARY'S. Neither is a quotation from Nasser. The account was published forty years after the events.

3. Attribution rule
The 30 April 1968 Ministry of Tourism report is signed by William Farid Basili, Director of Inquiries and Complaints. Do not attribute its words to Nasser. A government official's report, a presidential office inquiry, a later account of Nasser's visit, and a first-person presidential declaration are distinct evidence.

Defensible wording: "Journalist Mahmoud Fawzi reports that Nasser personally watched the apparition from a nearby balcony." Attribute explicitly; do not put this in quotation marks as Nasser's speech.
