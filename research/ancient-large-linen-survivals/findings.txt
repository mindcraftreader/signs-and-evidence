ANCIENT LINEN SHEETS AROUND SHROUD LENGTH OR LARGER
Reviewed 27 October 2026

Scope: ancient linen sheets approximately 14 feet (4.27 m) long or longer.
Distinguish length alone from both length and width. The Turin Shroud is
approximately 4.4 x 1.1 m. This is not a census of exact weave, condition,
first-century date, exposure history or continuous above-ground preservation.

DOCUMENTED GROUP
Catharine H. Roehrig, Life Along the Nile: Three Egyptians of Ancient Thebes,
Metropolitan Museum of Art Bulletin 60(1), Summer 2002, printed p.27,
PDF page 28, reports 76 long fringed linen sheets in three wooden chests
from Hatnofer/Hatnefer's tomb, ranging from 14 to 54 feet long. They had
been used, some repaired, and were laundered and folded before burial.
The museum's 2004 author essay repeats the exact count and length range.
This establishes survival to modern excavation of dozens of long sheets.
The summary does not establish the width and present condition of each sheet.

COUNT INTERPRETATION
76 recovered long sheets in one tomb + at least one distinct long sheet
from Kha's tomb = a documented minimum of 77 approximately 14-foot-or-longer
ancient linen sheets. This minimum uses reported length, not individually
verified width and completeness for all 77. Four individual catalogue
records above verify cloths larger than the Shroud in both dimensions.

The 1965 Met Bulletin describes 55 sheets in two chests. Do not add its
count to 76: it describes overlapping material. The later 2002 and 2004
sources specify three chests and 76. The 2022 Met essay summarizes over
70 sheets and shirts but does not individually restate all lengths.

No reliable worldwide total applying the requested size criterion was
located in this search. Do not invent a 100, 1000 or 2000-object estimate
from a single tomb or from total textile-fragment collection counts.
There is no established upper bound here. The supported finding is that
the category includes at least dozens, not merely a handful. A stricter
global count would require object-level dimension and condition screening
across collections; buried/folded ancient linen is not automatically a
matched preservation control for a repeatedly displayed relic.
