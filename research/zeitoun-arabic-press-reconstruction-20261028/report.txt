TRANSLATION METHOD
English descriptions in the register are factual research summaries,not complete verbatim article translations. Only fields explicitly labeledshortoriginalexcerpt/directtranslation are direct quotations. Reproduced expressive excerpts are kept short; original scans remain cited for detailed checking. Arabic names are retained and Latin spellings follow accessible source conventions; apparent spelling variations are not silently merged into new witnesses.
Verification tiers distinguish core Arabic scan checking,clock/date-only checking,partial checks and archive-translation leads. A scan-level label does not claim every word on the newspaper page has been transcribed.

CHRONOLOGY AND COUNTING
Publication dates are separate from observation dates. Relative weekdays are converted only as labeled candidates. Night labels may cross midnight; no timezone conversion was imposed on historical clock readings. Repeated brief recurrences in one night are not automatically different calendar appearances. Unspecified spiritualphenomena,dovesonly,privatebedroomvisions,healingclaims and waitinginterviews remain separately labeled.
A named person quoted in multiple newspapers is not multiple independent witnesses. TheMay4pressconference,the Ministryreport,and the patriarchalstatement recur across newspapers. These source dependencies are recorded. No statement in this resource settles supernatural causation; the task is faithful reconstruction.
