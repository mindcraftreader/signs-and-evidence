IMAM REZA / MASHHAD: PERSIAN PRIMARY-RECORD AND PATIENT AUDIT
3 November 2026
Research subtask: primary cases and commission records. No tally awarded.

FINDING
The Charan2024 paper is not the entire evidentiary record. Persian material contains named patients, a claim-review office, reported medical examinations, and at least two quoted physician certificates with medical-registration numbers. These are more specific than undifferentiated miracle stories. I did NOT recover an original signed patient report, original diagnostic pathology, paired clinical images, quantitative before/after results, or independent clinical follow-up sufficient to establish a best-Lourdes-case equivalent in this pass. Several institutional originals were inaccessible; that access failure is not evidence that the records do not exist.

Strongest concrete new record lead: Kolthum / Kolsum Rezaei, a young woman from Behshahr, reported breast-lump disappearance at the shrine in May1993. Strongest newly found scholarly lead: the1999 Hakim paper now being pursued separately by the clinical-paper agent. The anonymous BMC Nursing neurological model case remains potentially stronger clinically, but its provenance must be resolved by that agent.

1. KOLTHUM REZAEI: NAMED PATIENT, OFFICE NUMBER, PHYSICIAN NUMBER
Source: https://realmuslim.blogfa.com/post/180
Source class: devotional narrative/republication, not an original medical report.
The narrative places the event at the shrine on1Khordad1372 (May1993). It reports earlier surgery/biopsy and a malignant breast lump, but supplies no pathology. It quotes a shrine examination attributed to Dr Nosrati, medical-registration19410, stating no lump was found. It identifies welfare-office reference1034/560 dated4/3/1372 and an examination dated2/3/1372. Nosrati is described as Dar al-Shifa's trusted physician; no specialty or university affiliation is established. Treating names Asadollahpour and Pirgheibi are also mentioned without complete credentials.
What this adds: patient identity and a recoverable archival/physician trail.
Still needed: original certificate, biopsy report establishing malignancy, exact surgery and other treatment, objective pre/post findings and durability. A narrative's attributed certificate is not the certificate itself.
The same patient is also the subject of Hamidreza Soheili's literary account, https://hr-shafa.blogfa.com/post/420 . Repetitions of the story are not independent confirmations.

3. ISFAHAN WOMAN,32, RECOVERY IN1395 (2016-17)
Source: https://telegram.me/s/saiberiemamzaman313?before=11816
Source class: secondary Telegram reproduction, attributed to commission member Abbas Farazi-Nia; exact original report/date not recovered.
She reportedly recovered while hospitalized at Mehrgan Hospital, Isfahan, after shrine servants visited with its flag. Her case was reportedly accepted at the eighth commission session and records signed/approved by the shrine custodian were said to be retained at Astan Quds Razavi's Documents Center. The item names neither the woman, disease nor examining doctors. Farazi-Nia is identified as a cleric, not her medical examiner. This is an archive-location lead, not accessible clinical proof. The described recovery occurred in a hospital; it is linked to the shrine devotionally, not a recovery in its pool/courtyard.

COMMISSION: WHAT IS ACTUALLY RECOVERED
Asre Iranian,31July2022,issue3612,p4:
https://www.pishkhan.com/Archive/1401/05/14010509/AsreIranian651157310111448535784254.pdf
This primary newspaper report of Farazi-Nia's remarks describes a commission formed in1396 (2017-18) including physicians, a judge and shrine servants, with approval by all members before ceremonial announcement. Web PDF text opened; screenshot and direct download failed, so full visual QA was not completed. It does not establish when every earlier healed-person registration office originated.
The2021 reproduced Fars report describes two specialist physicians, a trusted cleric, a judge and three servants; another reproduction describes two physicians/two judges/two clerics/two servants. These versions must not be silently harmonized into a verified fixed membership list. No physicians' full names, clinical independence, written exclusion criteria, denominator or rejection totals were recovered. Reported scrutiny can be acknowledged without claiming the criteria match Lourdes.

AUTHOR AND ARCHIVE PROVENANCE: A USEFUL POSITIVE FINDING
Hamidreza Soheili's own post, https://hr-soheili.blogfa.com/post/482 , reproduces a shrine publication announcement and explains his40-story collection was commissioned with AQR public relations. He describes using claim forms, reported medical records and patient contact, with literary elaboration. His self-described background is writer/director/storyteller/critic, not physician. This supports an archive behind some stories, but means narrated dialogue should not be treated as a verbatim clinical interview without its underlying record.

It does not yet supply the inspectable clinical evidence required by our comparison. Accordingly: Charan's unsupported millions figure remains unsupported; the entire Mashhad case must be assessed separately, pending the1999 medical-paper lead and original dossiers.
