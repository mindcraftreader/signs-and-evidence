CLINICAL CONTEXT, NOT AN INDIVIDUAL EXPLANATION
Michele Cavo and colleagues at Bologna's Seragnoli Institute of Hematology and Medical Oncology reported objective response rates of 47–53% across conventional regimens in a 2002 randomized trial of 527 patients. These are not permanent-cure rates or this patient's probabilities. A. Riccardi and colleagues at the University of Pavia/IRCCS Policlinico San Matteo likewise reported complete and partial responses after conventional chemotherapy. This establishes that treatment could produce remission in that era; it does not establish which treatment Bhardwaj received or that it caused his recovery. Only the abstracts were inspected.
