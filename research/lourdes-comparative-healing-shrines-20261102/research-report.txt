LOURDES AND NON-CHRISTIAN HEALING SHRINES: INITIAL COMPARATIVE AUDIT
2 November 2026

USER'S TEST
The user accepts that individual healings can occur in all religious contexts. The relevant comparator is a durable religious pilgrimage setting with repeated exceptional recoveries, a specific devotional/site connection, objective before-and-after evidence, medical investigation and follow-up. Do not substitute a single unrelated recovery or mere popularity for this pattern. Apply the same documentation standard to Lourdes and comparators. Complete original dossiers are not in hand for the Lourdes shortlist either.

RESULT
The Imam Reza shrine, Mashhad, Iran (Twelver Shia Islam), is the strongest structural candidate recovered in this pass. This establishes a non-Christian shrine-centered healing tradition with an institutional review process. It does NOT establish medical equivalence with the best Lourdes cases. No non-Christian case in this pass meets the user's recent two-named-examining-doctors plus inspectable before/after documentation filter. That is a finding about recovered evidence, not proof that stronger evidence cannot exist.

MASHHAD: POSITIVE EVIDENCE
1. A World Council of Shia Centers reprint attributed to Abna24 quotes Abbas Farazi Nia, a clerical committee member, regarding two accepted healings: a disabled woman from Bushehr reviewed after one year and a teenager with leukemia. It describes review of records, interviews and field investigation, followed by public ceremonial announcements. It supplies neither patient names nor signed medical conclusions, diagnoses/treatment histories, lab values or before/after images. It is one publication chain, not multiple independent investigations.
https://mail.wocoshiac.org/en/news-events/all-news-en/all-news-b-en/razavi-timpani-played-on-occasion-of-healing-of-two-pilgrims

2. AkramSadat SadatHoseini, Habib Shareinia and Shahzad Pashaeypoor of Tehran University of Medical Sciences nursing departments, with MohammadMehdi Mohammadi of Kermanshah University of Medical Sciences, published A cross-cultural concept analysis of healing in nursing: a hybrid model, BMC Nursing 22:252 (2023), DOI 10.1186/s12912-023-01404-8. The paper explicitly acknowledges the Commission for Healed Affairs of Astan Quds Razavi. This is substantive academic evidence for an institutional connection, not independent clinical certification of the miracle claims.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10391964/

3. Its section Constructing a model case describes a 50-year-old woman with eight years of T7-T8 paraplegia attributed to a spinal artery problem and bladder/bowel incontinence. After prayer at the shrine she reportedly stood, then regained independent walking after physiotherapy. The paper says before/after records were examined and MRI/NCV/EMG afterward were nearly normal, with committee approval. These are authors' assertions, not published numerical results or scans.
Material provenance limit: this appears in a conceptual model-case section within a qualitative study, not a separately documented neurological case report. The article does not identify the patient, event date, treating neurologists or a case-specific source. It does not establish whether this presentation is a single actual case, a composite, or a constructed illustration. Do not describe it as an authenticated spinal cord regeneration case. Authorship/affiliation does not make the nursing authors the patient's neurologists. The fieldwork comprised ten interviews (four nurses, two internists, two clerics and two laypeople), not ten verified cures. Supporting data are not public; reasonable requests are directed to corresponding author.

4. Original Asre Iranian issue, 31 July 2022, issue3612, p4, reports Abbas Farazi Nia's comments about a commission formed in Iranian year1396 (2017-18). This is a report of an institutional speech; it does not establish that all earlier healing review started then or that no older office existed. The web PDF text was checked; complete page visual inspection has NOT been completed in this pass. Avoid converting this to a century-long clinical certification record.
https://www.pishkhan.com/Archive/1401/05/14010509/AsreIranian651157310111448535784254.pdf

5. Shia Studies' World Assembly's 20 October 2019 compilation supplies many named and dated devotional stories, with some hospital/doctor references, but not actual diagnostic records. OTHER CANDIDATES CHECKED
Sikh Golden Temple, Amritsar: Times of India,19 July2007, interviews Jatinder Kaur and her father Malwinder Singh regarding restored eyesight after bathing about18months earlier. Head granthi Giani Gurbachan Singh and SGPC head Avtar Singh Makkar affirm the religious interpretation/recovery. Article supplies no cause of blindness, acuity before/after, named ophthalmologists or hospital reports. Genuine candidate for location-linked healing testimony; no comparable medical evidence recovered.
https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/blind-woman-claims-miraculous-cure/articleshow/2215824.cms

Hindu Muthuswamy temple, Tamil Nadu: R Raguram, professor of psychiatry at NIMHANS, A Venkateswaran, Jayashree Ramakrishna and Mitchell G Weiss (Swiss Tropical Institute) published a BMJ observational study in2002.31patients, average six-week stay, mean BPRS52.9to42.9, no comparison group. This is actual measured psychiatric symptom improvement and merits its own evidential credit. It does not document abrupt reversal of a destructive physical lesion. Authors suggest supportive environment as possible contributor and do not claim demonstrated divine causation. Do not ridicule or call all psychiatric recovery meaningless.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1123553/

Jewish Ohel, Queens: Moshe Grossman's first-person account on Chabad.org reports daughter's kidney recovery after shrine petition and his own suspected renal cancer. He names NYU, University of Pennsylvania and Fox Chase but does not supply original charts or physicians' signed statements. His own renal mass was removed surgically and proved benign; no preoperative malignant histology is supplied. Thus not an established biopsy-confirmed cancer disappearance. Do not confuse institutional names in the narrative with independent institutional endorsement.
https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/3555176/jewish/The-Ohel-and-My-Medical-Miracles.htm

Mashhad qualifies for examination in the Lourdes comparison because of recurring place-linked claims and institutional medical review; it is not excluded merely as an individual answered prayer. Whether it qualifies as evidentially comparable remains unresolved pending recoverable clinical data. No new tally point awarded either way. No messages sent and no restricted library accessed.
