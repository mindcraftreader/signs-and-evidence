PU XIAN, KUALA LUMPUR, 2011 — DEEP AUDIT
Research pass: 2 November 2026
Scope: contemporary reporting, witness independence, surviving recordings, scientific/physical examination, and religious significance. No tally changed.

ASSESSMENT

This is a genuine contemporary public miracle CLAIM with better documentation than the initial English summaries suggested. It should remain in the comparative library. It has not been demonstrated here to be a supernatural event, an engineered fraud, or an adequately documented counterpart to Fatima or Zeitoun. The most promising surviving evidence is accessible video, but its independence and image content require much stronger examination than the presence of several YouTube links supplies.

CONTEMPORARY NEWSPAPER RECOVERY

A March 20, 2011 Malaysian Buddhist Information Network page explicitly credits Guang Ming Daily. This is a contemporary reprint, not the original newspaper facsimile:
https://www.mybuddhist.net/cms/shishidongtai/bendixinwen/2011/16031.html

The report names recorders 陈锦霖, 高福海 and 杨萍 (Yang Ping). It describes February 27, approximately 5–6 p.m., with 20–30 initial observers growing to over 100; four bronze statues allegedly moved for about an hour. Its first-event wording is Chinese New Year's Eve “February 3,” a calendar inconsistency requiring preservation rather than silent correction. The reporter looked for visible seams, touched metal, heard a hollow sound when tapping, and saw no anomaly during an arranged Sunday visit. This was no technical teardown. The Malaysian Buddhist Association declined judgment without seeing it. Tony Chew's convinced skeptics are unidentified. The article admits discrepancies between live perceptions and videos, and says the outdoor levitation was not captured as such despite over ten phones. Yang Ping explicitly interprets the manifestation as confirming Dharma, their teacher, and correct practice. Five historical Sin Chew/Kooky media links are printed, including two interview entries. Thus this is not merely an English aggregator story; neither is it a controlled authentication.

Calendar cross-check: Hong Kong government's published 2011 holiday list identifies February 3 as Lunar New Year's Day, so its eve was February 2: https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/201005/07/P201005070112.htm

ACADEMIC TREATMENT

Tan Lee Ooi, who completed his PhD at the National University of Singapore and was identified by the book's 2020 author biography as head of Mass Communication at UOW Malaysia KDU Penang University College, includes “Case 3: The Urban Miracle of the Talking Buddha Statues” in chapter 6 of Buddhist Revitalization and Chinese Religions in Malaysia (Amsterdam University Press, 2020). Publisher/catalog information was checked. A later focused search recovered the indexed case text (pp. 157–159) through a third-party mirror; this case section was read, but NOT through an authenticated publisher/library copy. Its underlying footnote 83 was not recovered. Tan discusses media reception: amateur-looking footage helped believers regard the images as authentic, and phones/YouTube presented a modern, technologically mediated miracle. That is social analysis, not controlled verification that a metal mouth physically moved. Do not label Tan an eyewitness, optical expert, or scientific authenticator on this basis. The case text points to Guang Ming Daily (2011c), so academic citation alone does not add an independent event witness.

COMPARATIVE IMPLICATION

Do not exclude this case by redefining all non-Christian religious signs as private: the positive claim concerns a group in a religious institution and includes a religious endorsement interpretation. The right comparative question is whether the underlying extraordinary physical event is securely established. On the recovered evidence it remains materially weaker than a rigorously established mass public apparition: the affirmative witness pool lacks documented adversarial diversity; surviving media lack a demonstrated independent simultaneous camera record; and the visible light argument has a concrete ordinary imaging issue requiring testing. These are evidentiary distinctions, not a presumption that Buddhist supernatural agency is impossible.

The defensible current disposition is: retain as a public Buddhist miracle candidate, substantially improve its source provenance, and withhold both “authenticated supernatural counterexample” and “proven hoax.” No conclusion about the truth of Catholic or Buddhist metaphysics follows merely from this audit.

HIGHEST-VALUE NEXT WORK

Recover the original Guang Ming/Sin Chew issue and interviews; verify the Tan case against a publisher/library copy and retrieve its footnotes; identify the actual three named recorders and their unedited files; determine which recordings overlap in time; stabilize fixed background landmarks before estimating lip/eyelid displacement; compare highlight/brightness changes on statue and reference surfaces. An engineering inspection would have to document access, instrument methods and findings rather than merely an absence of visible seams.
