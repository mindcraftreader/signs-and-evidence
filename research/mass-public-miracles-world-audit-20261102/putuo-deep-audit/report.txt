MOUNT PUTUO: DEEP SOURCE AUDIT
Completed 2 November 2026. No debate tally has been changed.

RESULT
There is a genuine public Buddhist consecration event, named participants describing an auspicious sky display, and a recoverable official broadcaster documentary containing archival ceremony and sky footage. This is substantially better than an unsupported internet legend. The recovered record does not presently establish a detached, clearly identifiable supernatural Guanyin before thousands, nor a comparator of equal evidential strength to the strongest Fatima/Zeitoun claims under discussion. This conclusion follows from the recovered evidence; it is not a presumption that Buddhist miracles cannot occur.

WHAT THE VIDEO ADDS
The official 26-minute player works. Its page is an official educational archive dated 30 July 2013, with CCTV imagery and matching broadcaster program text published in 2006. The latter identifies the opening as 1997 archival footage. This establishes a useful broadcast chain, not an unedited original-camera chain of custody. The archival cameraman, original tape, recording settings, location and complete uninterrupted rushes remain unidentified. [P 01–P 03]

Direct visual inspection, not inferred from text: selected paused frames at 1:00 and 1:05 show a congregation/statue/ceremony; 1:10 and 1:35 show intense sunlight through an irregular opening edged by dark clouds. At these inspected sky frames I could not resolve a detached luminous human body. At 1:15 a monk is interviewed; a subtitle starts describing a strong beam of sunlight. At 1:25 the ceremony includes prostration. These observations support the occurrence of a striking common stimulus and crowd response. They do not authenticate every later description, nor demonstrate fraud. They are compatible with ordinary sunlight breaking through clouds. A religious interpretation of the timing remains a separate claim.

Scope: this was selected-frame inspection, not continuous viewing, not audio transcription, not full optical analysis. Additional sampled frames at 19:50, 20:44, 21:34, 22:20, 23:01, 23:30 and 24:04 show landscape, cave, vegetation, temple/monks/interviews; they did not add a second apparition recording. Selected timestamped screenshots are saved. The 1:10 file was corrected from an initial filename of 0010 to 0110. Do not describe these samples as a complete viewing.

THE NAMED POSITIVE WITNESSES
Tang Yixiong: his claimed firsthand pilgrimage account is preserved on the older linked Big 5 webpage and a dated 2013 repost. He names the pilgrimage organizer and gives an itinerary. The silver-white standing figure and pink lotuslike clouds belong to his account. The older page is undated; the 2013 date is repost publication, not proof of contemporary reporting. His conversation with an unnamed TV cameraman is one witness reporting another person's words. It cannot be counted as a named independent journalist's deposition, still less a hostile witness. [P 04–P 05]

Abbot Jieren: the full interview excerpt was recovered in a 2010 repost attributed through a 2007 forum to Phoenix's 21 November 2007 interview. He describes a round opening containing Guanyin's head; the program narrator supplies the twenty-plus-minute duration. Other heavenly figures are reported through his comments about what others saw. His 1994 account explicitly says Miaoshan's companions did not see that earlier figure: it is not a second mass apparition. [P 06]

Shen Renyan: a separately named text attributed to the Putuo Buddhist Institute was found in a 2011 repost. It describes an opening/light, colored rays and congregational reaction, and mentions press/TV reporting of the consecration. It is a useful third named claimed firsthand strand. Its original composition and publication date were not established. His attached essay cites a 1996 Buddhist periodical, but that essay is about earlier events, not a prediction or contemporary report of October 1997. [P 09]

CONVERGENCE AND VARIATION
The common core is a consecration, a change in the cloudy sky/light, a religiously interpreted display and a responding congregation. The recovered sources do not uniformly specify the same visible form or duration. Tang reports good weather and a standing figure; the abbot's retrospective describes heavy clouds and a head in an opening. A municipal chronicle's indexed passage gives about ten seconds, while the interview narrator and a meteorological-magazine excerpt give about twenty minutes. These may measure different phases; they cannot be silently harmonized or treated as a proven contradiction. Estimates of attendance vary from 4,000/5,000-plus to Tang's 10,000-plus. These are audience estimates, not counts of independently recorded visual experiences. [P 04,P 06,P 10,P 11]

CONTEMPORARY PRESS SEARCH
The association history verifies that press coverage existed and preserves a photograph of clippings/publications. I inspected that image. It shows construction/consecration material and a headline about the large statue's production and installation. Some small text is illegible. It does not yet give us a dated independent article expressly reporting an apparition. Shen names several broadcasters/newspapers, including People's Daily, but the actual relevant 1997 issues remain unrecovered. The distinction is important: coverage of a ceremony does not automatically validate every later miracle claim about that ceremony. [P 07–P 09]

ADVERSARIAL TESTIMONY AND SCIENCE
No positively attesting, named prior skeptic or adversarial eyewitness was recovered. CCTV's retrospective has skeptical editorial questions about whether the same sky event at another time/place would receive religious meaning. The program makers are commentators nine years later; they are not thereby adversarial 1997 eyewitnesses. Their affiliation cannot substitute for personal observation. Conversely, Buddhist affiliations do not disqualify the named claimants.

No calibrated event-specific optical study with images, camera geometry, weather logs and tested competing models was recovered. A 2014 Meteorological Knowledge excerpt on the Chinese digital science museum domain describes sunlight through a cloud opening, but the PDF now redirects to 404. Only its indexed paragraph was read; author and full title are unverified. It is not being counted as a peer-reviewed explanation or laboratory replication. A municipal chronicle explicitly leaves the cause unresolved. [P 01,P 10,P 11]

ASSESSMENT OF THE ALTERNATIVE
An ordinary cloud opening with sunlight is unusually well connected to the actual accessible images and to witnesses' own light/cloud language. It is a substantive candidate here, not just the word 'pareidolia' offered without reference to evidence. However, selected archival frames do not prove that all personal figure-perceptions were mistaken, and they cannot calculate the probability of the timing coincidence. No documented magician/hoax construction was recovered. The strongest defensible ordinary explanation currently covers a public light display interpreted religiously; it does not reconstruct every person's experience.

VINDICATORY FORCE
This is unquestionably a Buddhist setting and the positive sources understand the display as Guanyin's response to consecration. It therefore belongs in a fair search for public religious signs outside apostolic Christianity; it should not be excluded as merely an individual healing. What is not established is a clear, universally shared autonomous figure, a specifically recorded advance prediction of this appearance, repeated appearances comparable to Zeitoun, or a modern instrument study ruling out the visible ordinary-light account. It remains a relevant but substantially weaker comparator, not a demonstrated equal counterexample and not a proved fraud.
