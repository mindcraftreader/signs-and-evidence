PUTUO — JOINT SKEPTICAL REVIEW, 2 NOVEMBER 2026

USER-PROPOSED ORDINARY MECHANISM
Sunlight illuminating a highly reflective new statue after an overcast interval could produce glare and visual adaptation/afterimages. Observers looking from statue toward clouds might perceive a residual figure against the sky; recognizable cloud shapes and religious expectations could also contribute. This is a hypothesis, not an event-specific experimentally established explanation. The sunlight/statue connection is reported, but individual fixation histories, luminance, duration and retinal adaptation are unknown. A bright glint need not generate a detailed whole-statue afterimage. Two hours after sunrise on a cloudy morning is not itself proof of laboratory-style dark adaptation.

DOCUMENTARY DISTINCTIONS
Published attendance estimates range from about 4,000 to more than 10,000; the Buddhist Association gives over 5,000. These are not counts of separately documented matching observations. The recovered collection has five named claimed observers with unequal source proximity, plus reported unnamed groups; it is not a full witness census. No systematic contemporaneous survey establishing what fraction saw a recognizable figure was recovered.
Tang describes a standing silver-white figure in the sky with clouds beneath. Jieren describes a head inside a round opening. Wang, through a later study, explicitly describes a seated figure composed of colored clouds. Shen chiefly describes lights/cloud opening and crowd reaction. Variation alone is not disproof; the same rule applies to Catholic cases. Their descriptions should not be rewritten as identical or universally agreed.

PROVISIONAL ASSESSMENT
An ordinary light display with interpretation and potentially afterimages is a concrete candidate grounded in the reported setting and accessible frames. The specific afterimage reconstruction is unproven. The stronger detached-figure claim remains weakly corroborated by the inspected images. Thousands of individual statements are not a necessary standard, but multiple detailed independent accounts or clear shared camera evidence would help establish the claim. Neither fraud nor supernatural cause is settled. No tally change entered without joint review.
