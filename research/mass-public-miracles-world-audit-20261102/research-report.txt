WORLD RELIGIONS: MASS PUBLIC APPARITIONS AND MIRACLES
Fresh comparative audit, 2 November 2026

RESULT
This discovery-and-source-tracing pass found three substantive new candidates: Mount Putuo 1997, Ajmer 2002, and Kuala Lumpur moving-statue reports 2011. No equivalent to Fatima/Zeitoun is established by the evidence inspected. These cases have not been fully adjudicated in the conversation and must not be added as automatic wins to either side. The prior5–0 tally concerns the previously debated candidates only. This report does not retract that result or the concession about the prior investigated record. It expands the record with specific cases, rather than replacing public signs with private healings or reincarnation.

The strongest next figure-apparition lead is Putuo; the strongest newly recovered immediate mainstream newspaper trail is Ajmer. The latter's articles confirm a claimed appearance and substantial attendance, not a journalist's direct verification of a supernatural person. Neither a skeptical description of reflection nor a devotee's identification settles the cause by itself.

SCOPE AND METHODS
Modern press/scientific era, primarily20th/21stcenturies. Older press-era boundary cases were flagged, not declared false solely because of date. No case excluded because the country was poor, the witnesses non-Western, or a religion unfamiliar. Searches covered English, Arabic, Chinese, Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Indonesian and Bengali, with unequal depth and access. Traditions screened: Hindu, Buddhist/Chinese popular religion, Muslim/Sufi, Jewish, Sikh, Jain, Japanese/Shinto-derived movements, Baha'i, Zoroastrian, Protestant/Pentecostal, Latter-day Saint, and limited Indigenous leads. This is targeted multilingual web discovery and public-archive tracing, not a search of all newspaper runs in each language. No new Akron paywalled database pass completed in this run.

Standards: distinguish reporters' direct observations from their reports of claims; crowd attendance from verified observers; event date from document/publication/repost date; independent reporting from copied wire/religious accounts; a religiously meaningful sign from proof of an entire religion. A contender need not copy every Fatima feature: different strengths could compensate. Repetition, access, images/recordings, specific observable behavior, investigation and pre-event records when claimed matter together. Missing documentation is not proof of fraud. Ordinary causes need actual case-applicable evidence, not generic labels. Individual cures, reincarnation, incorruptibility and biographies remain outside this public-sign audit, except to mark why discovery leads are out of scope.

1. MOUNT PUTUO, CHINA, 30 OCTOBER 1997 — STRONGEST NEW FIGURE-APPARITION LEAD
Claim: during consecration of the Nanhai Guanyin statue, an opening in cloud and bright illumination were interpreted as Guanyin appearing; some accounts describe a silver-white standing figure and lotus-colored clouds.

Traceable sources:
P01 CCTV broadcaster text dated4 April 2006, Zhou Shan: Fo Zhi Ming Shan, explicitly introduces footage as recorded in 1997. It quotes sunlight from an opening in cloud and includes a retrospective interview with Abbot Jieren. The text also questions the interpretation and notes diverse perceptions. Broadcaster authorship and archival-footage reference are verified; this audit has NOT played and independently examined the original sky footage. An official program-player page and metadata were recovered: episode1,25:59, GUID7634bed33ee74f91a3368d3e2b9ad09f. Do not translate that into CCTV scientifically authenticated a deity or a movingfigure was captured clearly.

P02 Named eyewitness Tang Yixiong (唐毅雄), reproduced22 September 2013. He says he traveled with Putuo magazine's pilgrimage group, saw a silver-white standing Guanyin and pink lotuslike clouds for several minutes, and spoke to an unnamed television cameraman. This is a claimed firsthand account, not merely a modern narrator's assertion. Its ORIGINAL publication date and original magazine issue remain unverified. The named travel party and unnamed cameraman are leads, not additional independent statements obtained here.

He describes a round opening with Guanyin's head and other people's differing visions. The preceding 1994 vision attributed to Abbot Miaoshan was explicitly NOT seen by the accompanying people. Do not merge that private 1994 vision into a 1997 mass observation. The extract's narration gives over20 minutes for the process.

P04 Official Zhejiang-hosted Zhoushan municipal chronicle, page409, records the 1997 consecration and over5,000 participants. Another passage indexed from the same chronicle describes a Guanyin-like sunlight scene and over10 seconds. The page text was read through web retrieval; local curl download failed; the full PDF was not independently read or rendered. Government inclusion of an account is not a government laboratory finding. Association retrospective includes ceremony photos. Neither establishes that all5,000 personally reported the full figure.

https://zt.pusa123.com/zt/30093.html

Assessment: genuine candidate with identifiable religious imagery, a sizeable documented ceremony, multiple attributable narratives, broadcaster coverage, and an official archival-player lead. It cannot be dismissed as nonexistent. Yet same-week independent apparition coverage has not been recovered. The descriptions vary in weather, appearance and duration; different accounts might be timing different parts, so variation is not automatically disproof. A cloud opening and sunlight are ordinary events; whether anything beyond that appears in authenticated footage remains the central unresolved question. No established appointed public miracle prediction located. OPEN: inspect original footage, identify same-week clippings and original Tang publication, distinguish attendance from specific sightings, and compare the accounts without presupposing a common conclusion.

2. AJMER SHARIF, INDIA, MARCH 2002 — STRONGEST NEW IMMEDIATE PRESS TRAIL
Claim: images on a Sufi shrine dome, interpreted as Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti. Times of India29 March 2002 quotes shrine worker Syed Irfan Usmani claiming a face and half-body plus Kaaba imagery. It reports roughly 7,000gathering; AP quotes police superintendent Saurab Shrivastav on crowd control and reports over 5,0000visitors. Those counts concern attendance, not collected independent apparition statements. Police credentials support their reported administrative observations, not supernatural causation.

Assessment: contemporary mainstream documentation and a named direct claimant make this a real public-apparition comparison lead. The material recovered describes images ON a dome, not a detached moving person bowing or moving between roof structures. AP reports a television explanation involving recently applied reflective paint; the station, original investigation and optical tests were not recovered. A local allegation of financial motivation is testimony, not demonstrated fraud. The peace-after-Gujarat interpretation is a worker's inference, not verified spoken words of the apparition. OPEN: recover the television report, original photographs/video, direct reporter observation and additional identified independent witnesses. Not established as an equivalent; not proven fraudulent by this pass.

4. SABARIMALA, INDIA — HUMAN-LIT CEREMONIAL LIGHT
Shaju Philip, IndianExpress26April 2011, reports the Temple Affairs Department's admission in Kerala High Court that the makaravilakku was man-made. This is a concrete ordinary cause, not merely a skeptical guess. Distinguish that ceremonial fire from the separate star calledMakaraJyothi; terminology varies in newspapers. Some believers know the ritual is human-performed, so this is not proof every participant was deceived.
https://indianexpress.com/article/news-archive/web/makara-vilakku-at-sabarimala-manmade/
Disposition: not an authenticated supernatural rival. No automatic tally point for screening this new case.

CONCLUSION AND NEXT WORK

The public-vindicatory exclusivity argument has not been overturned by this fresh pass. A blanket assertion that no modern non-Christian public apparition has ever been reported would, however, be false: Ajmer and Putuo provide traceable examples. The live question is whether their strongest primary evidence survives comparison. Neither generic claims nor attendance establish parity. No new candidate has yet been established as that peer.

Inspect Putuo's actual archival footage and same-week reporting first; examine Ajmer's original television reflection report and images second. The Malaysian moving statues remain a third visual-inspection lead. No entire-world exhaustion claim is justified. Do not substitute private healings or reincarnation for the agreed category, or silently change the tally. This pass completes a broad multilingual discovery and source audit, not a forensic investigation of every candidate.
