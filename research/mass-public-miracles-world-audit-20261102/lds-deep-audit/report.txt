LDS / MORMON PUBLIC MIRACLE AUDIT
Completed 2 November 2026. No tally changes. This is a targeted deep pass, not a claim to have exhausted every LDS archive.

FINDING
Latter-day Saints do have documented public religious-sign claims and near-contemporary named vision reports. It would be wrong to say they have none, or that everything is an anonymous story written many decades later. This pass did not establish a modern LDS equivalent to the strongest Fatima/Zeitoun case proposed in our conversation. The strongest recovered records are: four 1956 Los Angeles temple letters; the 1986 Cokeville rescue/angel reports; and 2022 Praia temple prayer/rain. They differ materially in public visibility, specific doctrinal message, prior prediction and ordinary alternatives.

THE LIVING-PROPHET CLAIM IS REAL
The official 4 April 2026 solemn-assembly report identifies Dallin H.Oaks as prophet, seer and revelator and dates the new First Presidency to 14 October 2025. Do not reuse stale leadership snippets listing Nelson as the current president. This establishes what LDS members affirm; it is not itself evidence for a supernatural sign. [L 01]
https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/solemn-assembly-april-2026-general-conference

1. LOS ANGELES TEMPLE DEDICATION, 11–14 MARCH 1956
The University of Virginia Mormon Studies archive of David O.McKay diary excerpts preserves four letters written that month: Ramona Carlsen 14 March, Catherine Farey 16 March, Elden Young 19 March, and Lorraine Cravens through a 29 March forwarding letter. They describe luminous figures or deceased leaders associated with the living president. Farey and Young place some perceptions on closed-circuit television; companions are reported both seeing something and not seeing it. These are genuine named, near-event records in the recovered transcription, not four new interviewees independently examined in this audit. The compiler locates originals in a dedication scrapbook, which remains uninspected. No corresponding recording was recovered. [L 07]
https://mormonstudies.as.virginia.edu/david-o-mckay-diary-excerpts/temples-1936-62/

This is the strongest modern temple-vision evidence recovered here, because the time lag is short and the content does support LDS prophetic authority if accurate. It is still a small set of selected personal experiences during a large religious ceremony. Attendance at a dedication, or watching its television feed, cannot be counted as seeing the same supernatural figure. The TV detail gives an experimentally relevant question—what was actually in the feed, and what else could viewers have seen? No recording permits that test now. It would be overclaiming to announce either a demonstrated television artifact or a demonstrated supernatural broadcast.

2. COKEVILLE, WYOMING, 16 MAY 1986
A real bombing occurred in a crowded elementary classroom, and all 154 hostages survived, although many were injured. A genuine 19 May 1986 AP front-page article has been recovered and rendered. It reports incomplete detonation and contemporaneous uncertainty about the bomb's full potential. [L 13]
https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstream/handle/20.500.12255/293640/Big_Spring_Herald_1986_05_19.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1

The 18 May Los Angeles Times quotes principal Max Excell invoking angels. [L 14]
https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1986-05-18-mn-20910-story.html

The best recovered first-person apparition interview is Rachel Walker Hollibaugh's 2010 state-archive account. She describes white figures around the danger area, a familiar woman and later family-photo identification. She explicitly interprets the experience religiously. Its archival provenance is good, but the interview is 24 years later; the PDF itself is marked a first draft. We do not have the first 1986 statement against which to compare the recollection. [L 24]
https://wyospcr.wyo.gov/index.php/files/164/Cokeville-Bombings/122/Rachel-Hollibaugh-Interview.pdf

Investigator Ron Hartley's 2010 interview gives his and his wife Claudia's account of their son's report. He says he initially challenged the story; he was not himself an eyewitness to the child's angels. The parents differ on some remembered details, with some interpretation explicitly developing years later. These facts warrant source comparison, not an accusation of lying. [L 11]
https://www.wyohistory.org/oral-histories/lead-investigator-ron-hartley

Certified bomb technician Rich Haskell gives meaningful positive evidence: he reports cut wiring he could not explain and his resulting conviction in angels. The same interview supplies concrete physical mitigation: leaking gasoline made explosive powder into paste, while an open window and movable ceiling tiles vented the blast. That explains how the device failed to produce its intended destructive effect; it does not establish who cut a wire. The complete 1986 forensic file was not recovered. [L 10]
https://www.wyohistory.org/oral-histories/certified-bomb-technician-rich-haskell-1986-bombing-cokeville-elementary-school

Cokeville is a serious rescue/angel claim deserving its own file, not a circus trick to dismiss by analogy. It is not established that 154 people saw angels. The bombing is well attested by secular press; those articles are not 154 apparition depositions. Its reported content is protection and comfort in response to prayer, without a specific public revelation that the LDS Church alone is true. Under the user's distinction, it is not automatically a vindicatory mass sign. It must not be substituted for one merely because the setting is predominantly Mormon. KSL's later survivor interviews are supplementary reporting, not an independent contemporary confirmation of the angels. [L 12]
https://www.ksl.com/article/news/utah/remembering-the-cokeville-miracle/266164

3. PRAIA, CABO VERDE, 19 JUNE 2022
An apostle's public temple dedicatory prayer explicitly thanks Joseph Smith and the living prophet and asks rain for a drought-stricken country. Subsequent church coverage attributes that day's rain to the prayer. The church photographer's rain images have identifiable dates/captions, although this pass read their page captions rather than independently analyzing the images. This is a genuinely public weather claim in an LDS context. It is a request for blessing, not a recorded promise of a specified supernatural sign at a deadline. [L 18, L 20]
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples/dedicatory-prayer/praia-cape-verde-temple/2022-06-19?lang=eng
https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/miracle-of-rain-continues-cape-verde-following-temple-dedicatory-prayer

The important counterevidence comes from the proponent: Church News reporter Scott Taylor, who was present, explicitly acknowledges rain was forecast that day. His defense emphasizes prayer preparation over months, convenient timing after the last dedication session, and continued rainfall. That is positive religious interpretation of real rain, but not evidence that contemporary weather models could not account for it. [L 19]
https://pt.thechurchnews.com/2022/6/30/23211427/scott-taylor-oracao-apostolo-templo-praia-milagre-chuva/

Cabo Verde's meteorological institute INMG gives ordinary seasonal expectations; national broadcaster RTC reported its forecast on 21 June. That RTC story is AFTER the dedication, so it cannot by itself prove a pre-event forecast existed. Taylor's acknowledgment supplies that particular point. Exact original bulletin, hourly rain-gauge data and a quantitative rarity study remain missing. I have not calculated coincidence odds. [L 21, L 22]
https://www.inmg.gov.cv/index.php/servico-educativo/blog/111-previsao-climatica-sazonal-para-cabo-verde-2022
https://www.rtc.cv/noticia/noticia-details/estacao-das-chuvas-inicia-mais-cedo-em-meados-de-junho-e-terminara-mais-tarde-em-meados-de-outubro-inmg-10141?categoria_id=21

4. MODERN TEMPLE LANGUAGE THAT MUST NOT BE INFLATED
Greg Hill's firsthand 1995 Bountiful choir article uses angel language while describing spiritual support and choir preparation; it does not specify a shared visible angel. The 2024 Urdaneta article likewise reports personal impressions, shining countenance and thanksgiving for angelic singing. Its 66, 000 open-house visitors are not 66, 000 apparition witnesses. Palmyra 2000's public weather change was called miraculous, but no specific prior apparition announcement was recovered. These are useful records of lived religion, not equal mass-apparition comparators. [L 08, L 09, L 23]

5. PUBLIC PROPHECY TESTS
Nelson's October 2019 statement that the next April conference would be unforgettable is securely recorded beforehand. Its stated context is the planned bicentennial of Joseph Smith's First Vision. In April 2020 Nelson explicitly expressed his surprise at the empty auditorium. This is not a specified prediction of the pandemic. It is a commonly discussed claim checked here, not a claim that we have surveyed every LDS prediction. [L 15, L 16]
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2019/10/57nelson?lang=eng
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2020/04/11nelson?lang=eng

The stronger historical example is Joseph Smith's Civil War prophecy: the manuscript copy is dated early 1833 and publication preceded 1861. That genuine chronological advantage should be conceded. Its South Carolina language nevertheless arose during an already public nullification/secession crisis, and the full prophecy extends well beyond the later Civil War. A careful verdict cannot count a hit while omitting context and the rest of the forecast. It is outside the twentieth-century category and is not an apparition. [L 17, L 25]
https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/paper-summary/revelation-25-december-1832-dc-87/1

6. HISTORICAL CONTROLS:1836 AND 1893
Kirtland 1836 does have contemporary written visionary claims. BYU distinguished professor of Luso-Afro-Brazilian Studies Frederick G.Williams collects nine accounts concerning his ancestor's reported visitor. That does not mean nine people independently saw that visitor: Ebenezer Robinson expressly said he heard the testimony but did not see the angel. Joseph Smith Papers identifies other early diaries reporting visions. Neither dismissing all accounts as late nor inflating the congregation into uniform angel eyewitnesses is justified. [L 03, L 04]
https://byustudies.byu.edu/article/an-angel-or-rather-the-savior-at-the-kirtland-temple-dedication-the-vision-of-frederick-g-williams
https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/paper-summary/journal-1835-1836/188

Salt Lake 1893 has cited contemporary diaries as well as later reminiscences. Brian H.Stuy's Dialogue study quotes Church leader B.H.Roberts saying great angel visions were not numerous, while affirming profound spiritual experience. This is counterevidence to universal visible manifestation, not proof every vision false. LaRene Gaunt's official retrospective provides helpful original-source leads. The original Roberts manuscript and some cited periodicals were not independently inspected here. [L 05, L 06]
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/ensign/1993/03/the-power-of-god-was-with-us?lang=eng
https://www.dialoguejournal.com/articles/come-let-us-go-up-to-the-mountain-of-the-lord-the-salt-lake-temple-dedication/

Both historical cases deserve assessment if the time boundary expands; being nineteenth-century does not make them false. They do not supply modern photographs, video or hostile contemporary apparition eyewitnesses missing from the recovered modern comparisons.

COMPARATIVE JUDGMENT
The 1956 letters give the best recovered modern near-contemporary named LDS vision evidence. Cokeville gives the best recovered modern public crisis with both investigation and angel reports. Praia gives the cleanest public religious act followed by an observable environmental event, but an actual forecast-based alternative is acknowledged in the favorable account. None currently combines a specific advance public sign announcement, a large openly accessible shared apparition, named adversarial corroboration and authenticated observational evidence at the level claimed for Fatima/Zeitoun.

This is a limited negative search result, not proof that apostolic Christianity owns all miracles, nor a proof LDS theology false. Equally, a generic assertion that every religion has the same evidential case would be unsupported by this audit. No scientifically authenticated supernatural cause or equal modern doctrinally vindicatory mass apparition was established in the recovered LDS set.
