WHAT THIS ADDS

The video inspection clarifies the empirical procedure: Dalleur moves a ray probe
associated with the selected VP across separate features and judges compatibility.
He does not merely assert that any crisp edge proves a point source. Consequently
the prior isolated contact-hardening example does not test the whole reconstruction.
That calculation remains correct general optics, not a successful historical
counter-fit. No observed defect in the central calculation was established here.

The separate cue locations are useful internal consistency checks. Lines drawn
from a common hypothesized VP, however, are not twelve statistically independent
endpoint measurements merely because they converge by construction. The evidence
lies in their agreement with the actual photographic features. This distinction
does not prove those feature assignments wrong. A useful further audit would
record independently selected caster/shadow endpoints or supporting tangents and
their residuals against competing VP locations.

The pending raw scan would allow those selections and image-processing robustness
to be checked more directly. Its absence is not counterevidence, and does not make
the historical witness and press corpus wait upon photogrammetry.
