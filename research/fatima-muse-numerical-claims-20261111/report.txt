MUSE'S NUMERICAL AND HISTORICAL CAMERA CLAIMS: PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECK
11 November 2026

RESULT

The newly checked material supports important parts of Muse's defense. The
original Kinkaid press manual really recommends ordinary-to-long focal
lengths; the approximately44mm-equivalent example is reproducible. Measured
Dagor-type distortion is small in the relevant field-angle range. Independent
conditional calculations support Muse's explanation that differential
endpoint movement, rather than total pixel displacement, is the relevant
quantity for a shadow line's direction. In the models tested, distortion
changes source elevation by tenths or hundredths of a degree, not14degrees.

This strengthens the case against Bradley's generic lens-distortion objection.
It does not independently identify every historical shadow correspondence,
authenticate a universal Portuguese newspaper lens inventory, or establish
the complete joint uncertainty exclusion claimed in the response video.
No new ordinary whole-scene fit or reversal of the photographic tally results.

The fractional distortion is displacement/[f*tan(angle)]. Consequently,
30degrees gives0.243percent,35gives0.400percent, and40gives0.663percent.
The earlier correction is retained: the often-quoted0.24percent belongs to
30degrees in these rows, not35degrees. This is a small source-reference
correction, not a route to a14degree light-source correction.

Low distortion for these specimens supports Muse's lens-class argument.
It does not establish every lens in every Portuguese press inventory had
the same measured curve. Manufacturer correction claims, patents, measured
bench data and historical ownership are different evidence types.

3. DIFFERENTIAL ENDPOINT DISPLACEMENT: NEW CALCULATION

New reproducible script: check_distortion.py; full data: distortion-checks.json.
Uses standard-library arithmetic, with explicit0-distortion baseline tests.

For the simple radial map p -> p*(1+k*r^2), the local tangential and radial
stretch factors are1+q and1+3q,where q=k*r^2. The maximum possible local line
rotation, if|q|<=d, is bounded by asin[d/(1-2d)] for d<0.5. Therefore:
|q|<=0.005 -> worst local image-line rotation0.289degrees
|q|<=0.010 -> worst local image-line rotation0.585degrees

This is a model-specific statement about differential stretching. It is not
a bound for arbitrary high-order polynomials, decentering, scan warping or
all physical lenses merely having small absolute point displacements.
Image-line rotation is not identical to final source-elevation error.

To propagate to a reconstructed source, constructed four short segments
along Dalleur's adopted rays near the hat,left stone,left hand,right-pebble
regions. These are NOT newly measured historical caster/shadow pairs. This
conditional stress test corrects their endpoints, least-squares refits the VP,
corrects the adopted horizon's center point, then recalculates elevation.

At37mm equivalent:
simple-curve coefficient-0.5percent ->28.1767degrees,change-0.0619
simple-curve coefficient+0.5percent ->28.2976degrees,change+0.0590
simple-curve coefficient-1.0percent ->28.1119degrees,change-0.1268
simple-curve coefficient+1.0percent ->28.3540degrees,change+0.1154
Reference uncorrected value28.2386degrees.

At30mm equivalent, even the+1percent stress case yields31.9898degrees,
not42.72degrees. These coefficients refer to the stated radius normalization;
they are not recovered distortion parameters from D115.

For the37mm,+1percent case, the constructed near-center hat segment rotates
only0.017degree, while the right-edge segment rotates0.425degree. The endpoint
displacement difference is respectively0.018pixel and0.194pixel. The common
movement of both endpoints is substantially less important for line rotation.
The source VP may still move; common displacement does not imply a completely
invariant reconstruction. Symmetry also does not guarantee exact cancellation
for arbitrary, asymmetrically distributed cues.

A second calculation fits a three-term radial polynomial to Magill specimenB's
seven measured points. Maximum fit residual is0.00157mm. Transferring this
specimen's angular distortion curve into the same constructed-ray experiment
changes source elevation by+0.0417degree at37mm and+0.0843at30mm. This is a
physically informed illustration, NOT calibration of Ruah's actual lens.

Conclusion supported: small conventional radial distortion does not naturally
produce the needed gap in these tests. Conclusion not supported: every lens
and all possible combined image errors have now been mathematically excluded.

5. WHAT THE HISTORICAL RECORD SUPPORTS

The Science Museum's period Goerz-Anschutz specimen,120mm on a107.95mm-wide
plate, corresponds horizontally to40.02mm equivalent, consistent with the
ordinary range. The museum documents rising/cross-shifting front movements.
This supports historical plausibility of the camera class while showing that
centered principal point cannot be a mechanical certainty merely from its name.
https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co8085135/goerz-anschutz-press-camera

Earlier documented maker catalogues include ordinary reflex minimum focal
constraints, but also specialist wide-angle lenses and alternative bodies.
Some designs are unsymmetrical, including Tessar and Protar families. This
qualifies a literal claim that every period lens was symmetric; it does not
show they had enough distortion to explain D115, nor establish their use.

Fresh English/Portuguese searches did not recover a dated newspaper lens
inventory tied to Ruah or his actual exposure. The report therefore retains
Muse's historical preference as supporting evidence, without labeling his
universal inventory claim independently authenticated. This is not evidence
that an unusual camera actually was used.

6. COMBINED UNCERTAINTY AND DISPOSITION

The reproduced central value, focal-range sensitivity, local pitch/roll
derivatives, small-distortion tests and real examples favor robustness under
ordinary retained assumptions. They do not automatically yield a complete
joint acceptance region over every selected shadow,tangent,principal point,
horizon,lens curve,format and scan uncertainty. A critic should not bridge
the remaining gap by simply listing unknowns. Nor should an auditor call all
these bounds independently checked without the relevant data.

The main remaining empirical task is a raw-scan feature inventory plus jointly
tested scene/calibration constraints. No new large distortion, no completed
ordinary solar fit and no evidence of knowing deception by Dalleur or Muse
was found. Bradley's generic lens objection is weakened further by the new
calculations; the previous failed-camera-rebuttal adjudication remains intact.
No new independent victory point, no tally reversal, no calibrated odds.
