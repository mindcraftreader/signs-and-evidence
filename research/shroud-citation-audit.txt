SHROUD LINEN-DATING METHODS: CITATION AUDIT
Snapshot: 27 October 2026

Question: how often have other scholarly works referenced the specific linen-dating papers?
Primary source: OpenAlex work records and their incoming-citation lists.
Secondary cross-check: Crossref is-referenced-by-count.
All counts are database snapshots, not counts of successful replications or endorsements.

  DOI 10.1016/j.vibspec.2013.04.001
  OpenAlex 46; Crossref 43.
  OpenAlex includes 29 records typed article, 7 conference papers, 4 book chapters,
  2 other records, 1 paratext, 1 editorial and 2 dissertations.
  13 citing works share an indexed author with the founding paper.
  31 have authors recorded and no indexed author overlap; 2 have no author data.

  2 reference entries and 1 dissertation.

WAXS method, 2019:
  DOI 10.3390/heritage2040171
  OpenAlex 23; Crossref 21.
  Includes 19 articles, 2 other records, 1 reference entry and 1 dissertation.
  6 share an author; 17 have no indexed author overlap.

There are 68 distinct OpenAlex citing-work IDs across these three founding papers.
That is database-ID deduplication only, not a claim of 68 independent experiments.
Some content has separate book/chapter or translation records; the raw WAXS list
includes a retracted article. These records remain in the raw counts for consistency
with the radiocarbon benchmarks; they are not endorsements. Do not silently turn
counts of all indexed work types into counts of peer-reviewed journal articles.

FTIR: Barta et al., Effect of heating and neutron irradiation on the FTIR dating,
Microchemical Journal 208 (2025), 112502, DOI 10.1016/j.microc.2024.112502.
Different author team directly examines the proposed approach under heating and
irradiation. Institutional abstract reviewed; full experiment not retrieved here.

Publisher abstract describes material, dye and degradation analysis of wool.
It is outside scholarly citation; it is not a reported application of the linen

INTERPRETATION
All four methods have measurable scholarly uptake. The FTIR/Raman foundation
has several dozen citations including substantial outside-author engagement.
Mechanical dating has fewer citations and much of its indexed uptake is in
conference and book/reference material. WAXS has two dozen citations to the
method paper, with outside materials-science and heritage engagement.
These results warrant more specific language than calling the work unaccepted
without identifying the intended meaning of acceptance. Under the user's
proposed metric (being referenced), all four pass a nonzero-uptake threshold.

Citation counts have diminishing informational returns; 14,592 citations do
not make a result 317 times as likely to be correct as one cited 46 times.
A reference may report, apply, review, criticize or merely mention a method.
Author-ID overlap measures shared authorship, not complete institutional or
intellectual independence. Missing author metadata and database coverage also
limit interpretation. No probability of Shroud authenticity is inferred here.
A citation count cannot independently settle sampling representativeness.

REPRODUCTION AND UNIVERSITY-LIBRARY CROSS-CHECK
Retrieve https://api.openalex.org/works/https://doi.org/{DOI}
and read cited_by_count. Retrieve /works?filter=cites:{OpenAlexWorkID}&per_page=200
for incoming citations to the small method papers. Crossref:
https://api.crossref.org/works/{DOI}, message.is-referenced-by-count.
Raw responses are in raw-records/. Citing lists retain all indexed records.
The two unsuccessful alternate-DOI lookups were not treated as separate works.
For Scopus or Web of Science, search exact DOIs and export citing records with
DOI, title, year, authors and document type. Keep database counts separate;
do not average them or add them. Library access is useful for this cross-check
and for full handbook/chapter texts, but was not necessary for the public audit.
