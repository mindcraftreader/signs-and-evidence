POSTULATOR'S RESPONSE LOCATED: PROVENANCE AND ACCESS
30 October2026

New finding: Saverio Gaeta and Andrea Tornielli's preface to Padre Pio. L'ultimo sospetto (Piemme, February2008), reproduced by Totus Tuus29February2008, attributes this statement to Fr. Paolino Rossi, postulator of Pio's cause, in Famiglia Cristiana45/2007,p53:
Tutto quello che è stato spacciato come inedito o come rivelazione era già stato ampiamente chiarito durante il processo di canonizzazione.
The next sentence says Pio otherwise could not have been raised to the altars. The preface's context explicitly includes Luzzatto's2007chemical-procurement allegations.

Independent press-reception study by Simona Cantoni, hosted by ISREC Bergamo, identifies interview False ombre su Padre Pio,11November2007, and quotes Rossi saying Luzzatto presents already-known and refuted material as new. This confirms the existence/content of an interview at a general level; it is not another tribunal decision or separate direct chemical experiment.

Primary source still missing: original Famiglia Cristiana issue45/2007,p53/full interview scan. Significance: More specific than an inference from canonization alone. A cause official publicly reported that the allegations had been addressed/clarified within the canonization process.
