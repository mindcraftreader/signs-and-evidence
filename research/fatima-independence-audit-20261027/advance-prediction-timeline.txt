FÁTIMA — DOCUMENTARY TIMELINE BEFORE THE OCTOBER 13 EVENT
7 November 2026

This timeline follows the dates of records or publications. Where the date
belongs to an interview whose surviving write-up is later, the entry says so.
The subject is the October gathering and the promised public sign.

Circa 27 May 1917 · Parish interview · Schedule
Lúcia told Manuel Marques Ferreira that the Lady had asked her to return
monthly for six months and would disclose what she wanted at the end.
The editors approximate the interview date. This sets out the series.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-1

Shortly after 13 June · Parish interview · Schedule
Lúcia said the Lady asked her to return on the 13th and learn to read so
that the Lady could tell her what she wanted. The exact interview date is
unspecified. This continues the monthly schedule.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-2

14 July · Parish interview · Public miracle promised in three months
Describing the previous day's encounter at Cova da Iria, Lúcia said she
asked for a miracle so everyone would believe. The reply promised to make
everyone believe in three months. Counting from July 13 gives October 13.
The text gives the interval and purpose. It leaves the form unspecified.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-3

23 July · O Século · Earlier public coverage of the series
The Meia Via correspondent reports the July gathering and an announced
return on the 13th a month later. The article shows that the meetings
were already public news. Its next date is August 13.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-3

19 August · O Mundo, front page · October 13 date in print
José do Vale reports that the Lady would return to the same place each
month and descend for the final time on October 13. He connects that visit
with peace and the end of the war. The article gives a public date for
the finale, with the war claim as that newspaper's reported wording.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-10

21 August · Parish interview · Final visit and associated visions
Lúcia reported the Valinhos encounter of August 19, a request to return to
the Cova, an identity disclosure on the last day, and the coming of Saint
Joseph with the Child Jesus, Christ and Marian figures. The text also
mentions a miracle in connection with the children's August detention.
The editor assigns the interview date from Ferreira's parish report.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-5

1 September · Raio de Luz · October 13 date in print
The magazine's introduction names October 13 as the day the Lady would
tell the children what more she wanted of them. The letter published with
it discusses unusual appearances at an earlier gathering. The October
statement specifies a disclosure to the children.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-20

4 September · Soldier's letter from France · Date and public miracle
António Ferreira de Andrade wrote to Lúcia's father that a fellow soldier
from the area, passing on family news, had told him that Our Lady would
perform a miracle on October 13 that everyone would marvel at. This is a
dated private letter showing that the explicit promise had reached France
39 days before the gathering. Andrade was reporting what he had heard.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-22

8 September · Carlos de Azevedo Mendes's letter · Date and disclosure
Mendes wrote after questioning the children separately on September 7.
He said they expected the Lady to reveal who she was and what she wanted
on October 13, after six visits. The quoted passage concerns the finale
and disclosure. DCF Selection doc. 7, printed p. 43, PDF p. 45.
https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/fontes/F001_DCF_selecao.pdf#page=45

15 September · Parish interview · Explicit date and expected visions
Reporting the September 13 encounter, Lúcia told Ferreira that the Lady
asked her to return to the same place on October 13. The account also
mentions Saint Joseph bringing peace and Christ blessing the people.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-8

Around 17 September · António dos Santos Alves interview · Supporting context
The priest's account of his conversation with Lúcia and Jacinta describes
the figures expected in October. The manuscript is undated, and the
editors estimate a date around September 17 for the interview material.
The document includes a later October 13 addition, separate from the
September account. Its advance material concerns the expected visions.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-9

27 September · Formigão interview · Exact date and purpose
Lúcia said that on October 13 the Lady would make people believe she
really appeared. She also said the Lady would disclose her identity and
mentioned Saint Joseph, the Child and peace. Formigão's surviving write-up
speaks prospectively about the coming October 13 as a test of the claims.
This is another interview with Lúcia. The soldier's earlier letter gives
a separate record of the prediction's circulation.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-10

By 11 October · Printed remembrance · Public scheduling context
A surviving Our Lady of Peace print bears the October 13 date. The DCF
editors date its production using the advertisement in O Mensageiro of
October 11. It shows preparations for the gathering.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-43

11 October · Formigão interview · Later write-up of an advance interview
In the surviving account Lúcia explicitly says the Lady promised a
miracle so people would believe she appeared. The DCF editors judge that
the literary write-up was probably made after October 13. The interview
predates the event. The exact wording in the original working notes was
unverified in this pass, so the write-up is supporting evidence.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-11

13 October, morning · O Século · Published before the display, on the day
Almeida's preview names that day's expected visit and says the Lady
would explain her reasons. The article refers to earlier unusual sky
reports and asks what would happen that day. The original article as
reproduced in Fátima 50 was visually checked on PDF page 4. The language
about a perceptible proof for all on PDF page 6 belongs to Almeida's
October 29 retrospective, a separate article.
https://hemerotecadigital.cm-lisboa.pt/Periodicos/Fatima50/N06/N06_master/Fatima50_A1_N06_13Out1967.pdf#page=4

The July interview, September 4 letter and September 27 interview form
the main documentary chain for the public sign. I located no record made
before the display specifying that the Sun would move or change color.
Lúcia's explicit reference to a sign in the Sun in her July 1924 official
interview is retrospective.
https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/sel-82
