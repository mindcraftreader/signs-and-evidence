This correction does not establish the reverse probability by itself. The audit

2. ASYMMETRIC STANDARDS

while allowing generic possibilities about perception, clouds, expectations or

not historical establishment. Failed developed challenges cannot keep their full

There is also an opposite risk: demanding that every ordinary alternative fully
reconstruct every detail before it can receive any evidential weight. Historical
inference can use partial explanations and source criticism. The same standard
must apply to both sides. A component analogy is neither a complete rebuttal nor
automatically worthless. Existing Coelho and odor entries already preserve their
limited observations without declaring a complete ordinary explanation.

Daniel supplies a cumulative argument for the latter; it is not automatically

The main elevation arithmetic was reproduced using Dalleur's inputs. The shadow

But reproducing a calculation from the author's inputs is not an independent
measurement of those inputs. A conditional pixel sketch likewise is not a full

The actual retained thread-pixel estimates were around4.6 degrees, with spread;
the compared cameras predicted roughly1–2 and9–11 degrees. The tested alternative

The report itself correctly records these limits. No new contrary fit or fatal
error is established by stating them again.

Therefore neither 'photogrammetry has been refuted' nor 'our work independently
proved an impossible light source' accurately describes what this research did.
The source of the remaining uncertainty is identifiable; it is not a newly found
defect in Dalleur's paper or an automatic reason to discard the argument.

5. TESTIMONY AND PHYSICAL MEASUREMENT MUST REMAIN DISTINCT

Contemporary witnesses and press establish substantial evidence of reported

not make the reports false or supply their cause. Photogrammetry is potentially
an additional physical constraint, subject to its particular inputs and limits.

Regional accounts retain affirmative value, including early records. Their
different dates, proximity to firsthand observation and reported phenomena must
remain visible. The records recovered do not supply a synchronized triangulation
dataset. Daniel does not need surveyors for regional corroboration to matter.
