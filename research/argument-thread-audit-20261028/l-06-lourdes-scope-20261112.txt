L-06 — LOURDES: THE "IMMACULATE CONCEPTION" NAME AND PRESERVATION
Scope note, 12 November 2026. No point either way. Main tally 20–0.

1. THE NAME (25 MARCH 1858)
Established beyond reasonable doubt: Bernadette reported the Lady's words
"Que soy era Immaculada Councepciou" ("Je suis l'Immaculée Conception") on
25 March 1858.
- Peyramale to Bishop Laurence, 10 April 1858 (Laurentin, LDA II,
  pp. 154–156), quotes the words and refers to his lost report of 25 March.
- Procureur impérial Dutour, report of 14 April 1858 (LDA II, pp. 174–175),
  a hostile official, records it as a bold device borrowed "au dogme
  récemment promulgué".
- Bishop Laurence's mandement of 18 January 1862 (reprinted in Bertrin,
  Lourdes: histoire critique, 1905, pp. 429–433) links it to the 1854
  definition.
The grammar (the dogma's abstract noun used as a self-designation) troubled
Peyramale, Rességuier (7 April 1858) and Dutour within weeks; it was not
invented later. No earlier use of the self-designation was found.

Not established: that Bernadette had never heard the phrase. Claims of her
ignorance are later and from believers (Fourcade 1868, p. 38; Estrade 1899,
via Bertrin pp. 63–64, verified in the local copy). Exposure is documented
around her: the parish Children of Mary under the invocation of the
Immaculate Conception; the 8 December feast; Bishop Laurence's support for
the definition; medals; the crowd singing "Ô Marie, conçue sans péché".
Dutour judged she had not found it on her own; no coach was identified, and
Peyramale reacted with disbelief. Defence points retained: circulating
forms used the participle ("conçue sans péché"), not the noun as a name;
she was illiterate, catechised irregularly in patois, and mispronounced the
words ("Concheption").

Agreed scope: the name counts as content pointing Lourdes to Rome (if the
apparition is genuine, the Lady names herself by a dogma only Rome had
defined). It is not independent proof of a supernatural source. Daniel's
Lourdes argument rests mainly on the directed spring and the documented
healing record, held within the cumulative case anchored by Fátima.

2. PRESERVATION
Daniel reaffirms (12 November) that Bernadette's preservation is supporting
context, not a core claim, as in lourdes-claim-scope-20261106.txt. Recorded
limits: Itigilov remains a scored non-Catholic preservation comparator;
Orthodox incorrupt relics (e.g. St Spyridon, St Alexander of Svir) exist
but were not examined; Bernadette's 1925 examination reported preserved
internal tissue with darkened skin, and wax masks cover the face and hands;
canonization causes do not accept incorruption as a required miracle.
