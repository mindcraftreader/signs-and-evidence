THREE-CASE ARGUMENT AND PRIORITY CHALLENGES
28 October 2026

PURPOSE AND PRESENT STATUS
This is a plan for assessing Daniel's cumulative case, followed by the first documented challenge. It is not a completed naturalistic explanation or twelve scored victories. The present research does not justify saying that all three cases have been explained away or that Christianity has thereby been rejected. Questions, missing documentation and conceivable mechanisms must not masquerade as positive counterevidence.

DANIEL'S ARGUMENT IN ITS STRONGEST CURRENT FORM
Fátima supplies a documented advance announcement of a public sign on an appointed date, a large public experience, contemporary reporting including witnesses outside the devotional circle, and reports beyond the Cova. The claim does not require that the astronomical Sun moved or that every observer saw precisely the same thing. The proposed agency is a Marian intervention serving an explicitly Christian message. The photography and drying reports are additional corroboration rather than indispensable premises.
Zeitoun supplies repeated public luminous-figure reports over several years, religiously meaningful gestures and location, witnesses across religious boundaries, and contemporary institutional attention. Large attendance matters; an exact million unique observers is not necessary. The church was Coptic Orthodox. The strongest government claims previously withheld for inadequate primary documentation are not silently reinstated as premises.
Pio supplies a different kind of evidence: a decades-long wound history with named medical examinations and reports of unusual persistence and bleeding, alongside repeated fragrance testimony extending to objects and places. The user's present core does not depend on fever, every confession story, bilocation, a particular healing or a one-week scarless disappearance. Variation in the wounds has already been acknowledged; noticing variation again earns no new concession.
Taken together, these cases concern different phenomena in different settings and converge on apostolic Christian meaning. An optical account of a solar experience would not explain skin lesions, nor would an account of lesions explain a sustained public apparition. The argument presents purposeful Christian agency as a positive explanation, rather than merely identifying an absence of explanation. Independent philosophical and historical arguments enter the wider worldview comparison. Establishing modern intervention would also change the prior plausibility of older miracle claims, including the Shroud discussion.
The requested standard is a comparative judgment on the balance of evidence. Neither a numerical 98/2 claim nor a claim of absolute certainty is required. Falsification need not provide a complete replacement explanation, but an objection must identify an actual defect that matters to the inference. Counting points is not assigning independent probability increments.

PRIORITY CHALLENGES
The order starts with Bignami as requested. Items after the first are investigation targets, not newly adjudicated objections.

1. PIO: WHAT DID THE BIGNAMI/FESTA CHEMICAL CONTROLS EXCLUDE?
Status: documented correction plus an open substantive challenge, P-02.
Actual finding: Ethan Muse's current footnote 41 withdraws his earlier use of Festa's alcohol/starch test to exclude recent carbolic acid. Festa described tests directed at iodine. This removes one purported exclusion of phenol; it does not establish phenol caused the wounds.
Counterweight: the friars' sworn report records continued bleeding and no healing during eight days without medicine applied at dressing changes, and removal of iodine. Paolino's later memoir describes wider medicine removal, seal checks and daily redressing. The later account must not be erased just because the short report names only iodine.
Material task: establish what chemical-maintenance hypothesis could survive these actual controls. No evidence of broken seals or covert application has been established. If the controls adequately exclude a proposed cause, that cause must lose weight.

2. PIO: CAN AN ORDINARY WOUND PROCESS FIT THE LONGITUDINAL RECORD?
Status: incomplete ordinary explanation, not a diagnosis.
Material task: compare the same sites across dated examinations and test whether recurrent or maintained superficial lesions could fit their persistence, distribution, reported bleeding and lack of expected deterioration. Merely noting that chronic ulcers or factitious lesions exist is insufficient. A proposal must also accommodate the observations made under supervision. No broad accusation of psychopathy follows from considering a mechanism.
Useful analogue, not a replication: Wioletta Barańska-Rybak and colleagues, Medical University of Gdańsk, describe a ten-year dermatitis artefacta course (2011). This shows that diagnostic delay can occur without knowing medical accomplices; it does not reproduce Pio's reported combination of findings. Full clinical applicability requires further examination.

3. PIO: ARE THE BLOOD-LOSS CLAIMS ACTUALLY MEASURED?
Status: quantitative inference needs audit.
Material task: separate bloody exudate volume, whole-blood volume, stained-cloth appearance, measured haemoglobin concentration, and the dates of blood counts. A large daily whole-blood loss sustained for decades is a stronger claim than repeated visibly bloodstained dressings. Defeating an inferred daily quantity would narrow a physiological argument, not show the wounds or blood were fictitious.

4. PIO: WHAT POSITIVELY EXCLUDES AN ORDINARY SOURCE FOR THE FRAGRANCE?
Status: source not established either way.
Material task: choose the best specific observations, particularly unsuspecting observers, otherwise isolated objects and reports remote from Pio. Record proximity, timing, access and whether the source was actually isolated. Real fragrance can be sincerely reported without its cause being established. Conversely, proposing perfume without evidence of an adequate source, access and distribution is not a sufficient response to the whole case. No claim of a decades-long perfume conspiracy has been demonstrated.

5. FATIMA: WHAT EXACTLY WAS PREDICTED BEFORE OCTOBER 13?
Status: public-sign promise survives; predictive specificity remains a comparative issue.
Material task: fix the pre-event wording and what it specified. An appointed public sign is a genuine success condition. The prediction also organized a crowd and attention; a naturalistic account can use that documented causal consequence. It must still explain why the subsequent experience was striking and public. Do not reopen the defeated unconditional-war-prophecy objection or claim the absence of predicted detailed choreography defeats the public-sign promise.

6. FATIMA: HOW MUCH CAN PERCEPTION AND ORDINARY LIGHTING ACTUALLY EXPLAIN?
Status: experimentally supported components, incomplete event explanation.
Material task: test a concrete combination against motion, colour, environmental illumination and reports from people not continuously looking at the Sun. Retinal afterimage research establishes mechanisms for some visual effects; it does not recreate the entire Fátima event. Calling everything mass hallucination is not an adequate account.
Primary mechanism reference: Qasim Zaidi (SUNY College of Optometry), Robert Ennis, Dingcai Cao and Barry Lee, Neural Locus of Color Afterimages (2012), Current Biology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2011.12.021
Auguste Meessen's proposed Fátima account and personal observations are a hypothesis, not a validated reproduction of every reported feature. https://www.meessen.net/AMeessen/Apparitions_and_Miracles_of_the_Sun.pdf

7. FATIMA: DOES THE PHOTOGRAPH REQUIRE A DISPLACED LIGHT SOURCE?
Status: geometric calculation reproduced previously; no demonstrated refutation.
Material task: test the feature correspondences, perspective, surface geometry, mixed illumination and combined uncertainty using the best available image. Philippe Dalleur (Pontifical University of the Holy Cross) infers approximately 25–32 degrees against a solar elevation near 42.7 degrees. The relevant discrepancy is about 11–18 degrees, rather than every larger number used in conversation. An inadequate uncertainty estimate is a question to investigate, not proof of a wrong result. If the result survives robust testing, it strengthens Daniel's case.
https://doi.org/10.12775/SetF.2021.001

8. FATIMA: DO THE DISTANT REPORTS ESTABLISH DIRECTIONAL CONVERGENCE?
Status: meaningful remote testimony; strongest triangulation formulation unestablished.
Material task: distinguish seeing unusual solar effects from seeing descent, and descent from a measured bearing toward the Cova. Preserve report date, witness location, awareness of the gathering and publication chain. Earlier work recovered a near-contemporary Granja report, but it was indirect and concerned rotation; stronger directional descriptions recovered through later interviews have different evidentiary strengths. No common false label should erase those distinctions. This challenge bears on triangulation, not the existence of remote testimony as such.

9. ZEITOUN: CAN A CONCRETE ORDINARY ACCOUNT FIT THE BEST PROLONGED FIGURE REPORTS?
Status: no adequate ordinary explanation established in this review.
Material task: select one of the strongest episodes and align independent observers' timing, positions, figure detail, gestures, duration and occlusions. The objective is to test a real optical or manufactured-source proposal against a complete episode. Cynthia Nelson's brief interpretive ambiguities cannot substitute for this work. Neither a possible reflection nor an isolated mistaken identification explains all prolonged figure reports.
Primary committee report: https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/report/report-of-the-committee-formed-by-his-holiness-the-pope-to-verify-the-authenticity-of-the-apparitions-of-the-virgin-mary/

10. ZEITOUN: WHAT LIGHT-SOURCE EXCLUSION WAS ACTUALLY PERFORMED?
Status: narrower lights-out observations supported; comprehensive exclusion not documented.
Material task: distinguish disconnected local streetlights from a verified suburb-wide grid shutdown, and reported equipment searches from a recovered search log with locations and times. A local cutoff excludes some lighting sources. It does not by itself prove the absence of every independently powered or distant source. However, the mere conceivable availability of another source is not evidence that a suitable apparatus existed or explains the observed figure. Do not restore unverified five-kilometre blackout, Nasser or nightly police-count claims only to knock them down.
Ministry report: https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/report/ministry-of-tourism-department-of-inquiries/

11. CUMULATIVE: WHICH EVIDENCE IS CONDITIONALLY INDEPENDENT?
Status: valid methodological requirement; no numerical discount calculated.
Material task: separate repeat publications of one testimony from independently recorded observations. Account for known shared interpretive traditions without asserting that the events themselves were copied or that all testimony is dependent. Geographical and phenomenological differences supply real additional evidential value. A dependency adjustment needs a reason and a scale; dependency alone is not an explanation or a blanket cancellation of the three cases.

12. CUMULATIVE: WHAT DOES THE COMPARATIVE RELIGIOUS RESULT DISCRIMINATE?
Status: bounded comparative search; no equally strong whole combination identified, no proof of universal absence.
Material task: apply the same documentary, public-observation and institutional standards to competing modern cases. Inferior comparisons should not be inflated to parity. Also distinguish evidence for purposeful Marian/Christian intervention from a demonstration of every proposition in apostolic Christianity. This is a scope-of-inference challenge; it does not independently undo any event. Philosophical arguments on Daniel's side must be considered in the wider worldview assessment rather than dismissed as irrelevant.

FIRST DETAILED CHALLENGE: BIGNAMI
Primary source: Capuchin editor Gerardo Di Flumeri, Le stigmate di Padre Pio da Pietrelcina (1995 edition), reproducing the earlier records.
Amico Bignami: professor of medical pathology, Royal University of Rome.
Giorgio Festa: examining surgeon in Rome.
Paolino da Casacalenda: convent superior and participant in the bandaging procedure.

Document 14, printed page 64 (PDF page 34): sworn attestation signed by Paolino, Basilio and Ludovico. The editor assigns July 1919; the short original text is undated. It reports eight days of unchanged wounds except bright red colour on the last day, daily blood and greater bleeding during the final Mass. It says no medicine was used in making the dressings and specifically records removal of the iodine bottle. These are positive observations against an uncomplicated rapid-healing prediction.

Document 25, printed pages 82–84 (PDF pages 43–44): Paolino's later memoir says all medicines, especially carbolic acid, were prohibited and removed. It describes daily removal of old dressings after seals were checked and fresh sealed dressings applied. He says the seals were always found intact. This is affirmative evidence of controls; it is not evidence that the seals were broken, or that daily changes enabled fraud. Its later date and additional detail should be compared with the shorter attestation, without assuming either version is automatically false.

Document 53, Festa's 31 August 1920 report, printed pages 210–211 (PDF page 107): Festa describes alcohol and glycerinated starch in connection with detecting/removing iodine pigmentation. He reports no iodine revealed and persistent lesions after withdrawal of iodine. This supports a challenge to Bignami's iodine-based explanation. It does not report a validated exclusion test for phenol.

Muse's current footnote 41 explicitly acknowledges that his earlier use of this test against carbolic acid was an error and limits its exclusionary value to iodine.

ASSESSMENT AND TALLY
The present challenges are unequal. Fátima has researched perceptual mechanisms for components of the reports; a complete explanation still has to fit the prediction, reporting and strongest environmental/remote claims. Pio has a documented chemical-use context and an actual correction to a chemical-exclusion argument, but no demonstrated ordinary explanation of the full wounds-and-fragrance combination. Zeitoun currently has the weakest developed positive ordinary countercase in this review.

A full rebuttal to this cumulative argument must make substantive progress across all three. Listing twelve questions does not accomplish that. Nor would disproving one case erase the other two. Explaining these three ordinarily, if accomplished, would itself address this miracle-based argument rather than independently disprove Christianity.
