ASSESSMENT

The geometrical method is legitimate, the principal arithmetic reproduces, and the reviewed critics have not supplied an ordinary-solar fit satisfying the retained image constraints. Muse's defenses address several objections that the assistant has repeatedly described too generically. Dalleur's inferred anomalous lighting deserves substantive positive evidential weight in the cumulative Fatima case.

This is not an independent replication of every image measurement. The remaining measurement-verification question is real, but it must not be described as an identified error, a successful refutation, or a reason to assign the photographic argument zero weight. Neither this audit nor the absence of a successful countermodel establishes divine causation by itself. No probability or new tally point is assigned.

WHAT CHECKS OUT

Object-shadow correspondences locate a projected light direction without knowing object heights or physical shadow lengths. The receiving surfaces need not share one plane. Farid independently explains this construction. Perspective preserves the necessary line incidence, not arbitrary Euclidean angles. Dalleur's actual equations account for perspective; an imprecise spoken description of preserved angles does not invalidate that calculation.

Re-running the preserved calculation gives elevations31.8593,30.2417,28.2386 and24.7624degrees at30,33,37 and45mm equivalent focal lengths. The corresponding differences from42.72degrees are10.8607,12.4783,14.4814 and17.9576degrees. These are conditional calculations, not a statistical confidence interval. Independent differentiation using the displayed rotation matrices reproduces pitch0.82401866 and roll-0.56458452 at37mm.

The same published-input check requires a large horizon-only or vertical-VP-only change to reach the solar elevation. Those checks rebut a handwave about a tiny isolated camera correction; they are not bounds on simultaneous changes to every input.

REMAINING LIMITS, STATED PRECISELY

1. Measurements. The published figures identify the selected features and give a fitted VP, but this review did not recover a complete per-feature endpoint table, residual list and quantified joint acceptance region excluding the solar solution. We have not independently reselected the original6K features. This is a verification limit, not evidence the selections are wrong. The convergence itself must count positively. Separate locations do not automatically establish statistically independent selection errors.

2. Lens bounds. Dalleur and Muse provide relevant historical and image-based constraints. Exact make/model is not logically necessary if plausible distortion can be bounded. Magill's measurements support the small-distortion premise. However, this audit did not authenticate an exhaustive1917 newspaper equipment inventory or reproduce Muse's claimed subdegree propagation for every candidate lens and every actual cue. Failure to complete that check is not a discovered large distortion.

3. Validation. Real known-source examples exist and support the procedure. They are not a measured error distribution for D115. A new blind study is one possible strengthening, not a mandatory prerequisite for historical inference: sufficiently justified worst-case measurement bounds could also establish exclusion.

4. Drying. The garment-related pattern is meaningful corroboration in the context of rain and drying testimony. A single monochrome exposure does not independently measure initial water content, elapsed time or infrared flux. The unsupported photographic-defect suggestion has not been established. That does not make instantaneous high-energy IR the only interpretation measured by the photograph itself.

SMALL CORRECTIONS FOUND; NONE REVERSES THE CENTRAL RESULT

The paper's45mm row differs by approximately0.01degree from equation5 with its printed inputs. Immaterial.

In printed equation9, the term containing a*sin(beta) uses sin(alpha). Multiplying the displayed rotation matrices yields cos(alpha), agreeing with equation10 and its stated roll factor. At beta=0 this term vanishes, so the central elevation is unchanged. This is an internal printed consistency issue, not a reason to reject the reported sensitivity factor; the independently calculated factor agrees with it.

The current website cites0.24percent Dagor distortion at35degrees. For the6.5-inch specimens in Magill table2, the conventional100*D/[f*tan(angle)] conversion gives approximately0.24-0.26percent at30degrees and0.40-0.42percent at35degrees. Thus the quoted field angle appears mismatched to these rows. The corrected values still support low distortion. See independent-checks.json; the actual1917 lens is not calibrated by this table.

WEIGHT AND CONTINUITY

This audit strengthens the assessment of Dalleur's case relative to the assistant's earlier generic cautions. It supplies an argument about external illumination which cannot be explained by retinal effects if its measurement reconstruction is accepted. Its agreement with descent testimony matters in the announced-sign context. It must be carried into the cumulative assessment as physical corroboration, not erased because replication remains incomplete.

No completed alternative solar fit, disqualifying error in the core geometry, or sufficiently quantified measurement correction closing the discrepancy was established here. Outstanding verification and a demonstrated refutation are different statuses. Reading a defense and still wanting to check its numbers does not make that defense unanswered.
