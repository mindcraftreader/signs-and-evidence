THREE MATERIAL GAINS

1. Marie Lemarchand now has a detailed reproduced treating-doctor report, beyond a general claim that Zola saw a miracle. Newly acquired Grandmaison de Bruno, Twenty Cures at Lourdes Medically Discussed (1912), chapter pp20–34, reproduces La Néele's report dated1September1892. It distinguishes observed September1891 findings from earlier family history, identifies repeated examinations, and gives26August1892post-return findings: wounds dry and replaced by soft pink scar tissue/crusts, normal oral mucosa, restored grip/walking and almost normal wrist flexion. The early scar tissue matters because a later quoted Tennésson argument says no scars. These are different observations and times, not license to describe pristine scar-free skin immediately.
The same medical advocate explicitly considers much of her paralysis/delirium and some pulmonary symptoms functional/hysterical; he disputes lupus in favor of trophoneurosis with secondary infected ulcers. He still regards abrupt infected-ulcer closure as extraordinary. Thus a favorable source itself separates a substantial organic-wound claim from a less secure assertion that every symptom represented irreversible anatomical disease. No modern explanatory experiment or original biopsy/culture was acquired. Printedp25was visually inspected.

2. Biré has specific near-contemporary eyewitness observation. Guinier's1909article, printedp597, says he saw the case at the Bureau in1908. It records white pearly discs, right central vessels imperceptible, left vessels estimatedone-thirdnormal, while she could read newspaper print and a watch's minutes with each eye. This page was visually inspected. The source supplies a concrete clinical observation, but does not prove anatomically destroyed optic nerves regenerated. Actual pre-recovery visual-acuity records, fundus photographs and the full oculist report remain absent. Modern Resch2008 names ophthalmologistLainey ofRouen and mentions later unnamed specialist checks; those have not been independently acquired. Her local parish reproduces the30July1910recognition quotation.

3. Seisson has a traceable1882certificate/register chain. Newly acquired Boissarie1891p347summarizes DrPigeon's18September1882report and explicitly points to Annalesvolume15p142. DominiqueJavel's1995ProvenceHistoriquearticle identifies a23August1882pre-journeycertificate,18Septemberpost-recoverycertificate,30AugustBureaurecordcahier4pp123–124and1896recheckno123. The source describes chronic bronchitis, gastritis and clinically diagnosed organic heart disease with hypertrophy, palpitation and edema; symptoms/function improved after bathing. No measured heart dimensions or modern cardiac tests establish a literal new heart. Original certificates not acquired. Recognition-date2versus12July1912 discrepancy between official language lists/parish/scholarly account retained, not used to impeach the recovery.

EARLIEST CASES: SUBSTANTIVE PROVENANCE IMPROVEMENTS AND LIMITS

Boissarie1891pp99–108was newly read, andCazenavep105visually inspected. Cazenave's observable lid eversion, outgrowths, tearing and lashes are described separately; lashes are said to return from that day rather than all instantly. Prior thermal treatment is named. Vergez's favorable opinion is transcribed by Bertrin1909appendix. This is meaningful documentation but not the original signed1858chart or a paired photograph.

Latapie is tied to shoulder dislocation and fixed final-two-finger flexion, with immediate movement after water immersion; Vergez's judgment is reproduced. A traumatic origin does not by itself establish complete nerve destruction or exclude every functional/contracture mechanism. No original neurological measurements were recovered.

Bouriette has an important source-severity variation: Bertrin1909calls the amaurosis incomplete; Boissarie1891describes residual confused light perception yet says vision lost20years; the modern official account distinguishes injury20years earlier from complete blindness in only the final2years. Boissarie relays Dozous's reported monocular reading test and remaining scar, but an explicit footnote says the narrative is taken from Lasserre. It must not count as a new independent firsthand doctor. The actual Dozous1874volume and originalVergezreport remain targets. No optic-nerve regrowth claim established.
