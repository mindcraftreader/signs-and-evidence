LOURDES EXPANDED AUDIT: RECOVERY YEARS 1960–PRESENT
3 November 2026

Seven case dossiers cover recovery decades 1960s, 1970s, 1980s and 2000s: Luigina Traverso, Serge Perrin, Delizia Cirolli, Jean-Pierre Bely, Danila Castelli, Bernadette Moriau and Antonia Raco. These are recovery dates, not recognition dates. The live official list still contains72 cases. No recognized recovery from the1990s,2010s or2020s appears in the inherited modern register; this is not evidence that nobody reported a healing in those decades.

The substantial NEW gains are the photographed Perrin medical report, including angiogram and visual-field plates, and the full Traverso diocesan decree with four named medical commissioners and their meeting/report dates. Existing modern reports are reused rather than counted as independent new corroboration.

SERGE PERRIN, 1 MAY 1970

A modern evangelization website reproduces photographed pages of Pierre Mouren's neurological and Dominique Bartoli's ophthalmic reports to the1976CMIL. All25 available medical-page images were downloaded unmodified, with URL/hash manifest. Fourteen images were visually inspected: printed pages29,31,33,35,37 and6,14,17–20,22,24–25. The whole webpage transcription was read. The edition's title/publication page has not been recovered, so this remains a third-party reproduction of historical primary material rather than independently authenticated archival originals.

https://www.mediadedieu.net/l/miracle-de-lourdes-la-guerison-de-serge-perrin/

The plates include actual1969carotid angiograms and pre/post visual-field plots. These should not be called before/after photographs of a vanished thrombus: the report itself disputes that original angiographic interpretation. The page33 caption identifies partly sub-intimal injection; page20 says carotid thrombosis cannot be retained on this evidence because injection artifact and likely spasm interrupted circulation. A precise vascular lesion was not established.

This does not make the whole case simply imaginary. Mouren regards the recurrent hemiplegia as vascular. Bartoli argues that residual temporal optic-disc pallor supports an organic injury to the visual pathways. Page24 summarizes prior visual acuity around1/10 and1/20 and severe field restriction; page25 reports26November1973acuity10/10in both eyes, correction on the right, and normal fields. Page14 reports a27December1973normal neurological examination,9/10right/10/10left uncorrected and10/10right with correction, alongside residual disc pallor. These are published examiner observations, not new measurements made by us.

The report also contains a substantive functional qualification. On page19 Mouren concludes that emotional distress likely played a major role in repeated partial loss-of-consciousness episodes and that these contained an important functional component. Page14's account of an earlier assessment found no conversion-neurosis signs. These judgments concern different aspects and examinations; neither should be silently omitted. A functional component in the blackouts does not, by itself, demonstrate a functional explanation for every motor and ocular deficit.

Page22 discusses an earlier report of42 late complete regressions among1018cerebrovascular cases, then maintains that Perrin's recovery was exceptional. That historical figure is not a matched probability for Perrin or a demonstration of his mechanism. Bartoli's concluding opinion on page25 regards the rapid ocular recovery and six-year durability as medically inexplicable. The modern transcription reproduces a15-member unanimous1976CMIL judgment, with acknowledged uncertainty about precise vascular cause. The actual photographed committee pages41–42 are not in the downloaded medical-page set, so the exact vote claim remains a transcription here.

Named physicians now have more precise roles: Mouren, Marseille medical professor/head nervous-diseases service; Bartoli, Troyes ophthalmic service; Drevillon, ophthalmologist attached to Angers hospital; Fresneau, hospital physician and professeur agrege at CHU Angers; Pasquier, hospital neuropsychiatrist; Sourice, longstanding treating GP. The latter four appear in the photographed page14 footnote. Their report supplies credentials, not a separate independent university personnel audit.

Assessment: a major improvement in access and specificity. The same primary material strengthens the clinical recovery evidence and qualifies a simplistic confirmed-thrombus/entirely-organic narrative. It does not establish either supernatural agency or a complete ordinary mechanism.

LUIGINA TRAVERSO, 23 JULY 1965

The full11October2012bishop decree, reproduced on OFTAL Casale's site, identifies four medical commissioners: Adriano Figarolo, Franco Balzaretti, Luigi Porta and Carlo Cebrelli. It records their20June2012meeting and a unanimous medical report delivered3July2012, reaffirming unexplained lasting recovery. The spiritual and medical commissions met separately before a final plenary review. The decree refers to a detailed dossier of clinical condition, unsuccessful treatments and sudden recovery.

https://oftalcasale.blogspot.com/2012/10/il-decreto-vescovile-di-monsignor.html

An original11March2012L'Ancora newspaper issue is downloaded. Only the Traverso article on printed/PDFpage6 was substantively read and visually inspected, not all56pages. It reproduces the FMA superior general's letter, names Balzaretti's CMIL presentation and dates Bureau reviews28July1966,31July1984and28July2010. Recovery occurred during Eucharistic adoration/procession, rather than an anatomically documented instant spinal reconstruction in a bath.

https://www.lancorastorico.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/ANC20120311_09.pdf

Official ASL Novara's indexed CV identifies Franco Balzaretti as Pavia-trained in medicine and emergency surgery, with Vercelli/Borgosesia day-surgery leadership and general-surgery service. Direct PDF download timed out; the indexed first-page contents were read. Other commissioners' specialties/affiliations are not independently established. The2025official diocese anniversary account documents continued public testimony, not sixty consecutive annual clinical examinations. Its1934birth date conflicts with the VU thesis's1938; prefer the official case biography. Some2012press mistakenly calls Bishop Perrier 'Terrier'.

Assessment: named medical scrutiny and durable function are substantive. Original operative notes, neurological tests, paired imaging and the full2011CMIL report remain absent. Do not equate institutional medical conclusions with possession of all their underlying data.

OTHER FIVE DOSSIERS

Cirolli1976: preserve the serious untreated malignant-tumor regression claim and competing neuroblastoma/Ewing classifications in CMILmember St John Dowling's1984clinical analysis. Her August pilgrimage preceded worsening in September and recovery near Christmas at home, with continuing Marian prayer and Lourdes water. The full Trifaud/Colvin booklet and original slides were not newly obtained. A delayed shrine-related recovery should not become instant bath-side tumor disappearance.

Bely1987: official bishop declaration and firsthand Bureau investigator Patrick Theillier's interview identify a Poitiers neurological service and an eleven-year investigation. The2003medical-spiritual booklet and18December1988Journal de la Grotte remain precise unacquired targets. Neither generic MSremission nor paired-MRI anatomical restoration has been demonstrated from the public material.

Castelli1989: the bath connection and long review are direct in institutional accounts. The official Diocese of Pavia chronology independently confirms20June2013recognition. The endocrine diagnosis, surgery history and disappearance of recurrent hypertensive crises are medically relevant, but the original catecholamine/metanephrine values, pathology, imaging and2011CMIL report remain unacquired. Avoid upgrading syndrome recovery to proven instant disappearance of metastatic cancer.

Moriau2008: retain the seven-page2019AMILfirsthand testimony of treating GP Christophe Fumery and the Bureau president, and the26–1medical vote. Her major functional recovery occurred at home after pilgrimage, not physically inside the baths. Francesco D'Alpa's published critique contains specific residual-deficit and rehabilitation leads quoted from her memoir; those original letters were not newly authenticated. His proposed mechanism is not experimentally demonstrated in this patient. Major functional improvement and perfect anatomical restoration are different claims.

Raco2009: retain quantitative FVC, strength and swallowing observations and the explicit diagnostic dispute. The2019vote was12–13; the2024vote17–4followed revised clinical PLScriteria. Normal MEP/MRI alone does not disprove PLS under the updated consensus. The supplied22-page report contains selected clinical excerpts, not the whole raw dossier. This case is a benchmark retained from the earlier audit, not a fresh independent examination.
