GIOVANNI SAVINO — EXTERNAL MEDICAL-RECORD SEARCH
6 November 2026

QUESTION
Locate publicly inspectable before/after clinical documentation of the 1949 eye injury and reported recovery, beyond the existing local archive.

RESULT
No paired original clinical records were located in this search. There is a concrete first-person bibliographic assertion that the hospital records were obtained by C. Bernard Ruffin, rather than only an anonymous assertion that documents exist. A reproduced passage from his third edition explicitly says the records did not record the condition at discharge. This is a material limit on that particular documentary source, not proof that no later eye examination was ever written.

NEW DIRECTLY INSPECTED EVIDENCE
Ethan Muse's current article, footnote27, reproduces an image of Ruffin's third-edition text. The image was downloaded and visually read in this pass; it is a biographical excerpt, not a scan of Savino's hospital chart. The excerpt says Ruffin obtained records from Foggia's Ospedali Riuniti in1989. It summarizes admission findings, using the contested spelling emoptalmo, and explicitly notes the absence of a discharge-condition entry. It then discusses interviews with the widow and son. Those interviews are testimony, not signed ophthalmological measurements.

INTERPRETATION
The external search improves the provenance of the claimed BEFORE record. It does not produce an inspectable clinical AFTER report. Neither eyewitness descriptions of destruction nor later photographs establish the full initial anatomy or a measured recovery by themselves. The precise medical term must be verified from the underlying document before deriving anatomical conclusions from it. No ordinary mechanism or fraud was established here. No debate tally changed.

NEXT DOCUMENT TARGETS
