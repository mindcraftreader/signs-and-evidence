ITIGILOV / ETIGELOV: DIRECT PRESERVATION COMPARISON
1 November 2026. Initial targeted follow-up, not completed medical audit.

Why reopened: Bernadette's bodily preservation is now Daniel's fourth pillar. Itigilov is a more directly relevant decades-long preservation comparator than the 37-day tukdam report. No change to earlier dispositions of Hindu milk / Thai solar claims, and no automatic debate point.

Evidence recovered:
1. Steven Lee Myers, New York Times, 1 October 2002, full contemporary article reproduced by University of Pennsylvania Russian and East European Studies. Death 1927; 2002 exhumation 75 years later; reported earlier opening and salt. Journalist records a dozen witnesses including two forensic experts and photographer. This university hosting is a newspaper republication, not university forensic research. URL https://rees.sas.upenn.edu/about/spotlight/lama-itigilov-ivolgin-datsan-buryatiya-russia
2. Stephen Boykewich, Moscow Times, 22 September 2005. Reporter visited displayed remains; describes slumped body with sunken eyes/nose. Reports direct telephone interview with Professor Viktor Zvyagin of Federal Center of Forensic Medicine, who had examined body November 2004 and analyzed hair/skin/nails, finding unusual preservation. Quoted researcher calls it different from mummification, incomprehensible to him, requiring research. The paper reports a comparison with a body dead 36 hours, but this must not be elevated into a complete organ autopsy. URL https://www.themoscowtimes.com/archive/pilgrims-flock-to-see-their-lama
3. First-party account by Yanzhima Vasilyeva, Itigilov Institute director, supplied to Buddhism in Russia: history of openings in 1955/1973/2002, tissue examinations, salt, public display. Has very strong assertions of metabolic activity and fourth state of death; none verified here. Claim that salt should destroy body is not accepted as established chemistry. Salt presence is acknowledged, timing varies between secondary accounts, efficacy not demonstrated. URL https://www.buddhism.ru/en/buddhism/history-of-buddhism-in-russia/vozvrashchenie-itigelova/
4. Justine Buck Quijada, Wesleyan University Department of Religion, American Ethnologist 39(1), 2012, pp138-154, DOI10.1111/j.1548-1425.2011.01354.x. Abstract and publisher bibliography accessed, NOT full body. Ethnographic study of claims of scientifically proven miracle, not itself a forensic certification. https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1548-1425.2011.01354.x

What remains: original signed 2002 examination report; original 2004 analytical results and comparison protocol; documented coffin contents and treatment timeline; historical identity/custody; precise external vs internal organ scope; full Quijada article and references; biological meaning of spectroscopy (normal organic composition is not proof of viable tissue).

Current conclusion: serious non-Christian preservation candidate deserving same evidentiary rules as Bernadette; neither medically established supernatural nor naturally explained by the present pass. Does not supply an event comparable to Fatima's prediction/public sign or Zeitoun's repeated public luminous appearances. It addresses the newly added preservation pillar specifically.
