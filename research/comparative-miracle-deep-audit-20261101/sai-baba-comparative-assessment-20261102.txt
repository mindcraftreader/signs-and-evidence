SATHYA SAI BABA — COMPARATIVE ASSESSMENT, 2 NOVEMBER 2026

Current judgment: ordinary conjuring/fraud is better supported than supernatural materialization for the performances covered by the magicians' analysis. This is an assessment of the recovered evidence, not an assertion that every alleged healing, bilocation or other incident has been individually explained. Baba remains a comparison in claim type to Padre Pio, but has not been established as a comparably strong supernatural case or a successful counterexample to Daniel's argument. This finding does not independently establish Pio's supernatural agency or Catholic exclusivity. No numeric debate tally changed.

MAGICIANS' ANALYSIS
Dale Beyerstein and magicians Leon Mandrake, Lou Crockett, Lon Mandrake and Velvet Mandrake analyzed materialization sequences in Richard Bock's devotee-distributed film Christ in Kashmir: The Hidden Years. Four authors describe substantial professional performance experience. The recovered item is an authored analysis reproduced in a critical compilation, not a peer-reviewed clinical or laboratory experiment.

Their findings, attributed to them rather than represented as an independent frame-by-frame audit here:
- Around 44 and 45 minutes of that film, they identified thumb-palming, transfers to a finger palm, and unveiling bundled necklaces; one sequence required assistance from the other hand to unfold the chain.
- Some other sequences showed opportunities compatible with concealment rather than an unambiguous visible transfer. They explicitly noted missing camera angles and edits.
- Ash from an inverted urn could be supplied by preloaded compacted ash scraped into powder. They offered a reproducible miniature demonstration. The proposed mechanism was consistent with observed flow stopping when the hand was removed; the actual urn interior was not inspected by these authors.
- A window-ledge sequence provided an opportunity to load an object, rather than clearly depicting pickup.
- They concluded extensive sleight of hand beyond reasonable doubt. That is their conclusion, not a court finding. Their evidentiary strength varies by sequence.

Separate 1992 video: Haraldsson and Wiseman's 1995 paper concerns another recording. It found an occluded interval in which a handoff could occur but no unequivocal visible transfer. That limited result remains intact; it does not refute the magicians' analysis of other footage or authenticate other claims. Original videos not independently reviewed in this pass.

CHARACTER EVIDENCE
Multiple named firsthand allegations of sexual misconduct exist, including Alaya Rahm, Al Rahm and Mark Roche in the BBC documentary. Baba denied wrongdoing. Goldstein's faith-based response is not independent exoneration. See sai-baba-abuse-allegations-20261102.txt and the locally saved original BBC transcript. Do not count family repetition as additional independent eyewitnesses or call criminal guilt judicially established.

DEFENSE AND LIMITS
University of Iceland psychologist Erlendur Haraldsson and Karlis Osis described apparent productions they could not explain under informal conditions, but their research expressly withheld firm conclusions absent controls. Baba declined their proposed controlled experiments. Favorable observations are part of the record; they do not independently exclude sleight of hand. General charitable achievements and prominent devotees do not answer the specific abuse or concealment evidence.
