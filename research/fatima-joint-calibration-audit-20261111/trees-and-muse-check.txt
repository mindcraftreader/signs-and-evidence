D115 BACKGROUND TREES AND MUSE: DIRECT CHECK
11 November 2026

RESULT
The background trees do not yield a usable independent calibration from the
reproductions inspected here. The visible features are chiefly crowns and
vegetation silhouettes; trunks/bases are obscured by the crowd. I did not
recover independently established heights, distances or identified homologous
points across the other photos. A treetop boundary cannot be substituted for
the geometric horizon. This is a data limit, not evidence for a wide lens.

A more important defense comes from Muse's other scene constraints. His
Bradley-response captions41–45minutes argue a31–46mm-equivalent interval
from heads near the image edges and other series images; threads reportedly
narrow it to33–39mm, with37mm best; period practice favors about44mm.
At50–55minutes he invokes straight staffs, small lens distortion, distributed
shadow convergence and the newspaper's probable equipment. These are
relevant substantive defenses. The new18.1mm-equivalent counter-calibration
has not been tested against the head-perspective constraints or independently
verified inventory. Muse's numeric bounds are his stated estimates; this
pass did not reproduce their complete measurement derivation.

MY PREVIOUS CLAIM: EXACT SCOPE
Holding the baseline's implied vertical vanishing point fixed does preserve
the projected family of truly parallel vertical lines, including any plumb
threads/trunks. That mathematical result survives. It does not preserve known
sizes, distances, camera station or head anatomy. It does not establish that
an18mm lens plus shift is consistent with all those additional constraints.
The prior calculation also moves the estimated horizon by about82pixels.
Therefore my reply to the threads was mathematically narrow, and must not be
read as an answer to all of Muse's independent scene arguments.

ACTUAL INSPECTION
Viewed the unannotated1669x1181D115image and the six-image shrine contact
sheet. Relevant pixel regions are recorded in trees-and-muse-check.json.
The prominent crown above/behind the central crowd is visible; its support
and base are hidden. Other images show vegetation but I did not independently
identify and measure a stable same-tree correspondence. Most public shrine
files are about600pixels wide. No generated detail or image enhancement used.
Original-scans multi-view reconstruction may supply further constraints;
this pass is not such a reconstruction and does not declare it impossible.

ADDITIONAL NUMERICAL CHECK
At illustrative manually identified image positions, ray obliquity differs:
left boy's head about25degrees baseline versus33degrees alternative;
right man's head18versus38; right woman's headscarf21versus42.
These are angles computed from competing intrinsics at selected pixels,
not estimated gaze, head pose, distortion measurements or a formal rejection.
They make the missing edge-perspective check consequential: the alternative
changes rays across the image even though its vertical VP is held fixed.
No observed face anatomy has been declared impossible from these numbers.

JUDGMENT
Trees alone neither establish nor refute the new camera configuration.
Muse's edge-perspective/historical-equipment defense is unanswered by my
paired-VP calculation. Consequently the latter remains an incomplete
mathematical counter-calibration, not a demonstrated plausible historical
alternative or a sufficient basis for reasonable doubt in the combined case.
The correct next test would jointly fit those extra constraints, rather
than repeating 'unknown camera' or declaring all scene details preserved.
No new tally, no reversal of the prior16–0record.
