D115: JOINT CALIBRATION AND THE REASONABLE-DOUBT CLAIM
11 November 2026

QUESTION
Can a concrete uncertainty in the photographic inference be defended, rather
than merely invoking an unspecified possible error?

RESULT
There is a demonstrable calibration ambiguity when lens/principal point and
attitude vary together: the published light-ray convergence and the baseline's
implied vertical-line convergence can be preserved while obtaining the solar
elevation. This narrows an earlier fringe argument: those projected vertical
directions alone cannot exclude every shifted wide-angle calibration.

However, no historical camera configuration or full-scene ordinary fit was
established. The most compact-shift solution uses a specialist ultra-wide
lens class, and the alternative changes the estimated ground horizon. I cannot
defend its probability or historical likelihood from the present sources.
Therefore this test does NOT establish that an ordinary source is probable,
that Dalleur's reconstruction is false, or that the cumulative Fatima case
falls short of reasonable doubt. Missing independent verification must not
be substituted for such an adverse finding.

EXACT CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
Dalleur's published inference places a dominant source about25–32degrees
above the horizon near a time when the Sun was about42–42.72degrees high.
If reliable, this is physical corroboration of a reported lower apparent
source. It does not itself photograph a trajectory or identify an agent.
The user combines it with advance prediction, press and regional testimony;
this test does not discard those other strands.

BASELINE AND NEW TEST
Unaltered published shadow VP on the1669x1181image:
(-287.76341666666667, 1478.9628611111111)
Adopted camera center: (834.5, 555.125); ground horizon: 411.405556px.
Baseline37mm equivalent gives28.238606709degrees.
The implied gravity-vertical VP is(834.5, 21028.78987455197). It is a consequence of the
baseline assumptions, NOT an independently measured plumb-line intersection.

For each alternative intrinsic calibration K, backproject both image VPs.
The source elevation is arcsin of the absolute normalized dot product of
the two resulting3Ddirections. Reprojecting returns both VPs exactly.
Changing K therefore changes the physical angle without changing either
projected line family. Equal x/y principal shifts are chosen for a simple
one-dimensional sensitivity path. They are not inferred from the photo.

about13.63mm in each axis. This is not a claim about sign of lens-board motion.
The ground horizon at the old image-center x shifts about80pixels upward.
Its small roll/pitch do not require9–11degree visibly slanting threads.

The26.67mm-equivalent Dagor example requires about23.52mm per axis and a
much larger horizon change. Normal30–37mm examples need still larger shifts.
These assumed shifts are NOT verified mechanical travel ranges.

STRONGEST DEFENSE OF DALLEUR
The catalogue itself calls the Hypergon a specialist lens, explicitly not a
general-purpose photographic lens. Its mount, illumination-correcting star
diaphragm, focal distance and the likely fixed-strut camera's focus position
create actual physical compatibility questions. A period-available lens plus
a shift mechanism is not proof of an assembled camera fitting this exposure.
Dalleur also estimates the horizon from tripod height and the slope; the new
calibration changes that estimate. I have not reconstructed the camera station,
terrain or known3Dhuman dimensions to test those constraints together.
There is no exact exposure inventory tying a different lens to Ruah.
The convergent shadow features and ordinary-camera historical argument remain
positive evidence. A target-fitting numerical configuration does not erase them.

PRIMARY SOURCE CHECKS
Dalleur's current author page explicitly estimates plate format/crop,
center of view, tripod height, downslope position, horizon and roll. The
25–32degree interval varies selected camera assumptions; it is not a fully
calibrated all-errors confidence interval. His worked controls are retained;
their success checks calculation on those scenes, not all D115 input choices.

The Goerz manufacturer catalogue c1906 printed21/PDF12 establishes front-board
translation in both axes but supplies no verified travel for Ruah's actual
camera. The1910catalogue printed28–29 (visually re-inspected this turn)
lists the60.325mmHypergon,135degree coverage, mounting dimensions and the
specialist-use qualification. It describes geometric correction; 'wide angle'
is not itself proof of fisheye distortion. Manufacturer specifications are
not independent bench calibration or evidence of use at Fatima.

CONCESSIONS RETAINED
No ordinary-solar angle from modest focal length, crop or small distortion
changes alone. No successful alternative exposure-time argument; the
contemporary phenomenon caption remains contrary to arbitrary redating.
No new refutation of the light-ray VP or actual shadow correspondence.
No claim that Dalleur ignores uncertainty. No dismissal based on credentials,
religion or absence of an instrumented1917experiment.

JUDGMENT AND SCOPE
What is sustained: a specific conditionality of the photographic angle;
one cannot equate repeated evaluation of adopted inputs with an independently
calibrated, uniquely established physical angle. The new paired-VP test adds
a concrete reason why vertical appearance alone cannot settle all calibrations.
What is not sustained: an historically demonstrated configuration, an ordinary
whole-scene model, or a defended probability that the required alternative is
large enough to constitute reasonable doubt in the cumulative argument.
I therefore cannot use this calculation alone to defend the categorical
negative verdict challenged by the user. No tally change or reopening is made.
This remains an exploratory research result, available for further testing.

NEXT DISCRIMINATOR
Recover mechanical bounds and focus/mount geometry for an actual historically
credible camera/lens combination; jointly test the estimated horizon against
site/height information and image perspective. Trace original shadow selections
to the6Kscan for independent measurements. Those tests could favor either side.
An original scan is helpful but its absence does not itself make a doubt reasonable.
