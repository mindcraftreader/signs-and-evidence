SHROUD: HOW THE JENGA BLOCKS INTERSECT WITH THE TALLY
4 November 2026

PURPOSE
Preserve established concessions, connect particular challenges to the claims
they test, and identify which remaining investigations could materially change
the cumulative comparison. This is a dependency map, not a new numerical tally.

THREE LEVELS
Jenga block: an observation, evidentiary claim or explanatory constraint.
Tally entry: the outcome of a particular challenge to one or more blocks.
Cumulative judgment: what the surviving evidence favors, accounting for
overlap, contrary evidence and the conclusion's actual scope.

1. CHRISTIAN INTERVENTION AS BACKGROUND
Blocks14 and23; later modern-miracle additions.
The recorded Marian assessment raises the credibility of a Christian relic
explanation. Completed modern-case objections retain their recorded results.
This is relevant background for the Shroud, not a newly measured Shroud date
and not an automatic additional point in the Shroud tally.

2. REPRESENTATIVENESS OF THE DATED STRIP
Blocks01–04,19–20.
Conceded: three labs are not three separated cloth regions; actual repairs
and historical repair skill exist; an interior collection is less exposed
to a specifically corner-local repair if its fibers originated there.
Open: whether enough of the exact dated strip is later replacement.
Connected challenge: S-REPAIR-01. Original weave, if established, would not
by itself dispose of the separate irradiation hypothesis.
Effect: the whole-cloth inference cannot be defended merely by counting labs;
the measured dates still retain evidential weight for the sampled material.

3. ALTERNATIVE DATES AND THEIR RELIABILITY
Blocks10–13.
Conceded: several methods yield ancient-compatible estimates, and a medieval
account must address their convergence.
Retained counterevidence: the local mechanical holdout reanalysis predicts
an approximately1330-year old-direction error for another reference. It is
our calculation from published data, not an independent laboratory experiment.
Unestablished: reliable joint extreme-tail odds from narrow model uncertainties.
Effect: weakens the astronomical-probability claim; does not explain away all
alternative dates or demonstrate one common bias.

4. THE IMAGE AND THE MANUFACTURING ACCOUNT
Blocks05–09,18,22 and24.
Retained: no reviewed historical counterpart or demonstrated complete process
matches the whole relevant combination. Optical, chemical and forensic
constraints must be assessed together.
Recent challenge outcome: medieval paintings demonstrate visual modeling
but do not establish the Shroud's claimed geometric fidelity. They did not
defeat that spatial-information block.
Recent correction: the assistant suggested public display would favor a
strong image. Daniel supplied a plausible purpose for faintness: a maker
aiming to imitate an actual burial trace. Assistant withdrew the proposed
motivational objection. Faintness remains a physical constraint, not a strong
standalone reason against manufacture.
Recent partial ordinary mechanism: negative-like tonality can arise from
relief rubbing without knowledge of photography. This addresses the claim
that negativity necessarily requires a separate remarkable accident; it
does not demonstrate the entire original image process.
Effect: historical skill and partial experiments reduce certain claims of
individual-feature impossibility, while the complete manufacturing problem
remains unresolved in our investigation.

5. HISTORY, ATTRIBUTION AND PROVENANCE
Blocks16–18 and21.
Retained: earlier cloth-image descriptions are relevant antecedents, and no
maker, commission or complete production chain has been recovered here.
Retained counterweights: no continuous identification/custody chain before
Lirey; medieval accusations exist; pollen/DNA do not uniquely date Palestinian
origin. Missing attribution is not a demonstrated impossibility of manufacture.
Effect: both historical accounts remain incomplete; source-specific assessment
is preferable to treating one gap as eliminating the other side's evidence.

RECENT DIALOGUE SHOULD NOT BE ERASED BY AN OLD TOTAL
The modern-miracle tally is a record of specific scored challenges, not a
running score for every Shroud exchange. This document records actual changes
in the new discussion without inventing user-approved numerical points.
No existing scored outcome is silently reopened or removed.

HIGHEST-VALUE NEXT DESK INVESTIGATIONS
A. Spatial geometry: define and inspect published quantitative body-distance,
   proportional and cloth-drape claims; distinguish fitted reconstructions
   from independent tests. A painting that looks lifelike is insufficient.
B. WAXS prediction: inspect how independent known-age reference textiles test
   the calendar-age conversion and its environmental assumptions. Existing
   favorable checks are positive evidence but not a population error curve.

These can progress through existing literature. Physical resolution of the
strip's age and isotope history ultimately needs better specimen evidence.
Do not repeat a scored challenge merely because the cumulative conclusion
still feels uncomfortable to one participant.
