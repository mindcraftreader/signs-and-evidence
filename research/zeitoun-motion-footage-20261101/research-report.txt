COMPLETED CHURCH-VIDEO AUDIT
All eight official church-linked MP4 files were downloaded and inspected, totaling 51 minutes 50.346 seconds. The pass inspected 627 frames at five-second intervals across the full timelines, 236 additional shot-change candidates, and selected one-second detail sequences. This was systematic visual sampling, not uninterrupted normal-speed playback of all eight files. No authenticated original motion footage of the Marian figure was recovered.

The files include interviews, historical crowd/church footage, still photographs, illustrations, animated overlays and reenactments. In ancient_pro_stmaryztn.mp4 around 02:04–02:07 an illustrated figure appears over a fixed church image; that is not authenticated 1968 motion footage. The later fox_stmaryztn.mp4 uses still photographs and interviews; its filename does not establish any connection with the proposed 1968 Fox production.

Local automatic transcripts are unverified finding aids. English contains errors; Arabic is especially unreliable, with radio_1_ar failing quality review. Empty transcription output is not evidence of absent speech. See church-film-audit.json for each file's duration, hash, URL, inspected-frame counts and assessment.

NEW CONTEMPORARY TELEVISION NOTICE
Al-Ahram, 9 May 1968, printed page 10, announces Dear Viewer / عزيزي المشاهد for Channel 5 that evening at 21:30. The notice names Mufid Fawzi as preparer and Amani Nashid as presenter, and includes bishops, a healing-committee head and twelve citizens who had seen the apparition. Original page and enlarged notice visually checked. This is a contemporary broadcast announcement, not a claim that original motion footage of the figure would be shown. Together with Watani's subsequent description, it gives a specific programme to trace. The complete issue and provenance are saved locally.

ONE NIGHT RECONSTRUCTED
april-30-reconstruction.txt assembles the reported night of 29–30 April 1968 from Bishop Athanasius, Air Force Major Asaad Iskandar and Louis Marcos, described as head of the English Department at Ain Shams University. Their reported observations overlap around 02:45–05:00. A later booklet transcription gives a disappearance and return; the duration variant is preserved. The New York Times reports Athanasius's account, not an additional newspaper eyewitness. No identified film crew is placed filming the figure during that night's window by the records recovered here.
