The calendar year is the year of the reported physical recovery. Reports
with a name withheld are eligible if the event can be distinguished and
dated. The dates of declaration, examination, publication, and Church
recognition are separate. The public search found no qualifying report.
The number of declarations made to the Bureau in 1998 and the number of
reported recoveries that occurred in 1998 remain unknown.

PUBLIC SOURCE COVERAGE

Screened the Sanctuary's Lourdes Magazine nos. 68–77 (all ten 1998
issues) and nos. 78–87 (all ten 1999 issues) for medical reports, case
follow-up, pilgrim testimony, and letters. Used text extracted from the
scans, checked case dates in context, and visually checked Walko's
1997 and 1998 chronology on the scan. No. 70 announces Patrick
Theillier's appointment; no. 77 contains his invitation to share healing
testimonies. The magazine reports cases selectively. Its approximate
6,500 declarations in no. 81, p. 37, are cumulative.

RECOVERIES FROM OTHER YEARS WITH 1998 BUREAU ACTIVITY

• Jean-Pierre Bély — reported recovery 9 October 1987; Bureau
  declaration in 1988; International Medical Committee reviewed the
  dossier in November 1998; Bishop Claude Dagens issued his decree on
  9 February 1999. The 1998 decision is a review date. Bély is already
  in the main forensic tally under 1987.
  Lourdes Magazine no. 76, November 1998, PDF pp. 14–18.
  https://lourdes.centredoc.fr/doc_num.php?explnum_id=5521#page=14
  https://eglise.catholique.fr/espace-presse/communiques-de-presse/369147-miracle-a-lourdes/

• Dorothy Walko — on 7 May 1997, reportedly stood and walked near the
  grotto after a bath; a witness helped interpret at the Bureau the
  same day and wrote on 7 June. On 20 May 1998 Theillier met with the
  doctors who had examined her; they recalled unexpected flexibility.
  He requested the treating physician's clinical records and reported
  receiving no reply. Walko's daughter wrote that her mother could still
  walk before her death on 5 February 1998. This is a dated 1997 report
  with a witness and a same-day post-event exam described in retrospect.
  Lourdes Magazine no. 81, May 1999, pp. 35–37, PDF pp. 21–22.
  https://lourdes.centredoc.fr/doc_num.php?explnum_id=5839#page=21

• Roxane D. — reported return of hearing in Canada in 1993 while her
  grandparents were on pilgrimage. Theillier dates the Bureau report
  to 4 October 1993 and reproduces a letter he received from her mother
  in August 1998. He later stated that the case never received an
  in-depth investigation. The mother described a repeat hearing exam;
  the public accounts do not reproduce paired audiology records.
  Lourdes Magazine no. 81, May 1999, p. 35, PDF p. 21.
  https://lourdes.centredoc.fr/doc_num.php?explnum_id=5839#page=21
  https://dokumen.pub/et-si-on-parlait-des-miracles-2001-2856168280.html

• Célestine Sabbe — reported recovery on 22 June 1958; the Bureau
  account describes an exam the next day and later follow-up. Theillier
  revisited the file after Dr Robert Côte approached him in June 1998.
  This also supplies the date missing in the 1960 audit's discussion
  of Sabbe's 1961 committee review. The committee found no demonstrated
  organic lesion.
  Lourdes Magazine no. 78, January 1999, pp. 36–37, PDF pp. 22–23.
  https://lourdes.centredoc.fr/doc_num.php?explnum_id=5525#page=22
  https://lourdes.centredoc.fr/doc_num.php?explnum_id=15543#page=1

• Louise Creton — wrote in 1933 that she recovered the ability to walk
  at home after a fall, while her parents prayed at Lourdes. Her daughter
  contacted the Bureau in 1998. The Sanctuary archive accepted the
  family's ex-voto in 2000. Notermans and Jansen describe the original
  letters and the deposit record in Material Religion 7, pp. 184–186.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/266949123_Ex-votos_in_lourdes_contested_materiality_of_miraculous_healings
