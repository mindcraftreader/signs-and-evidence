LOURDES MEDICAL EVIDENCE TALLY
7 November 2026

Selected Lourdes-linked cases graded for the strength and timing of clinical evidence. The selection includes all 25 patients in the published 1947–76 medical cohort and 42 reports matched to published accounts in Bertrin’s 1891 section. One of those 1891 reports is anonymous. The 1891 audit keeps 23 index-only entries in its own denominator and identifies a separate December 1891 case filed by Bertrin under 1892. The 1960 public source audit adds an unnamed Irish pilgrimage claim and updates the existing Gaultier entry.

Reviewed cases by grade

Solid before/after, fast recovery  10 total, 6 on the official baseline
Solid before/after, slow or staged recovery  4 total, 4 on the official baseline
Mid tier  64 total, 28 on the official baseline
Claim with limited accessible medical evidence  17 total, 2 on the official baseline

Selected cases  95 total. 40 on the official baseline and 55 additional Lourdes-linked reports.
Official baseline awaiting case grades  32 of 72.

Grades describe the documented clinical endpoint and its timing. The evidence for diagnosis, cause and ecclesiastical recognition is tracked separately.
The date is the recovery year. Each person is counted once where identifiable. Shrine visits, home use of Lourdes water, and devotion elsewhere retain their distinct associations.

SOLID BEFORE/AFTER, FAST RECOVERY

Specific dated clinical observations or tests on both sides, with a reasonably close window for a major change. The grade applies to the stated clinical endpoint. A window of days or roughly two weeks can establish a rapid clinical change without establishing the hour or biological mechanism.
