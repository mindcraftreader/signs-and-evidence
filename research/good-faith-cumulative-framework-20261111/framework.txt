C-03 — SINCERE, MULTIPLE-CAUSE FRAMEWORK
11 November 2026
Proposed cumulative challenge. No adjudicated tally point.

Question
Can different ordinary processes, operating through sincere participants and shared religious interpretation, adequately account for the combined Fatima, Zeitoun, Lourdes and Padre Pio evidence without an organized fraud?

Current finding
A coherent framework can be specified, and several of its components have empirical support. This pass does not establish an adequate ordinary account of the complete retained evidence, nor establish that this framework is more probable than Marian/Christian intervention. In particular, identifying processes that sometimes produce unusual experiences does not establish those processes as the causes of these events. The proposal is registered for discussion as C-03, not as an assistant win, a user concession or an automatic eighteenth defense.

The proposed account
1. Sincere personal experiences supply the seers' conviction.
2. Shared religious concepts shape the identity assigned to experiences and the actions people take.
3. Announcements, gatherings and pilgrimage organize observation at particular places and times.
4. Different physical, perceptual and clinical processes produce the reported phenomena.
5. Publicity, medical investigation and preservation determine which cases and details enter the surviving record.

Items 1–3 and 5 are insufficient without item 4. Religious expectation does not produce a displaced light source, a resolved moving figure, a healing or a skin lesion merely because the framework needs one. The framework must retain the advance prediction and immediate favorable journalism at Fatima, repeated named figure reports at Zeitoun, Lourdes's strongest clinical changes, and Pio's actual lesions. No grand conspiracy, character diagnosis or deliberate self-injury is assumed.

How the cumulative argument is tested
The inference being challenged is that four different cases with the same religious meaning must require either four unrelated extraordinary coincidences or organized deception if they are ordinary. A shared interpretation and observation process could connect them without either. This is a causal proposal, not a demonstrated estimate of its likelihood.

Common interpretation can explain common religious vocabulary. It cannot, by itself, explain the exceptional physical content of the vocabulary. The same background also belongs in the affirmative model: intentional Marian action would give a reason for recognizably Marian content, and a predicted sign supplies positive evidence of purpose. Neither model receives a probability bonus merely because it can be described in one sentence.

FATIMA: PERSONAL VISIONS, PUBLIC EXPECTATION, PERCEPTUAL EFFECTS

Candidate mechanism
The children sincerely interpret their experiences as an encounter with Mary. Their announcement brings observers to the Cova on October 13 and directs attention to an anticipated sign. Weather and solar viewing supply an unusual stimulus; adaptation and communication shape some reported colors and motion. The date is therefore partly generated by the announcement rather than a completely independent astronomical lottery.

Evidence for components
The critical edition preserves pre-event testimony; this is evidence for the prediction and for organized expectation, not evidence that the prediction was fabricated afterward. Local primary-source locator: DCF selected documents 3, 10 and 11.
https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/fontes/F001_DCF_selecao.pdf

Qasim Zaidi and colleagues at SUNY College of Optometry, the University of Chicago and the Max Planck Institute experimentally identified neural processes contributing to color afterimages at moderate light levels. This shows that some ordinary color changes do not require photopigment-bleaching intensities or injury. It does not reproduce a moonlike disc, a descending source, or environmental colors in observers avoiding solar fixation.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2011.12.021
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3562597/
Original full article already preserved under cumulative-disbelief-test-20261028/sources/zaidi-2012-pmc.html.

The generic possibility of sincere perception without the reported external object has experimental support. Albert Powers and Philip Corlett at Yale, with Christoph Mathys at SISSA and collaborating institutions, induced reports of absent tones after audiovisual conditioning, including in healthy controls. The authors' five-page report and methods/results were inspected. It is an auditory near-threshold laboratory task, not a reproduction of three children's repeated Marian encounters or an outdoor crowd event.
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan3458
https://www.tnu.ethz.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/documents/Publications/2017/2017_Powers_Mathys_Corlett.pdf

Unanswered requirements
The pale, comfortably viewed disc; the strongest movement/descent descriptions; early regional reports; environmental-color testimony; and the anomalous-light interpretation of D115 still require a joint account. Conditioning a crowd is not a mechanism for a measured displaced source. The latest camera counter-calibration has not met the additional scene constraints or supplied a demonstrated historical configuration. The children's steadfastness and sincere conviction are retained.

Verdict for this branch
Some ordinary perceptual components are supported; an adequate ordinary account of the complete event is not established. F-02, F-03, F-04, F-05, F-07 and F-08 remain closed as recorded. This framework does not reopen them by changing their names.

What would help distinguish the models
An ordinary account must reproduce the relevant combination under documented comparable illumination, or identify a concrete error sufficient to remove the independent photographic anomaly. Source-linked disagreement about crucial physical features under comparable same-episode viewing conditions could bear on perception, but neither mere variation nor failure of universal agreement would refute the user's claim.

ZEITOUN: A REAL LIGHT AND SINCERE INTERPRETATION AS A FIGURE

Candidate mechanism
A real local light stimulus, combined with shared Marian imagery, viewing conditions and crowd communication, yields sincere perception of a woman and gestures. This does not require an intentionally manufactured apparition.

Evidence for components
Cynthia Nelson of the American University in Cairo describes an outline whose visibility changed with her interpretation while also affirming a real unidentified light and disconnected streetlights. Her actual passage was reread in the original article, printed page 6. It supplies evidence of interpretive involvement in her experience. Her credentials do not make her observation override other witnesses.
https://doi.org/10.1017/S0084255900019951
Original local source: zeitoun-independence-audit-20261027/sources/nelson-1973.pdf

Unanswered requirements
The source of the light remains unidentified. The early reports of prolonged life-sized articulated figures, turning, hand movements and recurrence across separate nights exceed Nelson's limited ambiguous episode. No verified reflected-light or electrical model has fitted those observations. The existing one-pane Pepper's Ghost challenge is not restored. A corrected false alarm does not explain a different prolonged sighting.

Contemporary committee and named episcopal testimony remain affirmative evidence:
https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/report/report-of-the-committee-formed-by-his-holiness-the-pope-to-verify-the-authenticity-of-the-apparitions-of-the-virgin-mary/
https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/report/scenes-of-the-apparition/

Verdict for this branch
Interpretation contributed to at least one recorded experience; it is not established as a sufficient explanation of the sustained figure corpus. The physical source is an unfilled requirement, not a solved part of the model. Z-01 and Z-02 retain their results.

What would help distinguish the models
A date-, position- and episode-matched explanation of a strongest figure report, using actual line-of-sight and illumination evidence. A simulation of an arbitrary glow elsewhere or a generic pareidolia experiment is insufficient.

LOURDES: SINCERE VISION, CLINICAL VARIATION AND SELECTED RECOVERIES

Candidate mechanism
Bernadette sincerely experiences a religious vision. A shrine attracts ill people, concentrates attention and keeps selected recovery records. Ordinary remission, treatment, diagnostic uncertainty and changes in functional symptoms account for the clinical outcomes, with devotion supplying their shared attribution.

Evidence for components
Bernard Francois of Universite Claude Bernard Lyon 1, Esther Sternberg of the US National Institute of Mental Health, and Elizabeth Fee of the US National Library of Medicine reviewed early reports and twenty-five acknowledged 1947–76 cases. They document changing records and standards, and concrete reservations about eight of those twenty-five cases. Their article does not explain every cure or supply a disease-matched expected recovery rate. Its favorable acknowledgment that the phenomenon still needs explanation is retained.
https://doi.org/10.1093/jhmas/jrs041
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3854941/
Original full article preserved under lourdes-medical-audit-20261101/sources/francois-sternberg-fee-2012.txt.

Unanswered requirements
Bernadette's eighteen reports, Catholic message and reported direction to the spring still need an adequately supported account. The previous reflection/reinforcement proposal was withdrawn. The clinical model must address the best dated functional and structural changes, rather than borrowing errors from weaker files. Our selected ninety-five-case ledger is not a population trial; neither a shrine-visitor total nor a collection of exceptional recoveries supplies the missing disease-specific denominator. The duration and breadth of the pattern remain part of the affirmative case. Other healing traditions have not supplied the already requested evidentially matched public/vindicatory comparator.

Verdict for this branch
Diagnostic and treatment explanations are supported in particular respects; ordinary processes have not been shown to explain the full retained pattern. Calling all recoveries lucky would substitute a label for the missing clinical model. L-02 and L-04 are not reopened.

What would help distinguish the models
Case-linked diagnoses or treatment evidence that fit the strongest recoveries, and disease-, treatment- and follow-up-matched recovery data. Changing approval standards alone cannot establish that recovery incidence actually declined or resolve causation.

PADRE PIO: REAL LESIONS WITHOUT KNOWING DECEPTION

Candidate mechanism
A spontaneous bodily disorder produces lesions; care intended to treat them inadvertently maintains them, or an involuntary process contributes. The patient and attendants sincerely interpret their religious distribution as stigmata. Ordinary odor sources and perception would need a separate case-linked account for scent. The framework cannot classify actual lesions as mere visual misperception.

Evidence for components and the strongest contrary record
Amico Bignami, professor of medical pathology at Rome's Royal University, explicitly entertained a disease-plus-chemical-maintenance explanation while declining to allege pure simulation. I reread his report and Giorgio Festa's response in Di Flumeri's primary-source collection. This proves that a physician who saw Pio considered a nonconspiracy account; it does not prove his diagnosis.

The contrary evidence is substantial. Festa's October 1919 report says nothing had been applied for over three months, and his 1920 report describes persistence after withdrawal. The eight-day dressing account also reports continued lesions and bleeding. These are problems for iodine maintenance, not details to omit. Romanelli's observations and the later scarless endpoint remain in the record. Bignami's terminology must not be silently upgraded to a confirmed modern diagnosis.
Primary locators: Di Flumeri, documents 51–53, printed pp.173–213, especially pp.182 and 192–193. Local complete source: padre-pio-muse-audit-20261028/sources/di-flumeri-stigmate.pdf.
https://www.imagomundi.biz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PBSO37-Le_stigmate_di_Padre_Pio_da_Pietrelcina-Gerardo_di_Flumeri.pdf

Loretta Early of West Oakland Health Center and Joseph Lifschutz of the University of California, Berkeley reported a Baptist child's three-week stigmatization episode in 1974. Their abstract says close scrutiny made self-production unlikely and offers limited psychodynamic speculation. Full text was not acquired. It establishes neither a verified ordinary mechanism nor a fifty-year match. It is not promoted to an exclusivity counterexample or a medical explanation of Pio.
https://doi.org/10.1001/archpsyc.1974.01760080057009

Verdict for this branch
The record contains an actual nonfraud medical proposal, but it has serious unanswered objections and does not fit the retained chronology as a demonstrated diagnosis. A good-faith ordinary account of Pio's complete course has not been established. P-02, P-04 and P-05 retain their results.

What would help distinguish the models
A bodily process demonstrated to fit lesion morphology, location, withdrawal persistence and endpoint together, with case-linked evidence. A short-lived bruising disorder, isolated skin picking or unspecified unconscious action does not meet this requirement.

COMPARATIVE VERDICT AND TALLY RULE
The framework is more specific than the proposition that everyone could have made mistakes. It distinguishes mechanisms, supplies some empirical support, retains unfavorable evidence and identifies observations that could change its standing. Its main advantage is explaining how sincere people can converge on one interpretation without coordinated lying. Its main deficiency is that the physical and clinical processes doing the essential explanatory work remain incompletely connected to these cases.

Consequently, this first pass does not establish that the framework is a better explanation or a sufficient material reasonable doubt about the cumulative case. It also does not establish that conspiracy is the only possible ordinary hypothesis. These are distinct conclusions. No posterior or coincidence probability is estimated.
