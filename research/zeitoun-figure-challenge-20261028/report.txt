ZEITOUN: PROLONGED FIGURE VERSUS AMBIGUOUS-LIGHT CHALLENGE
28 October 2026

Scope: test the proposed argument that a real but ambiguous light, interpreted as a person, adequately undermines the strongest reports of a floating womanlike figure and gestures. This is not an exhaustive adjudication of every ordinary cause or every appearance.

Direct sources reread:
1. Watani5May1968, archived English transcription, with Arabic page images available from earlier audit. Athanasius reports the30April figure from2:45to5a.m., motion and outstretched hands. Louis Marcos reports personal observations Sunday3:45and Tuesday3:00a.m. His source is the same press-conference/newspaper channel, but he is a separately named claimed observer. This supports some temporal corroboration; no blind synchronised comparison of exact gestures is supplied.
2. Watani same issue, Zaki Shenouda narrative: initially regarded sights as reflections on earlier nights; reports a clear life-sized luminous figure and extended arms at3:30–5a.m.on28April. Explicit prior expectation exists, but his discrimination between earlier ambiguous sights and this fuller one must be retained.
3. Three-priest committee30April1968: separates interviews from own observations; describes full form, movement above domes, bowing at cross and blessing gesture. Joint party is one observation cluster. Exact dates of its own full-figure sighting unspecified in retrieved passage.
4.1969committee booklet, ST MARY’S APPARITIONS: Athanasius30April at2:45, disappearance after a short time, return4–5a.m. This differs from uninterrupted2:45–5wording. Both versions retain prolonged full-form observation and gestures.
5. Palmer1969interview as reproduced by archive: bishop explicitly distinguishes initial faint silhouette that he considered inadequate from later full figure with body/clothing movement; could not resolve fine facial features. Header29–30April versus narrated6April is unresolved without original edition. This prevents silently assigning all its detail to30April.

Actual critical findings:
- Duration/date transmission has real discrepancies.
- Same observation repeated in multiple publications is not independent replication.
- Exact fine facial identification is weaker than body-form identification.
- Prior expectation/crowd communication is present in parts of the record.

Why these do not sustain this particular core objection:
None of these findings establishes that the stronger prolonged figure descriptions arose from the brief ambiguous scene documented by Nelson. Detailed early claims remain. A source-quality qualification is not a demonstrated explanation or a material contradiction of the reported womanlike form and movements. No matched optical reconstruction, source identification or direct contrary same-episode account was established in this pass. The religious location and gestures remain part of the explanandum.

Decision:
Close the simple ambiguous-light/perceptual-identification objection AS DEVELOPED against the core figure claim. One successful user defense, current Daniel4assistant0. Do not interpret this as a probability, a completed exhaustive investigation, or proof every reported gesture/date is accurate. Stronger materially evidenced objections can be separately assessed; reopening this one requires new evidence or a materially stronger argument, not repetition.
