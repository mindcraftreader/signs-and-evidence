EARLIEST SOURCE FOR THE TWO EARLY DEATHS / LUCIA REMAINING
Research date: 11 November 2026

Finding
The earliest explicit text located is Lucia's account associated with December 1927, securely attested in a dated covering letter of 11 October 1928 by her confessor, the Jesuit Jose Aparicio da Silva, to Bishop Jose Alves Correia da Silva of Leiria.
This is earlier than the 1930s-1941 memoirs. It is nevertheless later than Francisco's death on 4 April 1919 and Jacinta's on 20 February 1920.
No source explicitly recording the full two-soon / Lucia-remains distinction before either child's death was located in this bounded search.

The primary document inspected
Documentacao Critica de Fatima, Selecao de documentos (1917-1930), Fatima: Santuario de Fatima, 2013, document 96, dated 1928-10-11, printed pp.353-355; originally DCF V-1, document 370, relevant p.737.
Official PDF: https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/fontes/F001_DCF_selecao.pdf#page=355
Physical PDF pages 355-357 correspond to printed pages353-355.
The date and transmission statement are on printed p353; explicit prediction on printed p354.
Original Portuguese core wording: 'sim a Jacinta e o Francisco levo-os em breve, mas tu ficas ca mais algum tempo' (accents retained in the saved page scan).
English translation: Yes, I will take Jacinta and Francisco soon, but you will remain here some time longer.
Aparicio says he is transcribing what Lucia wrote at his request, with a copy before him, and explains that she narrated in the third person at his instruction.
The text relates her request for permission to disclose this part of the message to a reported encounter dated17 December1927. The date17December is a narrated encounter date, not automatically an autograph dateline.
The copy is therefore independent documentary evidence that this account was in circulation by11October1928, but not an independent eyewitness confirmation of its being spoken in1917.

December1927 dating and explicit historian assessment
Fr. Dr. Luciano Cristino, then director of the Shrine's Studies and Dissemination Service, stated in his28March2007 conference, published9April2007, that Lucia wrote this account at the end ofDecember1927 at her spiritual director's instruction.
He says no earlier reference to the promise that Lucia would remain some time longer was known to them.
This is the Shrine's own historical account, not an inference drawn solely from an adversarial website.
https://www.fatima.pt/pt/news/conferencia-notas-biograficas-irma-lucia
Relevant paragraphs begin 'Em fins de Dezembro de1927', 'Nao conhecemos nenhuma referencia mais antiga' and 'De facto, no interrogatorio'.
Important distinction:1927 is the historian's composition dating; the dated primary transmission personally verified here is11October1928. The originalDecember1927 autograph was not inspected.

Related earlier evidence:2November1917
DCF selected document22, printed p118, question27, PDF page120.
Official PDF locator: https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/fontes/F001_DCF_selecao.pdf#page=120
Formigao asks Lucia whether it is true he heard she had told someone she would live more than twenty years.
Lucia answers that she does not remember.
This is an authentic earlier documentary trace of a reported statement about Lucia's longevity. It neither specifies Francisco and Jacinta's early deaths nor supplies an affirmative confirmation by Lucia of the reported longevity statement.
Cristino offers the charitable possibility that she was protecting what she understood as secret. That is an interpretation, not an explicit explanation contained in the1917 answer.
