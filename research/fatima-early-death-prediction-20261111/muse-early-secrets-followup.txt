Muse early-secrets provenance follow-up — 11 November 2026

Claim examined: the secrets were privately communicated and written before their public release, with later publication preserving earlier content.

Sources read:
1. Ethan Muse, Our Lady of Fatima, https://motivesofcredibility.substack.com/p/our-lady-of-fatima — section 3.2 distinguishes a secret known to exist in 1917 from public disclosure in 1941. The more detailed 1927/named-recipient argument occurs in his interview https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yln9fuDMRQ around 33:59–38:00 (saved transcript previously read).
2. Veritatis Splendor, In Defense of the Miracle of the Sun, section 2.1, https://splendoroftruth.substack.com/p/in-defense-of-the-miracle-of-the — author expressly is NOT Muse, but consulted him and developed his arguments with permission. Contains the detailed 1927 and pre-war recipient defense.
3. Frere Michel de la Sainte Trinite, The Whole Truth About Fatima, volume I, historical critique section, https://fatima.machado-family.com/vol1/ — source behind the named-confessors account. Footnote 888 identifies Lucia's statement to Fr. Hubert Jongen through De Marchi, p.344. That underlying De Marchi page and original 1946 interview publication have not been newly inspected here.
4. Fr. Dr. Luciano Cristino, then director of the Shrine Studies and Dissemination Service, 28 March 2007 conference: https://www.fatima.pt/pt/news/conferencia-notas-biograficas-irma-lucia — explicitly dates the written June-apparition dialogue to late December 1927 and says it reappears in practically the same words in the 1941 Fourth Memoir.

Findings:
- A blanket claim that the material first appeared only in the 1941 public memoirs is inadequate. Private provenance and delayed publication must be distinguished.
- The named-recipient defense is specific and checkable: in the account of the 1946 Jongen interview, Lucia directs Dhanis to two confessors, Jose da Silva Aparicio and Jose Bernardo Goncalves, about what she communicated in 1927. Asked about other recipients BEFORE THE WAR, she names the provincial superior, bishop of Leiria and Canon Galamba. Do not compress this into five separately verified 1927 confirmations.
- Frere Michel says the two early written texts were burned at confessors' instruction. His secondary report alone does not let us compare those lost complete texts word for word with 1941.
- For the particular two-cousins-soon/Lucia-remains promise, this project has stronger concrete evidence than the secondary interview report alone: the already visually checked Aparicio letter of 11 October 1928 transmits the account, and Cristino explicitly compares late-1927 writing with 1941. This supports continuity of this passage across those dates.
- The 1927/1928 record is after Francisco's 1919 and Jacinta's 1920 deaths. It establishes an earlier record than 1941, not by itself an independently recorded pre-death prediction. Related 1920 death traditions and the 1917 longevity question remain in the previous follow-up; they must not be erased.
- Later publication does not show lying. Secrecy, obedience, named recipients and continuity are substantive considerations supporting credibility. Possibilities of later invention or faulty memory have not been established merely by pointing to publication date.

Disposition: strengthen the provenance/continuity assessment and reject the simplistic first-in-1941 framing. No new tally score or alteration of agreed F-07 scope. Main tally remains 17–0.
