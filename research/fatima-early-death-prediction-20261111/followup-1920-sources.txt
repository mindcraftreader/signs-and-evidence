WIDER SEARCH: EARLY DEATH TRADITIONS BEFORE THE1927 ACCOUNT
11November2026. Follow-up requested by Daniel.

Newly located earlier related material
Formigao's published narrative records a broad popular belief that the children and their families would soon die and that the Lady had announced it. The passage is in the1921 booklet Os episodios maravilhosos de Fatima, original pp57-60, reprinted in DCF selected collection printedpp605-607. The critical-edition cross-reference at the end identifies the prior source as DCF III-2, document528, dated5June1920 in the bibliographic apparatus.
The original1920 newspaper issue was not independently inspected. The1921 text, with its1920 cross-reference, was visually checked in the official critical edition.
This is earlier evidence that a death-prediction tradition existed; it is not the precise statement excluding Lucia from the early deaths.
The formulation includes the whole family and all the children. The narrative expressly treats its basis as uncertain. It therefore cannot be silently translated into a specific fulfilled two-out-of-three prophecy.

Jacinta's own reported expectation of death
The same early narrative says that, before entering hospital, Jacinta said the Lady had appeared again and assured her she would die, making the operation futile. It also reports a later appearance promising to take her soon, accompanied by relief of pain.
This concerns her final illness rather than theJune1917 prediction. The retrieved text was published after her death. Do not substitute its reported event date for the date of the surviving testimony.
Formigao commissioned Alberto Diniz da Fonseca to draft the death account from his notes, according to Formigao's22January1930letter as cited in the critical-edition notes to the1921booklet. The attribution should not be upgraded to Formigao personally witnessing every reported exchange.

A genuine document written before Jacinta's death
Maria da Purificacao Godinho's19February1920letter to Formigao, DCF selected doc50, printedp205, reports Jacinta worsening after surgery and doctors being surprised she had survived so long. It asks about moving her and says there is something to tell him personally.
This is genuinely prospective evidence that her death was expected, but it does not identify the confidential matter or explicitly record the1917two-children prediction. It should not be counted as such.
Original Portuguese text was read in the official collection. The Shrine's historian Luciano Cristino discusses it here:

1919 observation /1920publication
Formigao's13October1919observations, published24April1920 in A Guarda677,p2, describe Jacinta as gravely ill and ask whether the Lady had promised her happiness in heaven as atLourdes. This is a question from the narrator, not an explicit report that Mary had named the two early deaths.

Expanded search scope
Downloaded the public471-account dataset used by fatima-1917.com, containingPortuguese excerpts and source dates from182documents; programmatically searched the Portuguese text for death, early taking to heaven and longevity, then read the relevant hits.
This dataset is limited to selected witness accounts of1917events. It is not a full search of all3811DCFdocuments and excludes some later illness-only documents and rumors. Its AI labels and summaries were not treated as original testimony.
Also checked the full1921booklet reprint in the selected collection, not merely the abridged III-3catalog entry; Formigao's1920notes;1923family testimony; the1917longevity question; and Portuguese, English and targetedFrench/German source searches.
The original allegation of theJune1917two-soon/Lucia-remains distinction still first appears in the located explicit account dated by Cristino tolateDecember1927, with the dated primary transmission verified11October1928.

Current verdict
The wider search finds earlier related evidence and requires a more nuanced statement than 'no death-prediction reports before1927'. It does not yet supply a pre-death document of the full selective prediction.
No claim of lying or retrospective fabrication follows merely from this documentary chronology. No new tally point, no reopening of the character result and no change to17-0.
