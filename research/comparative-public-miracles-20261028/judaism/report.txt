JEWISH MIRACLE COMPARISON — MEDIA / SCIENTIFIC AGE
Research pass: 28 October 2026. Primary-source-led, bounded search; no presumption that a comparison must exist.

FINDING
I found modern Jewish apparition, prediction and healing claims with identifiable witnesses and primary records. I did not find a Jewish case documented at the claimed public scale and repeated visibility of Zeitoun. The best qualitative apparition comparison is Rachel appearing to a group of Gaza soldiers in 2009. The strongest long-running apparition tradition found is posthumous sightings of the Lubavitcher Rebbe. Neither currently supplies comparable documented mass visibility. This does not prove global absence, and it is not an explanation of the Christian cases.

1. RACHEL TO GAZA SOLDIERS, 2009
Closest qualitative analogue: a luminous female figure, group observation, movement and a religiously meaningful message, rather than merely a recovery or lucky escape.

The earliest named first-person report located is Avner Azulai's radio account, transcribed in Hebrew by Kikar HaShabbat on 24 May 2009. The page attributes the interview to Uri Revach on Radio Moreshet the previous evening. He says his group heard Hebrew, saw a womanlike light with no visible face or limbs, initially fired because they thought she was a terrorist, and was warned away from a booby-trapped building. He says she identified herself to him as Mother Rachel and connected the rescue to Israel's prayers. This is an actual group-apparition claim, not just a commentator describing a symbolic presence.
https://www.kikar.co.il/haredim-news/794

Bar-Ilan University political-studies researcher Elisheva Rosman's 2018 article identifies four recorded testimonies: the named radio account plus three separately filmed soldiers. The filmed versions instead describe an elderly Arab woman. Four testimonies are not automatically four independent events. The original film cited is https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DhSz6YI1W8s ; the film itself was not viewed in this pass. Rosman's article and extracted text are saved locally. Her discipline contextualizes testimony and transmission; her position does not certify or refute a supernatural cause.
https://doi.org/10.3390/rel9100311
https://cris.biu.ac.il/en/persons/elisheva-rosman-stollman/

What prevents parity: the group size is not established; named co-witness statements are not independently recovered; no recording of the apparition itself or corresponding engineering report located; the named interview appeared months after January rumors. Ynet's 21 January2009 report records rabbinical endorsement and disagreement, not a firsthand journalist sighting or army confirmation.
https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-3659308,00.html

2. REBBE SIGHTINGS AFTER 1994
This is a sustained modern apparition tradition with some named accounts and occasional purported shared sightings. The supporter introduction on Chabad.fm explicitly says appearances generally happen to individuals. It claims fully awake encounters, conversation, diverse Jewish backgrounds and occasional physical effects. A Ramat Aviv forum contains comments dated2007 reporting a sighting by at least three students and another by a wife and children; these are partly anonymous or secondhand digital posts, not independently corroborated group depositions. The latest lead entry is dated2015, so the page should not be given a single2007 publication date.
https://www.chabad.fm/604/7448.html
https://www.chabad.fm/141/12924.html

The 'Seeing the Rebbe' collection includes named Chava Cohen's account of a1996 sighting followed five minutes later by a second woman's reported sighting in the same kitchen location; a2004-photo claim displays a figure from behind. Delayed publication, limited co-witness documentation and untested photographic identification prevent treating this as Zeitoun-level public evidence. It is unfair to dismiss all reports as mere symbolism, but equally unfair to count all worshippers at770 as people who optically saw an apparition.
https://seeingtherebbe.wordpress.com/

UCL Mental Health Science researcher Simon Dein's2010 article identifies30 interviews and four field visits. Hebrew University anthropology/psychology professor Yoram Bilu studied this tradition extensively. Bilu himself says his book relies more on texts, probably edited, than on interviews and observations. Their work documents the tradition and social practices; it does not establish the paranormal cause. These are stronger pointers to a real research corpus than unsourced lists of miracle stories.
https://doi.org/10.1177/0037768610383372
https://campanthropology.org/2021/04/19/yoram-bilu-on-his-book-with-us-more-than-ever/
https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/persons/yoram-bilu/

3. STRONG INDIVIDUAL HEALING LEADS
General surgeon Leonard Lovitch and his wife were interviewed by JEM in September2011. His first-person account describes an eye-muscle injury, surgery without correction, advice from the Rebbe concerning red wine for rituals, and recovery over subsequent weeks. Treating surgeon Steven E. Feldon is named. Rochester's faculty biography verifies Feldon's USC/Doheny career and neuro-ophthalmology credentials. The supportive words ascribed to Feldon remain Lovitch's recollection; no independent Feldon statement or medical record was recovered. This is a specific, checkable healing claim, not a verified clinical miracle.
https://www.chabad.org/therebbe/article_cdo/aid/2218150/jewish/Seeing-Is-Believing.htm
https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/people/112359117-steven-e-feldon

Yisroel Swimmer's2019 first-person interview names a1983 encounter and his uncle Dr Rodney Unterslak; he reports retinitis pigmentosa diagnosed by multiple specialists disappeared after a blessing. Specialists and diagnostic records are not reproduced. He does not claim he was totally blind before recovery. This is a second strong record-level research lead.
https://anash.org/vision-for-torah-study/

4. PUBLIC PREDICTION LEAD
JEM archives an address assigned to28May1967, with a transcript assuring a successful outcome for Jews in Israel before the Six-Day War and tying help to Torah and observance. An archived public utterance is evidentially better than a later unsupported claim that someone predicted a victory. It nevertheless predicts general success, not a specified extraordinary optical event at an appointed place and time. The archive's film attribution was inspected; original film authentication was not independently performed.
https://www.chabad.org/therebbe/livingtorah/player_cdo/aid/1515871/jewish/G-d-Is-Guarding-His-Children-You-Can-Help.htm

5. SCREENED OUT OF THE STRONG COMPARISON
Contemporary July2002 coverage confirms a public damp patch at the Western Wall, with messianic interpretations and a suggested pipe leak. That is an inspectable physical phenomenon, but not a mass luminous apparition. Meron pilgrimages have enormous crowds and miracle traditions, but the contemporary report describes deliberately lit bonfires. Attendance cannot be converted into miracle co-witnesses. Baba Sali's wounded-soldier cure and inexhaustible arak stories are famous modern claims; this pass found anecdotal reporting without the identifying medical particulars recovered in the Rebbe healing leads.
https://iol.co.za/news/world/2002-07-04-whose-plumbers-fix-whose-holy-pipes/
https://www.chabad.org/news/article_cdo/aid/4020521/jewish/Amid-Threats-of-War-in-Israels-North-600000-Stream-to-Meron-on-Lag-BaOmer.htm
https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-692447

SEARCH LIMITS AND NEXT DOCUMENTS
Searched English and Hebrew phrases for modern Jewish public/mass apparitions, luminous figures, posthumous Rebbe sightings, Ramat Aviv, Rachel's Gaza appearance, Meron light claims, wall moisture, wartime protection and modern rabbinical healings/predictions. Followed scholarly citations to Hebrew first-person records, not only summaries. This is a substantial targeted pass, not an exhaustive survey of every Jewish archive. Ancient Sinai/Exodus/Carmel narratives are excluded from the comparison at the user's explicit media-age constraint.

Most useful next evidence, if pursued: the full Azulai radio recording and separate named co-witnesses; precise date/unit/building for the Gaza story; the original Ovadiya film; original Lovitch medical records or an independent Feldon account; original Swimmer ophthalmology records. No outreach was made.
