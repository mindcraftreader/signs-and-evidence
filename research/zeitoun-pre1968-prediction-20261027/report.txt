ZEITOUN'S FIFTY-YEAR PROMISE: PRE-EVENT DOCUMENTATION AUDIT
27 October 2026

RESULT
No document actually written before 2 April 1968 recording the fifty-year promise was located in this pass. This is a retrieval result, not proof that no such document survives or that the tradition was invented. The story itself is well attested in later sources. It should currently be distinguished from Fatima's prediction, for which pre-event interrogation records have been inspected.

FIRM PRIMARY FINDING: NELSON'S RECORDED TRADITION
Cynthia Nelson, an anthropologist at the American University in Cairo, reports the story on printedp9 of her1973 Worldview article, The Virgin of Zeitoun. An unnamed Coptic informant describes a wealthy landowner, Ibrahim Pasha Khalil, being told in a dream to substitute a church for a villa and receiving a promise of a return fifty years later. This version also says appearances would continue for one year. Nelson presents it as a story circulating during the early apparition period, not as a pre1968 document she inspected. The article therefore establishes an early circulating tradition, with publication securely dated1973; it does not establish advance written registration. Do not infer an exact interview date from the generic description of the early period.

OFFICIAL PARISH HISTORY
The current official St Mary Zeitoun parish history describes a1924 request for land, a pledge by Khalil Ibrahim, his death during the collection of donations, and his son Tawfik fulfilling the building expense. It dates the church to1925. It does not mention the fifty-year prediction or display an early document containing it. Its silence is not a refutation; it means this accessible official page supplies no advance-document proof. Visible page text was saved locally.
https://www.stmaryztn.org/saintmary/en/the-church/about-the-church

The parish's current apparition page reproduces named witnesses and other historical accounts but does not supply a pre1968 prediction document. It also contains a potentially useful separate lighting-test lead: an unnamed garage-management engineer allegedly told Egyptian radio he switched off a suspected light and the apparition light persisted or increased. The date and original recording are not supplied. This has been passed to the Zeitoun evidence agent and should not yet count as an independently authenticated experiment.
https://www.stmaryztn.org/saintmary/en/apparition-story

CONCRETE FOUNDING-ARCHIVE LEADS, NOT INSPECTED ORIGINALS
1. A history attributed to Watani30March2008, issue2114, reproduced by Coptic historian Ezzat Andraos, identifies a20October1924 application from Tawfik Khalil to Pope KyrillosV and a subsequent land deed involving Tawfik and Victoria Ibrahim. These are precise leads for the parish/patriarchal archive, but the original application and deed were not retrieved. The reproduced narrative does not establish that either document contains the prediction.
https://www.coptichistory.org/new_page_5410.htm

2. Parish-associated writer Maged Kamel's2025 Copts United article cites royal order60 of1925, dated21May1925, authorizing construction. Again, the original order was not retrieved, and a construction authorization by itself would not establish a prophecy. The article attributes a fifty-year dream tradition to Gawdat Gabra's later book on Egyptian churches,p160. This is a secondary bibliographic lead, not a pre-event record.
https://www.copts-united.com/Article.php?A=835904&I=6005

3. In an Al-Ahram20October2025 centenary interview, parish priest Fr Basilios Sobhy describes efforts beginning in1912, fundraising in1914, the family donation, and consecration on4October1925. It does not reproduce a pre1968 prophecy record. This account identifies people and dates worth checking against the original register; it is itself post-event.

4. Otto F.A. Meinardus's Christian Egypt, Ancient and Modern has a genuine1965 first edition,518pp, OCLC1469397. Its contents have NOT been read in this pass; it is a potentially relevant pre-event church-history source, not evidence found for the promise. The Internet Archive item marks the PDF and OCR text private/access-restricted. No restricted file was retrieved. A1977 second edition must not be substituted as pre-event evidence.

VARIANTS TO KEEP VISIBLE
Later sources alternate among Khalil Ibrahim, Ibrahim Pasha Khalil and Tawfik as the dream's recipient; villa versus hotel;1918 versus around1920 or no dream year; a promise within fifty years versus after fifty years. Some can reflect naming/retelling conventions, but they must not silently be fused into one exact verbatim prediction. Nelson's additional one-year duration is also part of her recorded version.

SEARCH SCOPE AND NEXT DECISIVE RECORD
Searched English, Arabic and French combinations of the dream, founder names,1918/1920, fifty years,1924 application,1925 royal order and pre1968 documentation. Consulted original Nelson article, current official parish pages, modern Arabic historical accounts, local original-report corpus and pre-event book catalog/permissions metadata. This was not a physical search of church archives or an exhaustive reading of pre1968 Coptic periodicals.

The decisive retrieval would be a dated pre2April1968 letter, parish register entry, dedication booklet, published article or other authenticated document actually stating the promised future appearance. A dated early document about the building alone is insufficient. For now: reported prior promise, not independently verified advance written prediction.
