# Source library

Start with these original publications, archive records, and institutional sources. The [extended catalog](catalog.json) also includes secondary material, rejected leads, and earlier references. Inclusion identifies a source trail. Read the case assessment for the source’s evidentiary status.

## Shroud of Turin

- [Damon et al. — Radiocarbon Dating of the Shroud of Turin (1989)](https://doi.org/10.1038/337611a0) — The original three-laboratory dating report, with controls, pretreatment methods and the medieval calibrated interval. All Shroud portions came from one corner strip.
- [Casabianca et al. — Radiocarbon Dating: New Evidence from Raw Data (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1111/arcm.12467) — Reanalysis of released laboratory records and statistical heterogeneity; useful alongside the original report rather than as a fresh cloth date.
- [Fanti, Malfi and Crosilla — Mechanical and Opto-chemical Dating (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1051/matecconf/20153601001) — Open synthesis of FTIR, Raman and mechanical estimates. Read with the local calibration and common-bias audit; the loose fibers are not a new authenticated cloth excision.
- [De Caro et al. — X-ray Dating of a Turin Shroud’s Linen Sample (2022)](https://doi.org/10.3390/heritage5020047) — The WAXS first-century-age argument, including its cellulose-aging model and environmental assumptions. This is an aging comparison rather than direct radiocarbon dating.
- [Schwalbe and Rogers — Physics and Chemistry of the Shroud (1982)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0003-2670(01)85263-6) — STURP-era scientific synthesis of image and material observations. A starting point for checking image-formation claims against the reported measurements.
- [Moraes — Image Formation on the Holy Shroud: A Digital 3D Approach (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1111/arcm.70030) — A digital geometry test of body-versus-relief contact imagery. The model tests a proposed mechanism; it does not identify a medieval maker or reproduce every chemical feature.
- [Andrea Nicolotti — Les tribulations d’un linceul](https://iris.unito.it/bitstream/2318/1659750/5/Nicolotti%2C%20Les%20tribulations%20d%27un%20linceul%20%28L%27histoire%29.pdf) — Historian’s public university-hosted account of the Shroud’s documentary history; useful for separating secure medieval records from proposed earlier identifications.
- [Vernon Miller Scientific Photographic Archive](https://shroudphotos.com/) — Scientific photographs for inspecting what image, bloodstain and illumination claims actually refer to. Consult the archive’s image descriptions and reuse terms.

## Fátima

- [Documentação Crítica de Fátima — Selected Documents (2013)](https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/fontes/F001_DCF_selecao.pdf#page=34) — Official critical documentary edition. The linked July interview records the advance promise; the September interview is at PDF page 60. The promise should be distinguished from later detailed solar descriptions.
- [Avelino de Almeida — O Século, 15 October 1917](https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-87) — Contemporary correspondent’s report, in a DCF transcription with page scans. Distinguishes his observations, crowd reactions and what other observers said.
- [Avelino de Almeida — Ilustração Portuguesa, 29 October 1917](https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-228) — Almeida’s illustrated follow-up, explicitly describing dancing and colors as his own experience. It is another record by the same witness, rather than a new independent observer.
- [José Maria de Almeida Garrett — Detailed Witness Account](https://fatima-1917.com/pt/d/iii-1-345) — Detailed witness narrative preserved by the critical edition. Compare its date, provenance and feature inventory with the immediate newspaper reports.
- [Philippe Dalleur — Fátima’s Miracle Photo](https://sites.google.com/pusc.it/elevation-azimuth/fatimas-miracle-photo) — Proponent’s photographic argument, with links to its geometric method and validation examples. Read beside the local camera-assumptions and shadow-correspondence audits.
- [Criminisi, Reid and Zisserman — Single View Metrology (1999)](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/1999/Criminisi99/criminisi99.pdf) — Primary computer-vision method paper on extracting geometry from one image. Supplies methodological context; it is not a study of the Fátima photograph.

## Zeitoun

- [William Farid Basili — Ministry of Tourism Report, 30 April 1968](https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/report/ministry-of-tourism-department-of-inquiries/) — Archive translation of a contemporary institutional report; Arabic scan pages were retained in the research. Its first-night narrative is not automatically the author’s firsthand observation.
- [Three-priest Investigation Report, 30 April 1968](https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/report/report-of-the-committee-formed-by-his-holiness-the-pope-to-verify-the-authenticity-of-the-apparitions-of-the-virgin-mary/) — Contemporary investigation report reproduced with original Arabic scans. The modern English translation overstates some clerical titles; the signatories were priests.
- [Coptic Patriarchal Statement, 4 May 1968](https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/glossary/statement-by-the-coptic-orthodox-patriarch/) — Formal church statement documenting the institution’s conclusion. It should be distinguished from the separate observations and investigation reports on which it relies.
- [Watani Newspaper, 5 May 1968](https://ourladyofzeitoun.com/en/newspaper/watani-newspaper-may-5-1968/) — Contemporary reporting preserved with English translation and Arabic page scans. Useful for named witnesses, medical claims and what was publicly asserted at the time.
- [Cynthia Nelson — The Virgin of Zeitoun (1973)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0084255900019951) — Anthropologist’s published account of visits and social setting. Preserves ambiguity and false alarms as well as testimony; the original journal article was inspected in the research.
- [Gregorios Encyclopedia, Volume 20 — Original Arabic Appendix](https://coptic-treasures.com/book/موسوعة-الأنبا-غريغوريوس-الجزء-20-العذر/) — Arabic compilation, legal deposit 2007, with attributed witness extracts in its appendix. The extracts are not facsimiles of individually signed contemporary witness forms.

## Padre Pio

- [Gerardo Di Flumeri — Le stigmate di Padre Pio, Documentary Edition (1995)](https://www.imagomundi.biz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PBSO37-Le_stigmate_di_Padre_Pio_da_Pietrelcina-Gerardo_di_Flumeri.pdf) — Capuchin-edited collection reproducing physician reports and testimony. Relevant clinical records were read; the original handwritten documents were not inspected.
- [Francesco Galofaro — Medicine and Mysticism in Pio’s Stigmata (2020)](https://iris.unito.it/handle/2318/1765139) — University-hosted analysis comparing the medical and cause records. A secondary documentary analysis, rather than another examination of Pio’s wounds.
- [Holy Office Declaration, 31 May 1923](https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-15-1923-ocr.pdf) — Original Acta Apostolicae Sedis declaration at printed page 356. Documents the historical judgment that supernatural character had not been established; it is not a clinical fraud diagnosis.
- [Pietro Gerardo Violi — Consiglia De Martino Clinical Reconstruction](https://www.padrepioesangiovannirotondo.it/piosgr/il-miracolo-della-beatificazione-di-padre-pio/) — Nephrologist and postulation collaborator’s reconstruction of the beatification healing. Read beside the local audit of imaging, timing and cervical-swelling comparators.
- [Pietro Gerardo Violi — Matteo Pio Colella Clinical Reconstruction](https://www.padrepioesangiovannirotondo.it/piosgr/il-miracolo-della-canonizzazione-di-padre-pio/) — Postulation-linked clinical narrative of the canonization healing, with treating-team and illness chronology leads. It is not the complete ICU chart.

## Lourdes

- [Sanctuary of Lourdes — Recognized Healing Cases](https://www.lourdes-france.com/it/guarigioni-miracolose/) — Official case hub used to screen 72 published entries in the November 2026 audit. The brief entries are a discovery index, not the underlying medical dossiers.
- [Jim Garner — Lourdes, CMAJ (1974)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1955914/) — Journal report including Micheli and Traynor and published radiograph reproductions. Garner was a journalist/editor and describes restricted access to original records.
- [Barbara A. Neilan — Vittorio Micheli Clinical Account (2013)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6027009/) — Clinical secondary account derived with permission from Salmon’s medical report. The article contains no actual image plates; use Garner for the recovered radiograph reproductions.
- [Archbishop Malcolm McMahon — John Traynor Declaration (2024)](https://www.lourdes-france.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Statement-on-John-Traynor-Case-FINAL.pdf) — Complete two-page institutional account of the archival review and recognition. For the original 1926 medical minutes, follow the local audit’s source register.

## Comparative Miracles

- [Nairobi 1988 — Contemporary Newspaper Reproduction Index](https://web.stanford.edu/~ichriss/N.htm) — Hub for Kenya Times and Sunday Times reproductions about the robed visitor. Contemporary reporting can be checked separately from later supernatural interpretations.
- [T. Jayaraman — Behind the Milk-drinking Miracle](https://www.imsc.res.in/~jayaram/Articles/milkb.html) — Physicist’s account of investigating the 1995 milk event, including replication and runoff evidence. Compare what was experimentally shown with the original public claims.
- [Rungrawee Chalermsripinyorat — Dhammakaya Study (2002)](https://digital.car.chula.ac.th/manusya/vol5/iss1/2/) — University-hosted study of the movement and controversy. Includes a solar-event witness quotation through The Nation; it is not an independently controlled investigation of the event.
- [Sufi Piercing Case — Original Medical Citation (2001)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11572849/) — PubMed record for the cheek-piercing case. The original full article was not recovered; the local follow-up distinguishes its abstract from a later firsthand chapter and unresolved injury baselines.
- [Raguram et al. — Traditional Community Resources for Mental Health (BMJ, 2002)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1123553/) — Primary observational study of psychiatric outcomes at a Hindu healing temple. Relevant to comparative healing, with a different endpoint from reversal of destructive bodily lesions.

## Biblical Signs and Discernment

- [Douay-Rheims Bible — Public-domain Source Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1582) — Source edition for the local 105-group passage collection. Its register keeps reference, speaker, verse text and category together, including signs and warnings about deception.
- [John 10 — Believe the Works](https://ebible.org/engDRA/JHN10.htm) — Read verses 21, 25, 32 and 37–38 in context: Jesus appeals to his works as testimony. One of the central passages in the biblical preamble.
- [Matthew 24 — Signs and Discernment](https://ebible.org/engDRA/MAT24.htm) — Context for warnings about deceptive signs and claims. Read alongside the passages appealing to miracles rather than treating either group in isolation.

## Cumulative Argument

- [Fairfield and Charman — Explicit Bayesian Analysis for Process Tracing (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2017.14) — Methodological paper on weighing evidence and rival explanations with explicit assumptions. Provides tools for a cumulative argument without supplying miracle-specific probabilities.
- [Xie and Hayes — Sensitivity to Evidential Dependencies (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9285361/) — Experimental work on judgments under evidence dependence. Useful for checking whether multiple reports or methods add independent information.
