## Fátima: a predicted public solar sign

The test is the seven criteria agreed under main-tally entry F-09: a specific sign announced in advance; same-day independent or hostile eyewitness press; distant witnesses; physical effects; a single bounded event seen crowd-wide; no matching cluster of eye injuries; physical corroboration. Fátima scores 6/7 ([report](research/solar-reference-class-20261112/report.txt), [scores](research/solar-reference-class-20261112/scores.csv)).

| # | Candidate | Recorded outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dhammakaya solar event, Thailand | The reporting, low-sun conditions, and fundraising context fell short of the proposed Fátima comparison. Main-tally entry C-01. |
| 2 | Palmar de Troya, Spain, 1968–1970 | Named early witnesses report solar rotation and colours, and the movement became a rival church with its own pope. The miracle announced for 15 May 1970 has no recorded solar fulfilment in independent fieldwork, and the strongest press is a retrospective interview. 0/7 against Fátima’s 6/7. |

Lubbock (1988) and Necedah (1950) were also scored under F-09 (0/7 and ½/7). As unapproved Catholic claims they are not rival religions and are not counted here.

## Lourdes: a directed shrine with a medical record

Daniel’s test: the shrine’s divine figure directed a seer to dig, find or build at a specific place, **and** the site has documented medical before-and-after healings for a century or more, comparable to the Lourdes Medical Bureau (examining cures since 1883). Daniel grants that healings occur in all traditions. First pass, 12 November 2026 ([report](research/lourdes-shrine-comparators-20261112/report.txt)).

| # | Candidate | Recorded outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Tinos, Panagia Evangelistria (Greek Orthodox, 1823) | Meets the founding direction: in visions the Virgin told Pelagia to dig for an icon and build a church. No physician records or case files found. Greek church archives not yet searched. |
| 11 | Our Lady of Kazan (Russian Orthodox, 1579) | Meets the founding direction: the Virgin showed Matrona in dreams where to dig. Traditional healing accounts only; no medical records. |
| 12 | Imam Reza shrine, Mashhad (Shia Islam) | A tomb shrine, not founded on a divine direction. Earlier audits found no case with Lourdes-equivalent clinical records. |

Tinos and Kazan are Orthodox rather than rival religions, so they bear on Rome against Orthodoxy. They match Lourdes on the founding story and fail on documentation. What points Lourdes to Rome specifically is the 25 March 1858 statement “I am the Immaculate Conception”.
