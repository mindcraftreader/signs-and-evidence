**Lourdes begins in 1858 with Bernadette Soubirous, a fourteen-year-old girl from a poor family in southwestern France.** She suffered from asthma and had received little schooling. [Shrine’s biography](https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/bernadette-soubirous/)

On **February 11**, Bernadette was gathering firewood with her sister and a friend near the **grotto of Massabielle**. According to her account, she heard something like a gust of wind and saw a young woman dressed in white, with a blue sash and yellow roses at her feet. Bernadette took out her rosary and prayed. Her companions did not see the woman. She subsequently reported **eighteen appearances**, ending on July 16. [Apparition chronology](https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/apparitions/)

The woman initially withheld her name. Her messages emphasized **prayer, repentance and concern for sinners**. She asked Bernadette to return repeatedly, and later instructed her to tell the priests that people should come in procession and that a chapel should be built there. Bernadette faced questioning from civil authorities and skepticism from the parish priest, **Father Dominique Peyramale**. [Shrine’s account](https://bernadette-usa.lourdes-france.com/ip.php?pg=The+Apparitions)

**The spring enters the story on February 25.** The woman directed Bernadette to drink and wash at a particular spot. Bernadette scraped at the ground and initially found muddy water; the flow subsequently became clearer and more plentiful. Reports of healings followed. The bathing facilities developed afterward—the original account concerns uncovering a spring, rather than finding a ready-made pool. [The spring’s discovery](https://bernadette-usa.lourdes-france.com/ip.php?pg=The+Apparitions)

Then, on **March 25**, Bernadette again asked the woman’s name. She reported receiving the answer:

> **“I am the Immaculate Conception.”**

The shrine’s account says Bernadette repeated the unfamiliar expression while going to tell Peyramale. Its significance was specifically Catholic: **Pope Pius IX had formally defined the Immaculate Conception in 1854**—the doctrine that Mary was preserved from original sin from the first moment of her conception. [Apparition account](https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/apparitions/), [Catholic Catechism §491](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_one/section_two/chapter_two/artcile_3/paragraph_2_conceived_by_the_power_of_the_holy_spirit_and_born_of_the_virgin_mary.html)
