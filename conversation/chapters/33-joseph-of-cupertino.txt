> “Eyewitnesses beyond objection reported the celebrated elevations from the ground and great flights of that servant of God while in ecstasy and rapture.”

He also says the question of Joseph’s virtues was resolved favorably after he left that office. I checked this against the original Latin scan, printed p.257. **He was discussing bodily elevation and actual flights, not merely spiritual uplift.** [Local source: benedict-xiv-book3-1788.pdf]

- **1735:** Clement XII approved Joseph’s heroic virtues.
